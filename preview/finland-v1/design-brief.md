# Finland / Birch & Blue — pre-model visual contract

Primary reference is this written design, not a supplied concept image. This procedurally authored keepsake interprets silver-birch bark and Finnish Lakeland; it is not a traditional Finnish artifact or geographic reconstruction.

## Research
- ThisisFINLAND, Finnish Ministry for Foreign Affairs: https://finland.fi/life-society/iconic-finnish-nature-symbols-stand-out — silver birch is the national tree, white bark and historical bark use for baskets/roofs/shoes. This supports a pale bark-inspired exterior, not a claim of authentic basket construction.
- Arboretum Mustila: https://mustila.fi/en/plants/betulapendula — pale bark darkens and fissures with age; silver birch is culturally familiar in Finland.
- Visit Finland: https://www.visitfinland.com/en/places-to-go/lakeland/ and https://www.visitfinland.com/en/articles/lakeland-lake-hopping/ — lakes, islands and forest form a blue/green labyrinth. The lid uses invented island outlines, not named lake cartography.
Research text/photos are not embedded in textures. All maps are original procedural images.
