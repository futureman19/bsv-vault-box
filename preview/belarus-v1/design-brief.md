# Belarus / Golden Straw — pre-model visual contract

## Research and interpretation
Primary reference is this written brief, not an existing approved illustration.
UNESCO, Straw weaving in Belarus, art, craft and skills (inscribed 2022): https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/straw-weaving-in-belarus-art-craft-and-skills-01889 describes cereal-straw boxes, baskets and decorative works, living transmission and diverse practitioners. The Committee's decision https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/17.COM/7.b.45 confirms the material and craft context. Retrieved by web search for this brief.

This design celebrates golden cereal-straw surfaces with a geometric woven lid panel. It does NOT reproduce a particular artisan's work, ritual pawuk, sacred symbol or historical pattern. The interlace is an original procedural design. Dark timber backing and satin brass are design extrapolations; this is not a functional straw container or claim of Belarusian artisan authorship. No reference photos are embedded.

## Measurable contract, frozen before modeling
- Wide box: 3.72 units across, approximately 2.48 deep, body skins z .10–1.56; lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 centered z 1.828. Retain hollow liner, four feet, connected rear hinges and family brass hasp/medallion/ring.
- Warm honey-gold straw body skins (base map median approximately RGB 185–215 / 140–180 / 65–105), finer alternating over/under strip pattern; dark brown timber lid and backing. Portable generated color/roughness maps no larger than 512 square. Not photographic scans.
- Lid carries a real raised rectangular woven mat: 21 warp ribbons and 13 weft ribbons, thin rectangular cross-sections; .12 unit pitch with alternating over/under relief. Extent approximately ±1.25 X, ±.78 Y. Lowest surface seated at slab top 1.978, highest <2.09. Four dark/gold edge bindings. All mat geometry parents to Lid_Hinge.
- Narrow dark amber seam: height .108, rear narrowed for inherited hinge clearance. Satin warm brass fittings, not straw-colored metal throughout. Four dark timber feet.
- Front is -Y; rear hinge +Y; right +X. Keep exact inherited pivot positions, staged -80° latch release over first quarter, then -105° lid lift. Include every moving mesh in 81-pose collision sampling; only explicit mating bearing contacts excluded.
- Source poster must be rendered from saved authored source. Offline standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including all runtime assets. Native open/close/reset/drag, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB fallback and no-JS poster.
