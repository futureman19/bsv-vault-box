# Honduras — Río Plátano / Rainforest & River

Written before model authoring. Primary reference is this sourced visual contract, not a supplied illustration. Landscape-inspired miniature, not a geographic replica or a representation of every Honduran community.

## Research
UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Río Plátano Biosphere Reserve: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/196/ (consulted for this brief). Its description documents rugged granite mountains, densely forested headwaters, lowland meanders, coastal wetlands and a watershed reaching the Caribbean. The landscape-to-coast sequence informs this design; no source photographs or cultural symbols are reproduced.
UNESCO Honduras-submitted tentative extension description: https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6383/ distinguishes mountainous broadleaf forest from the coastal lagoon mosaic. This supports the contrast of relief and smooth blue-green water, not a claim of accurate cartography.
