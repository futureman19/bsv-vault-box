# Cameroon v1 — Fako / Basalt & Rainforest

Pre-model visual contract. Original landscape-inspired design, not a reconstruction of a supplied marketing illustration. Cameroon has many cultures and landscapes; this miniature selects Mount Cameroon, not a claim to represent them all. No sacred iconography or craft authenticity claim.

## Research and primary concept
- Mount Cameroon National Park: https://mtcameroonnationalpark.org/about-the-park/ describes basaltic volcanism, numerous scoria/cinder cones, and the western slope's rainforest-to-grassland gradient.
- Oregon State University, Volcano World: https://volcano.oregonstate.edu/mount-cameroon describes the elongated basaltic-to-trachybasaltic massif and numerous flank cinder cones.
- Management plan, hosted by World Bank: https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/290571468007177234/pdf/E23260V30REPLA00Box391426B00PUBLIC0.pdf describes lowland/submontane forest, montane grassland and volcanic soils.
Research is factual inspiration only; no source photographs/textures copied. The primary concept is an authored elongated volcanic relief with darker summit and green lower flanks, mounted on a warm charcoal volcanic-stone keepsake box. Not a measured geographic model. No glowing lava, glacier, cedar tree, national flag or copied ritual motifs.
