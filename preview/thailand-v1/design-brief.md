## Research
- Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art, Sawankhalok (Si Satchanalai) kilns: https://publications.asia.si.edu/publications/seaceramics/place/location.php?group=43&key=102 — green/blue-green celadon, incised decoration and later covered boxes.
- Tokyo National Museum, Thai Celadon Ware: https://www.tnm.jp/modules/r_free_page/index.php?id=436&lang=en — clear light blue-green glaze over engraved designs; a fifteenth-century dish with carved waves.
- Smithsonian, Green glaze: https://publications.asia.si.edu/publications/seaceramics/materials/subgroup.php?key=12 — regional iron-bearing glaze and firing context. Celadon is shared across Asia, not exclusive to Thailand.

Research informs materials, not copied pixels or replica claims. The wave composition is invented; no sacred emblem, inscription, artisan attribution or antique authenticity claim.
