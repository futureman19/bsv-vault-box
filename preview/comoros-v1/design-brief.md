# Comoros — Karthala & Perfume Isles

Written before model authoring. A stylized collectible interpretation, not a scale geological reconstruction or a traditional Comorian chest replica.

## Research
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Karthala — Karthala is a shield volcano on Grande Comore; its broad summit caldera informs a low crater relief, not a pointed stratovolcano.
- https://cnn.com/travel/article/comoros-islands — reporting on Karthala and Comoros's ylang-ylang harvesting/distillation informs yellow botanical accents.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comoro_Islands — volcanic island setting informs dark basalt and a restrained turquoise ocean reveal.
Sources consulted as text; no source photographs or textures copied. No sacred motifs, flags, territorial maps or claims of exact botanical reproduction.

## Measurable visual contract
- Retain collection wide keepsake silhouette approximately 3.72 x 2.38; body height approximately 1.9, hollow five-sided interior. Dark charcoal basalt body with small irregular pores, portable procedural color/roughness/normal maps at <=512px.
- Palette: charcoal basalt (#343d3c target), muted green vegetation (#455b35), restrained ocean turquoise (#397f83), warm yellow ylang-inspired petals (#dbc367), satin antique brass. These are design targets rather than colorimetry guarantees under lighting.
- Lid: a low asymmetric shield-volcano relief with a visibly recessed crater, max rise 0.65 above lid. Subordinate clustered green growth and at least three six-petal yellow ylang-inspired blossoms, distinct from inherited cedar or glacier art. All terrain and flowers are lid descendants.
- Narrow turquoise mineral/ocean-inspired seam <=0.10 height; not a claim of water sealing a real box. Brass family latch, rings and physically connected rear hinges. No floating decorative terrain.
- Front = -Y, rear = +Y, right = +X, Blender up = +Z; glTF mapping (X,Z,-Y). Retain base pivots and staged 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening.
- Collision-sample 81 poses across every moving part versus static surfaces and latch versus lid; only explicit mating bearings excluded. Preserve named inheritance and report any other intentional intersections rather than silently exempting them.
- Offline standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, embedded GLB/runtime/maps/actual-source poster; drag, open/close, reset, reduced motion and fallback. Desktop and 390x844 emulated mobile file:// checks, no network requests/errors/overflow. Mobile emulation is not phone hardware proof.
