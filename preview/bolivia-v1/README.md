## Visual verdict and limits
Complete stylized collectible with readable pale body, blue polygon lid, ochre relief and restrained brass. Actual mobile and open-desktop screenshots inspected. The pale coursed body can read as ceramic/planks rather than unmistakable crystalline halite; fine ridge depth and fittings become small on mobile. The regularized honeycomb is an invented salt-flat abstraction, not a measured polygon map. The ochre relief was refined from a simple cone to irregular faceted contours, but remains a stylized mound, not a geographic model of Tunupa. Wet-sky blue and dry raised crust are a deliberate artistic combination. Lid underside hides landscape when opened from the front; rear-open evidence shows its other side. Render retains some sampling noise. No photoreal-equivalence or physical-phone performance claim. Browser ran on desktop RTX 4060; touch is emulation.
