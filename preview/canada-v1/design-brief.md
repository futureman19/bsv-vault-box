# Canada v1 — Maple & Shield

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced written contract; there is no supplied or previously approved Canada illustration.

## Research and interpretation
- Natural Resources Canada, sugar maple fact sheet: https://tidcf.nrcan.gc.ca/en/trees/factsheet/86 — describes light yellowish-brown, hard wood, curly grain, and use in furniture/cabinetwork. This informs the pale honey maple body, not a claim of harvested material.
- Parks Canada, Georgian Bay Islands: https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/georg — describes granite outcrops of the Canadian Shield.
- Parks Canada announcement: https://www.canada.ca/en/parks-canada/news/2016/08/2016-georgian-bay-islands-national-park-announcement-grand-total-investment-1-5-m.html — identifies windswept white pines and granite shores.
These are factual inspirations, not copied imagery. A regional maple-cabinet / Shield-water miniature is not representative of every Canadian landscape or culture. No Indigenous motif, sacred symbol, official flag or national insignia is reproduced.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth about 2.48, exterior height 1.46; lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centered at z=1.828. Hollow inherited five-sided interior.
- Body and lid border: light honey maple, target sRGB map midtones around (193,151,94), small irregular curly grain rather than cedar-red. Alternating real finger-jointed corner courses. 512px color/roughness and 256px endgrain maps, independently generated.
- Dark green-gray gasket, maximum .108 high; restrained satin brass corner caps, solid medallion, side ring and inherited functional rear hinge mounts. No flag painted on a generic box.
- Lid top: shallow blue lake inlay, footprint 2.86 x 1.62, top z=2.014; three asymmetric rounded pink-gray granite outcrops, tallest less than .40 above lid. Outcrops are abstract Shield shores, not an accurate named island survey.
- One windswept pine on the rear-right outcrop, root near (0.66,0.42,2.17), crown swept toward +X and maximum z under 3.05. Actual bent trunk and branching plus compact fine needle fans; no opaque cone. Every rock, water and tree component parented to Lid_Hinge. Tree roots intersect the granite surface.
- Pink-gray granite feet, warm light wood, deep lake-blue accent and subdued evergreen crown. Grain remains visible but subordinate at normal mobile size.
- Front is -Y, hinge rear +Y, right +X. glTF maps (X,Z,-Y). Retain exactly the mechanism pivots: staged 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening, 81 sampled poses of all moving/static meshes and latch-versus-lid. Only inherited named mating bearing contacts exempt.
- Actual Blender-rendered poster. Fully embedded offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, no remote runtime assets; rotation, touch, open/close, reset, reduced motion, real invalid-GLB and no-JS poster fallback.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source reopening, bidirectional vertex parity <1e-5, finite and nondegenerate actual GLTFLoader geometry, ancestry and dimensions, maple/stone texture variation and roughness green-channel variation, sampled motion clearance, floor ray and hinge attachment. Desktop + 390x844 touch-emulated mobile over offline file://; zero external HTTP requests/errors/overflow. Exact byte/SHA-256 delivery inventory and ZIP readback; additive-only Git scope.
Negative control: detach a tree component and require ancestry test rejection. Preserve any real failed execution and fix it, not the criteria. Visual inspection of real renders/screenshots is separate from technical PASS. No claim of photorealism, botanical reconstruction, continuous collision proof, physical-phone performance, minting, hosting or chain validation.
