# North Macedonia — Ohrid / Limestone & Lake

Written before model authoring. Primary reference is this sourced written contract, not supplied concept art.

UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/99 describes Lake Ohrid as an ancient tectonic lake with endemic freshwater species and cultural shore settlements; the property is shared by North Macedonia and Albania. The lake is not exclusively North Macedonian. Pale limestone-like stone is an artistic regional landscape interpretation, not a claim of quarry provenance. No copied photographs, sacred symbols, traditional pattern claims or geographic accuracy.
