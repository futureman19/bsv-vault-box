# Sao Tome and Principe / Phonolite & Rainforest

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced contract; no supplied marketing image exists for this pilot.

## Research and interpretation
- UNESCO MAB, Island of Principe: https://www.unesco.org/en/mab/island-principe — volcanic island; southern phonolitic peaks 500–948 m and primary rainforest. Search-retrieved source excerpt informs the material/forest association, not exact geography.
- Pico Cão Grande: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pico_C%C3%A3o_Grande — needle-shaped volcanic plug on Sao Tome, principally phonolite, rising roughly 370 m above surrounding terrain. This distinct tall, rounded needle is the lid silhouette reference.
- Geoheritage of the Príncipe UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s12371-023-00887-w.pdf — rainforest on basaltic slopes, phonolitic southern peaks; search-retrieved abstract supports the broader landscape vocabulary.
No research photographs or text are embedded in textures. The island-shaped forest bed and needle are an invented composition, not a cartographic model, species inventory or replica of craft. Pico Cão Grande is on Sao Tome, not Principe. The design does not represent every culture or landscape of the country.

No horizontal cedar grain or timber finger joints. Narrow muted green reveal z 1.564–1.672, rear narrowed for hinge clearance.
- Satin dark stone lid width 3.72, depth 2.38, thickness .30 centered z 1.828; small brass corner fittings, brass front hasp/medallion and left ring. Hardware is collection language, not a claim of traditional local metalwork.
- Central irregular forest landform, approximately 2.7 by 1.5, rooted at lid top 1.978, highest low terrain <2.30. Canopy clusters in coherent muted greens, never alternating per-triangle checkerboard.

It must read as a tall narrow rock, not a generic conical volcano. No crater or snow cap. Low surrounding wooded hills are subordinate.
