# Malaysia v1 — Songket / Crimson & Gold

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research
- UNESCO, Songket, https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/songket-01505 (retrieved 2026-11-04): Malaysian handwoven fabric, supplementary gold/silver threads appearing to float above a colored ground; geometric and organic patterns; Malay Peninsula and Sarawak. Inscribed 2021.
- UNESCO decision https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/16.com/8.b.22 identifies bearers particularly in Terengganu and Kelantan and intergenerational transmission.

Interpretation: a crimson textile-clad keepsake with gold diamond lattice and raised geometric thread ornament. Original abstract motifs, not reproduction of a named traditional pattern, artisan authorship, or representation of all Malaysian cultures. No source photography embedded.
