# Senegal — Saloum / Mangrove & Shell

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced design contract, not supplied concept artwork.

UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1359/ describes brackish channels, over 200 islands/islets, mangrove and dry forest, and a landscape shaped by fishing and shellfish gathering. The interpretation centre https://www.audeltadusaloum.com/le-delta-du-saloum?lang=en identifies the Saloum, Bandiala and Diombos branches and mangrove habitat. These inform an invented landscape miniature, not a geographic map. No burial mound, sacred motif, harvested shell, photographic texture or claim of traditional artisan authorship is used.

- Wide 3.72 x 2.38 family lid, body skins to z1.56, lid top z1.978; continuous hollow liner. Shell-sand body, generated fine mineral flecks, warm ivory/sand sRGB approximately #c5b492 with modest variation; not timber grain. Four sand-colored feet.
- Muted dark green seam thickness .108, rear narrower to clear hinges. Warm satin brass family latch, corner caps, ring and medallion; unseen mechanics are inherited extrapolations.
- Teal water inset approximately 3.1 x1.75 on lid. Three irregular low banks separate visibly connected branching channels. Banks rise .04–.12 above water, with closed sides; six small mangrove miniatures across banks, each with four exposed splayed roots, branching trunks and clustered muted green canopies. Roots meet banks; every ornament belongs to lid. Miniature height stays below z2.65.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Same inherited Lid_Hinge/Latch_Pivot transforms; staged 80-degree latch then 105-degree lid. 81 poses, all moving versus static and latch versus lid, explicit mating bearings only excluded.

- Acceptance: independently reopened source, hollow ray, hinge attachment, full motion sampling, source/GLB geometry parity <1e-5, actual GLTFLoader zero nonfinite/degenerate triangles; theme counts/parentage/bounds/root seating/channel sample rays and a deliberate detached-tree negative control. Visual intent is a stylized Saloum-inspired mangrove miniature, not photorealism, ecological simulation or faithful cartography. Mobile emulation is not physical-phone performance. Cosmetic opening is not blockchain activity. No minting, custody, deployment or storage availability is established.
