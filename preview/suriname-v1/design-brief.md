# Suriname — Granite & Rainforest

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this researched visual contract, not an existing illustration. A stylized landscape miniature, not a geographic replica or representation of every Surinamese community.

## Research
- UNESCO/IUCN evaluation https://whc.unesco.org/document/151540 describes the Central Suriname Nature Reserve's upper Coppename watershed, lowland/montane tropical forests and distinctive granite inselbergs rising above forest.
- UNEP-WCMC https://world-heritage-datasheets.unep-wcmc.org/datasheet/output/site/central-suriname-nature-reserve describes bare rounded granite domes, including Voltzberg, and the ancient Guyana Shield basement. Textual inspiration only; no third-party photos/textures embedded.
