# Madagascar — Tsingy / Limestone Forest

Brief frozen before modeling. One stylized landscape-built collectible, not a surveyed site or a traditional Malagasy craft replica. No supplied primary marketing illustration exists for this country; the measurable design below is the primary visual contract. No sacred motifs or unsupported inscriptions.

## Research and provenance
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Andrefana Dry Forests: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/494/ describes western Madagascar's karstic limestone uplands, sharp tsingy needles and the contrast of grey karst with forest green. The property includes Tsingy de Bemaraha and was expanded in 2023. Retrieved by web search for this brief.
- UNESCO/NHK description: https://whc.unesco.org/en/documents/104118/ describes the limestone needle forest and Manambolo canyon. These are factual landscape references, not licensed texture inputs.
All geometry/maps will be independently procedural. No UNESCO photographs, text or logos embedded in the asset. This miniature does not imply limestone extraction from a protected site.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: main exterior approximately 3.72 X × 2.38 Y, body height approximately 1.9, inherited hollow liner/floor. Not a cube.
- Body: grey-warm limestone with broken vertical fluting, dark crevice cuts and subordinate chalk grain. Generated portable <=512px color/roughness maps. Palette target chalk #aaa796, weathered grey #777c73, crevice #343d36; avoid Iceland-black basalt or Moroccan red strata.
- Lid: satin limestone slab with a miniature asymmetric forest of at least 18 tapered, irregular, faceted tsingy pinnacles, heights 0.18–0.68 above lid. Cluster biased to rear/right, lower sparse foreground, visible gaps/gullies rather than one mountain cone. All pinnacles rooted in lid terrain and parented to Lid_Hinge. No glacier, cedar tree or copied Japan wood.
- Muted forest-green patches (#44543a family) in sheltered lid gullies, not neon grass. At least 6 distinct patches. Narrow dark green/grey seam under 0.12 high, clear of hinge motion.
- Inherited restrained satin brass hardware, front hasp/medallion, rear mechanically seated hinges, corner fittings and feet. Local front=-Y, rear=+Y, head-on right=+X, up=+Z; glTF=(X,Z,-Y).
- Exact inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot locations and hierarchy. Latch releases 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees. Reverse closes lid then latch. Every ornament travels with lid; sample 81 poses including all moving geometry, against static and latch-vs-lid. Only narrowly named intentional bearing contacts exempt.
- Actual-source render poster, never generated promotional art. Standalone HTML embeds runtime/model/maps/poster and is strictly <5,000,000 bytes.

## Acceptance gates written before authoring
1. Independent saved-source reopening: hollow floor, correct pivot hierarchy/location, hinge attachment, expected limestone/pinnacle/green meshes; geometry and source/export parity within 1e-5.
2. Real GLTFLoader: finite geometry, zero degenerate triangles, decoded map variation, portable materials and all landmark ancestry.
3. 81 sampled motion poses: zero unintended moving/static or latch/lid surface intersections; sampled evidence, not continuous mechanical certification.
4. Offline file:// Edge desktop and emulated mobile: native open/close, drag/touch, interrupted reset, reduced motion, no external HTTP requests/errors/overflow, framed open silhouette, real corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster fallback.
5. Exact artifact bytes and SHA-256 inventory, explicit required deliverable set, HTML cap fails closed. Acceptance tests include negative controls or preserved actual failures, never unconditional PASS fields.
6. Inspect actual desktop/mobile closed/open renders for silhouette, grey limestone, pinnacles and green gullies. Technical pass does not establish photorealism, cultural completeness or physical-phone performance.

One bounded initial build plus up to two correction passes. Human per-asset review waived by task; complete independently when gates pass. No blockchain, production, hosting, shared-base changes or merging.
