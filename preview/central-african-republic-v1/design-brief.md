# Central African Republic — Dzanga Forest Clearing

## Research and interpretation (before modeling)
UNESCO's Sangha Trinational entry https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1380 identifies humid tropical forest, wetlands and ecologically important forest clearings. Its component inventory https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1380/maps identifies Dzanga-Ndoki National Park specifically in the Central African Republic. UNESCO's Dzanga clearing photograph record https://whc.unesco.org/en/documents/115221 corroborates this landscape; the photograph is All Rights Reserved and is NOT copied, embedded or used as texture. Sources retrieved via web search for this brief.

Concept: a dark warm timber keepsake chest carrying a sculptural miniature forest clearing: low irregular green canopy clusters surrounding a warm sandy oval, with a shallow dark water inset. This is an independently authored landscape abstraction, not a survey, a particular wood-species claim, a traditional craft reproduction, or a representation of all the country's peoples. No sacred symbols, flags or invented inscriptions. No external image reference is the art target: the measurable contract below is the original design.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide family chest, nominal body width 3.72, depth 2.38, height about 1.9; retain inherited hollow liner and articulation anchor dimensions exactly.
- Dark warm brown timber body: target base atlas median RGB near (85,48,28), varied fine grain, not red cedar. Procedurally generated portable color/roughness/normal maps, at most 512x512 each.
- Satin dark timber lid, thin muted forest-green seam below 0.12 height; seam must not obstruct hinges. Restrained aged brass hardware and feet. Preserve existing physically attached hinges/latch rather than redesigning mechanism.
- Lid landscape: sandy irregular clearing centered around (0,-0.05), nominal extent 1.35x0.8; subdued ochre target RGB (155,119,65). At least five separated broadleaf canopy clusters along rear and sides, varying height 0.25–0.65 above lid; dark green palette (35,65,35) to (75,100,44). Do not reuse a cedar/pine silhouette. Small shallow dark water inset inside clearing; no glacier or mountain.
- Every landscape component is a descendant of Lid_Hinge. Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z; glTF is (X,Z,-Y). Keep geometry within lid footprint and under 0.85 units above lid.
- Preserve original pivots and staged 80-degree latch release, then 105-degree lid opening. Independently reopen saved source and collision-sample 81 poses for all moving components vs static parts, plus latch vs lid; only explicitly named mating bearings exempt.
- Offline embedded runtime/GLB/source-render poster; standalone HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes. Desktop and 390x844 touch-emulated mobile file:// interaction, reduced motion, reset, fallback, no requests/errors/overflow.
- Acceptance: source, source/export parity, actual-loader geometry, theme dimensions/materials/parentage, motion, browser and delivery exact-byte SHA-256 checks. Checks must fail on invalid data. Visual review of actual source/browser captures is separate from technical gates; no photorealism claim.
