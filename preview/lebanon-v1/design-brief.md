# Lebanon v1 — Cedar & Limestone

Written before modeling. Original written concept; no supplied primary illustration. This is a landscape-inspired collectible, not a replica, sacred-site model or botanical survey.

## Research
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/850/ : Qadisha's rugged cliffs and nearby remnant cedar forest in northern Lebanon; identifies Cedrus libani. This informs the contrasting pale rocky plinth and broad cedar crown.
- ICOMOS/IUCN evaluation, https://whc.unesco.org/document/154441 : describes Jurassic-origin valley and limestone caves. This informs limestone material, not surveyed terrain.
No source photographs or textures will be copied. All maps and geometry are procedural. No assertion of harvested Lebanese cedar or traditional artisan authorship.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Wide family box approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height approximately 1.9; hollow liner. Front = -Y, rear = +Y, up = Z.
- Body pale warm limestone: cream/beige portable generated color map, restrained irregular strata/pores and roughness variation, not wood grain. Warm cedar-brown inset lid; muted dark-green seam <=0.12 high.
- Gold/brass family corner fittings, latch, ring and connected rear hinges; inherited exact Lid_Hinge / Latch_Pivot transforms. Latch releases 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees.
- Main lid motif: one clearly branched cedar with broad flattened irregular dark-green canopy tiers, not a conical pine; root centered near (+0.55,+0.35), within 0.25 units. Cedar total height above lid 0.65–1.15; crown width 0.8–1.65. Low irregular pale rocky root bed fully seated on lid, max 0.22 high. All ornaments parented under lid.
- Editable named Blender source, GLB with embedded maps; no copied Japan tree art. Reuse mechanics only. Source-rendered poster.
- Standalone HTML <5,000,000 bytes, embedded runtime/model/poster, no external runtime requests. Drag, Open/Close, Reset and reduced-motion handling retained.
