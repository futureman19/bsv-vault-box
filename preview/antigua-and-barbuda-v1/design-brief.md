# Antigua and Barbuda / Limestone & Pink Shore

Frozen before modeling. Primary reference is this written contract, not an existing illustration. Landscape-inspired original miniature, not a geographic replica or claim to represent all island culture.

## Research
Antigua and Barbuda Tourism Authority: https://www.visitantiguabarbuda.com/beaches/ describes Antigua's Devil's Bridge as an Atlantic-carved natural limestone arch, and Barbuda's pink beaches as accumulations of tiny pink shells. https://www.visitantiguabarbuda.com/barbuda/ identifies pink shores, caves and mangroves. https://www.visitantiguabarbuda.com/things-to-do/barbuda-must-see-attractions/ describes the narrow barrier shore alongside Codrington Lagoon. These factual associations inform authored shapes; no website pixels or textures are copied.

## Visual contract
- Wide pale porous limestone body: 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, walls z .10–1.56, cream/shell palette with fine irregular pores rather than wood grain. Continuous hollow liner, four limestone feet.
- Lid slab center z 1.828, thickness .30. Narrow dark-teal mineral seam z 1.618, <=.108 height, rear narrowed for mechanism clearance. Gold/brass family hardware and small corner guards.
- Lid terrain is an invented paired coastal vignette: turquoise water inset (about 2.9 x 1.65), pale-pink crescent barrier shore at left/front and limestone natural-bridge miniature at rear/right. True open arch aperture >=.20 high, visible empty space under crown. Not a literal scale model of Devil's Bridge or Codrington Lagoon. No flags, sacred motifs or inscriptions.
- Limestone base-color target roughly RGB 190–225/177–212/144–185; pink shell sand roughly 218/153/149; lagoon teal roughly 20/125/132. Procedural compact <=512px color/roughness maps, no photographic claims.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X. All new lid terrain parented to Lid_Hinge. Original pivots; latch release 80 degrees then lid 105 degrees. All moving parts in 81-pose sampled surface collision check with only inherited named bearing exemptions.
- Real source-render poster and editable packed Blender source. Standalone file:// HTML <5,000,000 bytes, fully embedded runtime, reduced motion, native desktop/mobile-emulated controls, no external requests, corrupt-input and no-JS poster fallbacks.

## Acceptance
Independent saved-source reopening and bidirectional export vertex parity <1e-5; actual GLTFLoader finite geometry/no degenerates; measured arch aperture and terrain ownership; decoded map variation; source-render nonblank coverage; desktop/mobile closed/open captures and inspection; precise SHA-256 inventory and byte-preserving Git checks. Tests are assertions, not unconditional PASS labels. Sampled collision is not continuous engineering proof; browser emulation is not physical-phone performance. Technical acceptance does not claim photorealism or cultural/geographic completeness.
