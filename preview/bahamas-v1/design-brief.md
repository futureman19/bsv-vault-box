# Bahamas / Pink Sands & Blue Hole

Frozen before modeling. Primary design reference is this written contract, not a supplied illustration. A landscape-inspired collectible, not an accurate map or traditional craft replica.

## Research
- Bahamas Ministry of Tourism, Pink Sand Beach: https://www.bahamas.com/natural-wonders/harbour-island-pink-sand-beach — Harbour Island's eastern shoreline has pale pink sand incorporating red/pink foraminiferal shell fragments. The page loosely calls these organisms insects; this design does not adopt that biological error.
- Bahamas Ministry of Tourism, Dean's Blue Hole: https://www.bahamas.com/natural-wonders/deans-blue-hole — Long Island's blue hole and surrounding turquoise lagoon/white beaches inspire the dark circular water inset.
- https://travel.usnews.com/Bahamas/Things_To_Do/Deans_Blue_Hole_66386/ describes a water-filled limestone cavern.
These are two distinct places combined in an invented miniature. No geographical fidelity, depth scale, harvested coral, scans, copied photography, or cultural-pattern authenticity is claimed. Research informs original procedural geometry/maps only.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth about 2.48, exterior z .10–1.56; lid 3.72 × 2.38 × .30, center z 1.828. Hollow inherited liner and mechanical bearing positions unchanged.
- Warm pale limestone body: creamy granular mineral map, target sRGB approximately #d9c8a7; subordinate pale coral flecks, not cedar grain. Dominant-face UV mapping, three portable 256px maps (color, roughness, sand). Four low limestone feet.
- Satin golden/brass fittings, small corner caps, front medallion and left ring. Narrow dark teal seam height .108; retain the proven rear hinge clearance.
- Lid: aquamarine inset, approximately 3.22 × 1.88, on the stone slab. Two irregular raised pink-sand banks, a long curved beach crescent and a smaller cay, occupy different parts of the water. Sand target #e6b7ad; water target #238f9a, dark blue hole #073b60. Colors are artistic targets, not calibrated geological measurement.
- Blue hole is a circular dark inset within the turquoise lagoon, center around (.55,.12), radius .34; concentric bathymetric color rings, not a drilled opening into the box. Beaches stay within lid bounds and above the water. Relief under .30 above lid, with no inherited trees/glaciers.
- All water, banks and rings are direct children of Lid_Hinge. Front = -Y, back hinge = +Y, right = +X. Same pivots as included mechanism; latch releases 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees. 81 sampled poses cover all moving mesh surfaces against static surfaces and latch against lid; only named mating bearings excluded.
- Offline embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes. Actual Blender source poster, no generated promotional substitution. Desktop and 390×844 touch-emulated file:// checks: rotate, open/close, reset, reduced motion, no external requests, genuine corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster fallback.
- Independent saved-source reopen, source/GLB vertex parity <1e-5, actual-loader finite and nondegenerate geometry, decoded map variation, lid ancestry/landmark counts, exact SHA-256 inventory and archive readback.

## Visual acceptance and limits
Inspect actual closed/open desktop/mobile views for pink-sand/aqua contrast, visible blue disk, connected brass mechanism and readable wide silhouette. This is stylized enamel-like water and compact procedural mineral relief, not photoreal water, coastal simulation, or a literal souvenir made of sand. Passing technical checks does not prove photoreal equivalence. Per-asset human review waived for this collection batch.
