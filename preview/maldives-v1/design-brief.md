# Maldives — Faro / Lagoon & Sand

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced visual contract, not a supplied concept image.

## Research
- Maldives Permanent Mission, Geography: https://un.mdvmission.gov.mv/index.php/en/maldives/geography describes coral reefs enclosing lagoons and cut by natural channels.
- FAO, Abdulla Naseer, Profile and Status of Coral Reefs in Maldives: https://fao.org/3/X5627E/x5627e0a.htm describes faros (ring-shaped reefs with sandy lagoons), coralline sand islands and low elevation.
- Government atoll overview: http://atollsofmaldives.gov.mv describes variation between open reef structures and nearly closed lagoons.
These support a low annular reef miniature, not a mountain, named mapped atoll, cultural pattern or harvested-coral product. No reference photographs/textures copied.
