# Sudan v1 — Meroë / Desert Gold

Written before modeling. A contemporary collectible interpretation of Sudan's Kushite architectural landscape, not a replica, archaeological reconstruction, or representation of all Sudanese cultures.

## Research
UNESCO, Archaeological Sites of the Island of Meroe: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1336/ describes the semi-desert between Nile and Atbara, Kushite pyramids and reddish-brown hills. UNESCO inscription decision https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/4293/ independently describes the distinctive funerary pyramids, riverine/semi-desert contrast and iron-working evidence. Retrieved via web search for this brief. These factual inspirations inform shapes and palette; no source photographs/textures are copied. The sandstone-like surface and brass fittings are artistic choices, not claims about original monument materials or ancient chest hardware. No funerary inscriptions or sacred symbols.

## Visual contract
- Wide keepsake body approximately 3.72 × 2.38 units; body height approximately 1.9; retain existing hollow liner and proven mechanical pivots. Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Primary surface: warm ochre/sandy gold with reddish-brown irregular sediment flecks, a fine porous roughness/normal finish and restrained horizontal masonry joints. No cedar grain, forest/moss, Iceland glacier, or Moroccan ceramic decoration.
- Lid: low asymmetric desert dune field carrying three distinct steep-sided square pyramids, different heights approximately 0.55, 0.8 and 1.05 units above their seated bases. Shallow stepped courses or incised masonry should convey crafted architecture, not a generic smooth mountain. All three have real mesh volume and lid ancestry. Monument placement must avoid hinges and latch sweep. No literal miniature burial contents.
- Brass corner fittings and front clasp maintain family identity; narrow dark brown seam under 0.12 unit high. Hollow dark warm interior. Four stone feet. Extrapolated rear/interior details are contemporary design.
- Palette targets: ochre body dominates, cream sand highlights, russet dune shadows, muted brass hardware. Generated compact maps at 512 pixels or below unless an explicit measured budget requires otherwise. Procedural, not scanned or hand-painted claims.
- Same staged 80° latch release, then 105° lid opening; reset and reverse close. Sample all moving meshes versus static meshes and latch versus lid at 81 poses, excluding only explicit inherited mating bearings. Ornaments travel with lid.
- Standalone offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes, embedded GLB/runtime/poster, no external requests. Poster is actual saved-source render. Reduced motion, rotation, toggle, interrupted reset, corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback.

## Acceptance established before implementation
Saved-source reopening checks dimensions, hollow floor, pivot hierarchy, three seated pyramids, terrain attachment and 81 collision poses. Independent GLB/source parity tolerance 1e-5 units. Actual GLTFLoader checks finite/nondegenerate geometry and texture decoding. Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile checks controls, framing, errors/network and overflow; emulation is not physical phone testing. Theme tests must reject missing pyramids and wrong palette/ancestry; preserve any real failure and fix. Exact bytes/SHA-256 inventory and delivery readback. Inspect actual render/browser screenshots separately from technical tests. No reference-equivalence or photorealism claim. No per-asset human review required by this card.
