# DR Congo v1 — Raffia & River

Written before model authoring. Original procedural design, not a replica of a supplied concept or ethnographic object.

## Research basis
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Kuba artist, Double Prestige Panel, 1999.522.15: https://metmuseum.org/en/art/collection/search/318398 identifies raffia palm fiber/vegetable dye and the Sankuru River region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo; describes tan/black geometric embroidery with asymmetry and irregularity.
- The Met, Bushong-Kuba artists, Prestige panel, 2023.606.2: https://metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/897905 identifies raffia fiber, Kasai province, and interlacing diamond forms with variation. These are particular cultural traditions, not a claim that one motif represents every Congolese community.
- UNESCO, Salonga National Park: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/280/ describes tropical rainforest in the central Congo river basin, accessible by water. This informs the low forest/river relief, not a geographic scale map or botanical reconstruction.
Research text/images are not copied into maps. No named royal/sacred motif, historic textile facsimile, harvested material or conservation endorsement is claimed.

## Measurable visual contract
- Theme: Raffia & River. Wide keepsake silhouette approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y; body/lid height approximately 1.9 units, excluding low lid relief. Continuous hollow five-sided inner shell.
- Body: warm ochre/tan woven-raffia-inspired panels framed by dark brown edges. Original interrupted diagonal/diamond geometry in a portable generated color map, subtle weave roughness/normal texture. Targets tan #a8763e, pale fiber #cba76b, espresso #32271d; material/light response may differ. No photographic textile copied.
- Dark umber satin lid with inset rainforest-green terrain (#264c38 to #587045), irregular low overlapping canopy forms rather than one cedar or pine. Relief no more than 0.65 above lid; at least 12 canopy forms grouped on both banks.
- One continuous sinuous blue-green river inlay across lid, width approximately 0.15–0.26, with real geometric banks/relief. Not the precise Congo course; no waterfall or glacier. River and all canopy/terrain meshes belong to Lid_Hinge and travel rigidly with it.
- Refined warm brass collection hardware, front hasp/medallion, corner fittings and connected rear hinges. Narrow dark seam less than 0.12 units. Retain known mechanism pivots and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid rotation, reverse order on closing. Front Blender -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Reuse mechanism only, not Japan cedar/tree/granite identity. Hidden interior/back are design extrapolations. Lid flora is stylized miniature relief, not species identification.
