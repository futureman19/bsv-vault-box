# Cote d'Ivoire — Indigo Stripweave

Written before modeling. A wide collectible chest inspired by Ivorian narrow-strip cotton weaving, not a reproduction of a named community's ceremonial garment.

## Research
UNESCO, Traditional skills of loincloth weaving in Côte d’Ivoire, https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-skills-of-loincloth-weaving-in-cote-d-ivoire-01949 (consulted for this design): dyed cotton threads are woven in narrow strips then joined; Gouro, Baoule, Malinke, Senufo, Koulango, Nafana and Abron communities have distinct traditions. UNESCO decision https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/18.COM/8.B.27 notes some garments are reserved for specific classes. Therefore use invented non-symbolic strip rhythms, not copied sacred/status motifs. No source photographs or textile scans are embedded. Indigo/ochre/cream are artistic palette choices, not claims about all Ivorian weaving.

## Visual contract
- Wide body approximately 3.72 x 2.38 units, body height approximately 1.9; five-sided hollow interior. Dark walnut-colored structural wood, with predominantly indigo woven panels, cream narrow stripes and restrained ochre accent stripes.
- At least seven visibly separated parallel textile strips on the lid, with raised longitudinal joining seams and fine procedural warp/weft surface variation. Body uses the same invented textile rhythm. No cedar tree, glacier or copied country landmark.
- Lid ornament: a low raised woven inset with geometric strip joins; every ornament and seam is a child of Lid_Hinge. Keep ornament below 0.20 units over lid to retain the low keepsake silhouette.
- Retain refined warm brass family hardware, front latch at -Y, rear hinges at +Y, head-on right +X. Narrow dark seam under 0.12 units. No ivory material or elephant-tusk imagery.
- Preserve exact base pivots and staged 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid rotation. Sample 81 poses, all moving versus static surfaces plus latch versus lid. Exempt only explicit intentional mating bearings; textile joins may seat into the lid but must not be blanket-exempt from static collision.
- Palette targets (sRGB artistic reference): indigo #243b62, cream #ded3ae, ochre #ba873a, walnut #39271d. Readability at desktop and mobile, physically raised joins rather than only paint.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved .blend reopening; actual GLTFLoader geometry/material checks; source/export nearest-vertex parity <= 1e-5; hollow interior ray; complete sampled articulation collisions; lid-parentage/strip-count/palette/map-variation tests capable of failing. Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile: open/close, drag, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-model and no-JS poster fallback, zero external requests or unexpected JS errors, no overflow. Actual source-render poster only. Entire HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, all assets embedded. Exact bytes/SHA-256 inventory and delivery readback. Visual assessment separate from technical success: stylized procedural textile, not handwoven fabric, photorealism or cultural-authenticity certification. No minting, chain operations, publishing or production modifications.
