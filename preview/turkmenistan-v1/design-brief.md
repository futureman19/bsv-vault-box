# Turkmenistan / Crimson Weave — pre-model visual contract

## Research and interpretation
- UNESCO, Traditional Turkmen carpet making art in Turkmenistan, inscribed 2019: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-in-turkmenistan-01486 — hand-woven wool textiles, dense texture and clear linear geometric arrangements; designs relate to five main Turkmen tribes and the makers' environments.
- United Nations Turkmenistan, 17 December 2019: https://turkmenistan.un.org/en/28686-traditional-turkmen-carpet-making-art-turkmenistan-inscribed-list-intangible-cultural — confirms the inscription and cultural identity/community context.
Source descriptions retrieved via web search. Direct extraction backend unavailable. No source images or textile patterns are copied. This written contract is the primary reference: no supplied approved concept exists.

The miniature translates woven textile craft into a wide crimson textile-clad keepsake box with a raised miniature carpet on its lid. The octagons, nested diamonds and borders are independently invented geometric ornaments, NOT authentic tribal gul reproductions, sacred imagery, museum replicas, artisan work or comprehensive representations of Turkmen culture.
