# Mozambique v1 — Bazaruto / Tidal Dunes

Written before modeling. A landscape-inspired collectible, not a geographic scale model or representation of all Mozambican cultures.

## Research
- Mozambique conservation authority ANAC: https://www.anac.gov.mz/en/parques/bazaruto/ describes the five-island park, turquoise natural pools and giant white parabolic sand dunes.
- NASA Earth Observatory: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/bazaruto-archipelago-national-park-154448 describes current-carved channels, tan sand flats, turquoise shallows, darker deep water and active eastern-shore dunes.
These factual descriptions inform independently generated geometry/maps. No NASA or third-party imagery is copied, embedded or licensed for NFT use. No sacred/craft symbols or wildlife claims are invented.
