# Sri Lanka / Dumbara Weave — v1

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced written visual contract, not an existing approved image. A contemporary craft-inspired keepsake box, not an authentic artisan-made mat or claim to reproduce symbolic motifs.

## Research
- UNESCO, Traditional Craftsmanship of Making Dumbara Ratã Kalãla: https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-5638 — identifies hana-fibre mats, the kinnara community, and intergenerational weaving, inscribed in 2021.
- Sri Lanka National Inventory, nomination document: https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/47638-EN.pdf — ivory-white washed fibre; red, yellow and black dyes; warp and weft construction.
- Time Out, Arts and crafts in Sri Lanka: https://www.timeout.com/sri-lanka/art/arts-and-crafts-in-sri-lanka — confirms Dumbara weaving as a living craft distinct from lacquer work.
Research retrieved through web search; direct web extraction backend was unavailable. No source photography, video or motif drawings are copied. Geometric diamonds below are invented, not attributed traditional symbols.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve wide family body approx 3.72 x 2.38, body height approx 1.9; hollow five-sided liner and gold/brass hardware. Front -Y, rear +Y; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Warm dark timber structural box with inset woven ivory panels across front/sides and a broad lid panel. Procedural portable color/roughness maps at <=512 pixels per dimension; visible fine crossed fibre grain, not photographic texture.
- Palette: ivory #c8b68b, near-black #25251f, muted brick red #893b2d and ochre #b69236. Woven panels occupy the majority of the front's unobscured surface. Continuous narrow dark seam <=0.12 high.
- Lid carries three raised interlaced diamond ornaments (invented geometric motifs), red/black/ochre contrasting threads on ivory. Overall ornament footprint within x +/-1.45 and y +/-0.8; height above lid <=0.20. At least 12 physically raised thread segments, genuine gaps, no painting-only substitution. All ornament meshes parented beneath Lid_Hinge. Fine map weave supplements, not replaces, raised geometry.
- Brass family clasp and connected rear hinge hardware retained; no flags, sacred imagery, inscriptions or claims of community endorsement.
- 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening using inherited pivots. Independently reopen source, hollow-interior ray and all-part 81-pose collision sampling, narrowly named bearing exemptions only.
- Standalone embedded runtime/GLB/source-render poster HTML <5,000,000 bytes. Real file:// desktop and emulated-mobile controls, rotation, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback, no network/errors/overflow.
- Independent source/export parity, finite/nondegenerate runtime geometry, material and raised diamond checks. Save exact byte/SHA-256 delivery inventory. Any real failures retained and explained; passing technical checks do not prove photorealism, authentic craft manufacture or phone performance.
