# Germany / Erzgebirge Woodcraft — v1 visual contract

Written before model authoring. Regional craft inspiration, not a claim to represent every German culture or a replica of an artisan's work.

## Sources and interpretation
- Smithsonian Center for Folklife, Charleen Smith-Riedel, “The German Toymakers of the Erzgebirge” (2021): https://folklife.si.edu/magazine/german-toymakers-erzgebirge — describes miners turning to woodcarving, village workshops, wooden figures, and natural blonde-wood miniatures. Retrieved search text informs this design; no photographs are used as textures.
- State of Saxony, “The Ore Mountains”: https://www.regionen.sachsen.de/en/the-ore-mountains-3974.html — describes regional woodworking techniques including Reifendrehen and Spanbaumstechen. Its World Heritage wording is historical; no present listing claim relies on that page.

The authored design is a pale honey-wood keepsake with a low wooden village vignette: three turned, tiered trees and two gabled houses. It is a digital craft-inspired composition, not authentic regional manufacture, a protected maker mark, a scale town, or a demonstration of the named traditional techniques. No religious or national symbols.

## Measurable contract
- Retain wide body 3.72 × approximately 2.48 units, body skins z=0.10–1.56; lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × 0.30 centred at z=1.828. Hollow five-sided liner retained.
- Pale honey body grain: generated 512px base-color target approximately RGB (190,145,88), fine longitudinal dark grain; dark endgrain finger joints. Real alternating eight-course wood joinery, not painted joints. Compact roughness map, no fetched imagery.
- Satin gold/brass family hardware, four small lid corner plates, retained connected rear hinges, front hasp, side ring. Muted dark-brown seam height 0.108; rear strip narrowed for hinge clearance. Four squat wood feet replace stone feet.
- Lid-borne oval wooden display base approx 2.65 × 1.55, z=1.97–2.04. Three lathe-profile tiered wooden trees spaced across the rear half (y=0.35–0.55), with roots touching the base. Two small gabled houses at the front (y=-0.30), warm cream wood walls and deep reddish-brown roofs, no signage. Ornament max z < 2.95. All vignette objects descendants of Lid_Hinge.
- Front = Blender -Y, rear = +Y, right = +X, up = +Z. glTF (X,Z,-Y). Same base pivot transforms. Latch release 80 degrees first, then lid 105 degrees; inspect full-open rear view.
- All moving meshes sampled against static meshes and latch against lid across 81 poses, only named mating bearing exclusions. Sampled surface checks are not manufacturing/continuous clearance certification.
- Actual saved-source poster, standalone offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes including runtime/GLB/images; no external requests. Native desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// controls, rotation, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback.

## Acceptance
Independent reopened-source theme checks: dimensions/palette maps, three tree meshes, two houses/two roofs, lid ancestry, root attachment rays, ornament height, unchanged pivot transforms. Source/export nearest vertex tolerance 1e-5; actual GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry and texture variation. Strict delivery bytes, SHA-256 inventory and archive readback. Negative control must fail the theme ancestry assertion. Visual review checks honey wood and readable toy-village silhouette; passing technical gates does not establish photorealism. No per-asset human review required for this batch.
