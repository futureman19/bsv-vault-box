# Mauritania / Richat • Eye of the Sahara

Design contract established before modeling. One original, procedurally authored landscape collectible, not a replica of historic craft or an archaeological artifact.

## Research and interpretation
- NASA Earth Observatory, Richat Structure: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/richat-structure-92071/ describes an eroded domed anticline with older exposed central rocks, igneous and sedimentary rocks and displaced strata.
- NASA Earth Observatory, The Eye of Sahara: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-eye-of-sahara-150060/ describes concentric ridges (cuestas) produced by differential erosion and surrounding longitudinal/transverse dunes.
These textual geological observations inspire original geometry and procedural maps. No NASA photographs/pixels, logos or third-party textures will be incorporated. The miniature is an artistic abstraction, not a measured geological model. This represents one Mauritanian landscape, not the country's whole culture. No sacred symbols or unsupported inscriptions.
