# South Korea / Lacquer & Pearl

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced design contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research
The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Shell and Resin exhibition describes Korean mother-of-pearl lacquer from the twelfth century through contemporary master artists: https://metmuseum.org/exhibitions/shell-and-resin . Its Mother-of-Pearl photo gallery describes minute chrysanthemum petals/leaves, pearl borders and brass-wire edges in Korean lacquer: https://metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2006/mother-of-pearl/photo-gallery . These historical Korean traditions inspire a contemporary South Korea collection design; they are not claimed exclusive to modern national borders. No museum pixels, tortoiseshell, inscriptions or actual shell scans are used.
