# Nigeria v1 — Indigo / Resist

Written before model authoring. A wide brass-fitted collectible box with deep indigo textile-inspired panels and raised circular resist motifs. This is a contemporary material study inspired by Yoruba àdìrẹ, not a claim to represent every Nigerian culture, reproduce an antique, or simulate actual dyed cloth.

## Research and interpretation
- V&A, “Àdìrẹ – 'tied and dyed' indigo textiles”: https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/adire-tied-and-dyed-indigo-textiles — describes Yoruba south-western Nigerian indigo resist-dyed cotton, raffia ties producing circles, and starch-resist patterning. Consulted through web search excerpts during this task.
- V&A collection O147380: https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O147380/adire-cloth-textile-unknown — identifies Ibadan àdìrẹ oniko, larger bound points and smaller circles made around seeds/stones; blue/white fabric made from joined panels.

Primary design reference is the documented resist-circle process, NOT a copied museum textile or photograph. All patterns are independently generated; no museum image pixels, royal iconography, sacred symbols, flags or borrowed textile designs are embedded. Gold fittings are the BoxSV family language, not a claim about historic àdìrẹ hardware. Rear/interior are contemporary extrapolations. The material is a rigid textile-covered collectible, not loose cloth or terrain.

## Measurable visual contract
- Blender axes: front -Y, hinges +Y, right +X, up Z. glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Wide body skins: width 3.72, depth 2.38, body z .10–1.56. Five-sided hollow liner; four low feet. Lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × .30, centered at z=1.828.
- Body/lid surface uses compact independently generated indigo resist-circle color atlas (512 × 512), roughness and shallow normal map. Dominant sRGB indigo approximately #17366b, pale blue resist marks approximately #b9d4dd. Uneven rings/dots and fine woven grain, not a plain blue repaint. At least 20 distinguishable resist circles in the atlas. No claim of cloth physics or photographic realism.
- Slim near-black indigo seam, at most .108 high, not a luminous strip. Narrow rear seam keeps inherited moving hinge leaves clear.
- Lid ornament: three raised indigo medallion discs across X at -1.05, 0, 1.05; each bounded by brass circular relief, center radius .25, outer radii .29–.32. These reinterpret tied circular resist marks, not a historic symbol. All lid ornament is physically seated at z≈1.98–2.05 and parented to Lid_Hinge. No tree, glacier or mountain inherited from another country.
- Warm satin brass hardware, corner caps, articulated front hasp and circular medallion, small left ring. Low indigo feet; inner lining muted midnight blue. Inherited mechanically connected hinges and pivots unchanged.
- Staged 80° latch release in the first quarter, then 105° lid rotation; same pivots as the Japan trimmed mechanism. All moving meshes included in 81-pose moving/static and latch/lid intersection sampling. Only named concentric bearing contacts exempt.
- Offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including actual GLB, Three runtime and actual-source-render poster. Open/close, rotate, interrupted reset, reduced motion, genuine parser-failure and no-JS fallbacks.

## Acceptance gates fixed before modeling
1. Independent saved-source reopen: named pivots and hierarchy, hollow floor, ten hinge attachment contacts, 81 sampled poses with zero unintended intersections.
2. Saved source vs imported GLB world-vertex maximum bidirectional distance <0.00001; packed procedural maps present.
3. Actual browser GLTFLoader: no nonfinite vertices/degenerate triangles; three ornament discs and three rings belong to lid; all three portable maps decode, color variation >30/255; atlas 512 × 512; indigo dominance and pale resist coverage measured.
4. Desktop and 390×844 touch emulation over offline file://: native control actions/rotation, full-open framing, no overflow, no HTTP requests or unexpected errors, reduced motion, parser failure and no-JS poster.
5. Build byte cap plus complete SHA-256 inventory and archive readback; explicitly require source, GLB, HTML, maps, poster, scripts and evidence. Tests must assert real data and fail when missing/mismatched. Record actual defects, not invented failures.
