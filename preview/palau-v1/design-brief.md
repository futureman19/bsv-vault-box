# Palau / Rock Islands & Lagoon — brief-driven v1

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced contract; no user-supplied Palau artwork exists. This is an original stylized collectible, not a geographic replica.

## Research
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Rock Islands Southern Lagoon: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1386/ describes forested coralline limestone islands scattered in a barrier-reef-protected marine lagoon, shaped by weather, wind and vegetation. Accessed during this task via web search.
- UNESCO inscription decision: https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/4783/ describes the forested limestone island/lagoon landscape.
These sources motivate limestone, forest and lagoon forms only. No photographs, heritage symbols, sacred imagery or traditional craft patterns are copied. Island arrangement is invented; the landscape does not represent all of Palau.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve the family wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 wide, 2.38 deep and 1.9 high; hollow liner/floor and attached brass hardware. No tall cube.
- Body: warm pale coralline-limestone interpretation, ivory/sand base (#b8b498–#ddd3b3), irregular subtle pores and mineral mottling from generated portable maps, not wood stripes. Compact maps <=1024px.
- Lid: shallow turquoise lagoon plate within the existing lid footprint, contrasting deep teal edge/reveal. Five or more asymmetric low forested limestone island miniatures with pale exposed shoreline bases and darker green canopies. Islands must visibly emerge from lagoon, not float; terrain highest point <=0.85 above lid.
- Lagoon target turquoise/teal (#167f87–#41b5ad); foliage muted evergreen (#245d3c–#57824a). Retain clear water lanes between islands and a readable water border in normal desktop/mobile view. Original island placement, not a map.
- Satin aged brass clasp, ring and restrained corner fittings inherited from functioning family mechanism; no new symbols or fabricated historical inscriptions. Narrow seam <=0.12 high.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X, up +Z. glTF mapping (X,Z,-Y). Preserve existing Lid_Hinge/Latch_Pivot and exact pivots: latch releases 80 degrees first, lid opens 105 degrees afterward. All lid terrain/water/ornament belongs to lid hierarchy.
- Collision sampling: >=81 staged poses; every moving mesh versus static and latch versus lid, only explicit existing mating bearing contacts exempt. Rooted island/lagoon geometry may intentionally overlap within its co-moving lid assembly.
- Render actual saved source for poster. Standalone offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes embeds GLB, JS and poster, no network. Rotation, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced-motion, parser-failure and no-JS poster fallback.

## Acceptance
Independent saved-source reopen; hollow interior ray and pivot/terrain ownership; full sampled collisions; source/export evaluated-vertex parity tolerance 1e-5; actual GLTFLoader finite geometry and nondegenerate triangles; decoded maps; desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// tests; visual inspection of closed/open screenshots; exact-byte SHA-256 inventory and archive readback. Negative controls must show assertions can fail. Preserve real failures and corrections. No photorealism, exact geographic fidelity, physical phone performance, continuous engineering clearance, chain storage, minting or production readiness claims.
