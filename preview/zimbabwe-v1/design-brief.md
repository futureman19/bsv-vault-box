# Zimbabwe / Granite & Kopjes — v1

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced brief, not a supplied illustration. A wide keepsake vault combines granite dry-stone courses with a miniature weathered kopje on its lid. This is an invented composition, not a reconstruction of a monument, sacred site, or traditional artisan object.

## Research
- UNESCO, Great Zimbabwe National Monument: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/364/ — cut granite blocks in regular courses and dry-stone masonry; basis for the body construction vocabulary, not a claim to replicate Great Enclosure geometry.
- UNESCO, Matobo Hills: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/306/ — distinctive granite landforms shaped by differential weathering; inspiration for rounded stacked lid boulders.
- UNESCO advisory evaluation https://whc.unesco.org/document/153151 — kopjes, domes and heaped boulders; also documents living sacred significance. No rock paintings, shrines, birds or sacred emblems reproduced.
Sources retrieved via web search excerpts. No third-party image pixels or textures used.

## Measurable visual contract
- Body width 3.72, depth approximately 2.48, external stone courses z=.10–1.56. Continuous hollow inner liner remains behind the dry-stone skin. Eight staggered block courses; narrow real gaps; at least 160 individual blocks. One-segment small bevels preserve compactness.
- Authored granite color map 256 square: warm gray/buff base near sRGB (155,143,122), subordinate darker mineral grains; separate roughness map, no wood grain. Darker liner and charcoal-olive seam, seam height .108 or less. Lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 centered z=1.828.
- Lid miniature: three overlapping rounded granite boulders forming one central offset stack, on a low oval ochre ground plinth. Top below z=3.10. One smaller side boulder; all terrain and rocks parented to Lid_Hinge. No random jagged per-vertex deformation. Plinth rests at lid top z=1.978; stack contacts checked independently.
- Satin brass corner caps, inherited hasp, solid medallion and side ring; four stone feet. Hardware is family fiction, not Zimbabwean archaeological metalwork.
- Front -Y, back +Y, right +X, up +Z. Preserve inherited lid and latch pivots exactly. Latch release 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees; 81 full-sequence collision samples cover all moving meshes. Only inherited named mating bearings exempt.
- Offline standalone HTML <5,000,000 bytes; real source-render poster; embedded GLB/runtime/maps; open/close, rotate, reset, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallbacks. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// tests. Emulation is not phone performance.

## Acceptance
Independent source reopen, source/export world-vertex parity <1e-5, real GLTFLoader finite geometry and zero degenerate triangles, lid ancestry, stack contact and silhouette bounds, map variation, exact bytes and SHA-256 inventory. Negative controls must reject detached lid ornaments and wrong geometry counts. Actual source/mobile images inspected separately from technical gates. Stylized material and invented composition; no photorealism, geographic precision or cultural completeness claimed.
