# Pakistan v1 — Multan Blue

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this contract, not a supplied illustration.

## Research
- Dawn, “Blue magic on clay”: https://dawn.com/news/859001/art-and-craft-blue-magic-on-clay describes Multan kashikari: white glaze, turquoise/deep blue, floral and geometric patterning.
- Express Tribune, “Multan’s got the blues”: https://tribune.com.pk/story/196218/multans-got-the-blues describes the living blue-pottery craft and floral designs.
These support palette/craft inspiration only. No copied photographs, sacred text, shrine replica, artisan-authorship claim or claim to represent all Pakistan. Invented floral geometry is a contemporary interpretation.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body 3.72 × 2.48, wall z .10–1.56; lid 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 centred z 1.828. Continuous hollow liner and floor retained.
- Continuous ivory ceramic side/front skins with compact repeating cobalt/turquoise floral map. Four butt-jointed panels, no overlapping coplanar corners. Deep cobalt lid. Ivory dominant body with blue floral detail; no cedar grain.
- Three raised eight-petal turquoise/ivory rosettes across lid at x -1.05,0,1.05, y 0. Each flower radius .30–.43, relief top below 2.15. All 27 petal/centre objects parent to Lid_Hinge. These are invented ornamental relief, not traditional pattern facsimiles.
- Satin brass family hardware; dark blue seam height .108; four speckled stone feet. Retain mechanically attached rear hinges, front latch, side ring and medallion. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Original pivot positions; latch release 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees. Every moving mesh collision sampled at 81 poses against static meshes; latch vs lid sampled too, with only named mating bearing exclusions.
- Procedural portable 512px color/roughness maps and 256px stone map. Source render is the only poster.
- Offline embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// interaction, framing, fallback, reduced motion and zero external requests. Physical-phone speed and photorealism not claimed.
- Independent saved-source reopening, source/export vertex parity <1e-5, actual-loader geometry, theme checks, negative control, inventory/ZIP readback. Technical acceptance is not human visual approval.
