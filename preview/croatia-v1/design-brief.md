# Croatia / Plitvice Tufa — frozen pre-model contract

## Research and interpretation
UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/98 describes Croatia's Plitvice lake system, limestone/chalk and continuing travertine barrier deposition creating lakes and waterfalls. The park authority https://np-plitvicka-jezera.hr/en/natural-and-cultural-heritage/natural-heritage/tufa/creator-of-waterfalls-and-lakes describes interconnected cascading lakes separated by growing tufa barriers. Consulted for factual landscape inspiration; no source photographs/textures copied.

This is an invented miniature of stepped water basins, not a geographic model or a claim to represent every Croatian tradition. Primary design reference is this written contract; no prior approved Croatia illustration exists.

## Visual contract
- Wide pale warm limestone/tufa box, 3.72 × 2.38 lid footprint, body exterior approximately 1.46 high; five-sided hollow liner. Cream dominant body with fine porous generated color/roughness maps, no timber finger joints.
- Three rounded irregular tufa terraces on lid, spanning X -1.45 to +1.45 and Y roughly -.85 to +.85. Highest water basin at +X, lowest at -X. Turquoise water insets at three distinct heights, connected by two narrow descending cascade ribbons. Maximum ornament Z <2.65; lid top Z 1.978. All terraces, water and small muted green bank accents parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Pale stone palette approximately sRGB #cec3a4, small darker pores; turquoise #328f8b water, muted olive bank accents and satin brass family hardware. Water stylized opaque glossy inlay, not fluid simulation or photoreal transparency.
- Dark olive narrow seam under .12 high, rear narrowed for hinge clearance. Compact brass corner caps, original family latch, side ring and four stone feet. Rear/interior invented.
- Preserve actual baseline pivot transforms and 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening. Include all moving ornaments in 81-pose moving/static and latch/lid collision sampling; only named mating bearings excluded.
