# Paraguay / Ñandutí Sunlace — visual contract before modeling

## Research and interpretation
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, https://www.britannica.com/art/nanduti : Paraguayan ñandutí is lace with spoke-like foundation threads, recalling spider webs or sun rays.
- Asociación de Ñandutí en Japón, https://nanduti.or.jp/spanish/nanduti : traditional Paraguayan lace, radial rounded forms, natural white and contemporary colored threads; Itauguá is a principal production center, alongside Pirayu and Ypacaraí.
- The Craft Atlas, https://craftatlas.co/crafts/nanduti : radial motifs are linked together on stretched cloth before release.
Sources consulted for factual inspiration, not texture/image harvesting. The written contract is the primary reference; there is no user-supplied Paraguay illustration. This is an invented craft-inspired decorative composition, NOT an authentic artisan pattern, a sacred motif, or a claim that one craft represents all Paraguay.

## Measurable design
- Preserve the wide collection mechanism envelope: about 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep, body around 1.9 tall; hollow continuous interior, inherited rear hinge / front latch pivots.
- Body: deep wine-red lacquered timber (target sRGB #692F35 to #98444A), fine procedural grain, dark interior. This timber finish is an artistic backing, not a claim of a particular Paraguayan wood species.
- Lid: warm dark timber backing carrying raised ivory and ochre sunlace-inspired openwork. One central radial rosette (radius 0.56–0.70) and four smaller radial rosettes (radius 0.25–0.36), with genuine modeled spokes/rings/petals and visible negative spaces. At least 16 spokes in central rosette. Relief 0.025–0.10 above backing; all ornaments descend from Lid_Hinge and travel with lid.
- Palette: ivory #E8D9AE, muted saffron #C79742, wine red backing; satin brass inherited fittings. No flags, text inscriptions or borrowed craft photographs. Thin dark seam <0.12 tall; gold fittings remain visually subordinate to the radial lid composition.
- Front = Blender -Y, back hinge = +Y, right = +X; glTF conversion (X,Z,-Y). Use proven 80° latch release then 105° lid opening, reverse for close; no change to pivot locations. Collision-test all moving ornaments and hardware across 81 poses, with only explicitly named inherited mating bearing exemptions.
- All maps procedural and portable; small texture budget, actual saved-source poster, no AI promotional substitution. Model is decorative rigid geometry, not physically flexible textile simulation.
