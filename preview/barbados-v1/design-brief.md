# Barbados / Coral Terraces — frozen pre-model contract

Research-led original miniature, not a supplied illustration or a claim to represent all Barbadian culture.

## Sources consulted before modeling
- https://barbados.org/architec.htm describes natural coral limestone cut from ancient sea-cliff terraces as characteristic building stone; chattel houses rest on coral blocks. Only factual inspiration is reused, not photographs or text as textures.
- https://doi.org/10.1144/gsl.jgs.1891.047.01-04.17 historical geological account describes pale porous coral limestone, successive shelves/terraces and coral/shell debris. Geological inspiration, not endorsement of every historical geological interpretation.

## Visual contract
Wide keepsake silhouette: body approx 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, height 1.9; inherited hollow liner and gold hardware, front -Y, back +Y, up Z. Pale warm ivory/cream porous limestone body (#d7c7a1 target midtone), subtle irregular pits rather than cedar grain. Four stone feet. Narrow dark teal seam, less than .12 high, interrupted for hardware clearance. Satin limestone lid with a shallow turquoise coastal inlay and three irregular stepped pale coral-rock terraces toward its back/right. Terrain extent within lid footprint; low relief max .65 above lid, not an alpine peak. Water/terraces are invented coastal composition, not geographic map or coral harvest. No flags, sacred motifs, houses or factual inscriptions.

Portable authored color/roughness/normal maps, <=1024 per dimension. Same mechanical pivots and 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening. Entire coastal ornament is lid-owned. All moving meshes included in 81-pose collision sampling; only explicit inherited mating bearing contacts exempt.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved .blend reopen; hollow floor ray; source/export world-vertex parity <1e-5; all new terrain lid ancestry; measured three stepped terraces and water/stone material separation; decoded nonconstant maps (roughness GREEN in glTF); no degenerate/nonfinite runtime triangles. Actual source-render poster must be nonblank. File:// desktop and mobile touch emulation: open/close, drag, reset, reduced motion, real corrupt-model and no-JS poster fallback, no external requests/errors/overflow. Offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes with every runtime asset embedded. Exact SHA-256 inventory/readback, folder-local byte-preserving Git attributes. Screenshots visually inspected separately from numerical tests. No photorealism, geographic accuracy, continuous collision proof or physical-phone performance claims. At most two bounded visual corrections.
