## Research and interpretation
UNESCO's [Tinian marble craftsmanship listing](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/01103) describes Tinos' marble-working knowledge, vein manipulation, vessels and fanlights, and the small tool chest presented to graduating craftspeople. Its [committee decision](https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/10.COM/10.b.17) confirms master–apprentice transmission and island identity. Consulted for factual inspiration, not copied pixels. This is an invented marble keepsake with a fanlight-inspired openwork lid relief, not an authentic traditional pattern, religious symbol, artisan-made object or reproduction of a specific historic fanlight.
