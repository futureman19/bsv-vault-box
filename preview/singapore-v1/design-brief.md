# Singapore v1 — Orchid & Celadon

Frozen written design contract before modeling. No supplied concept image; this brief is the primary reference. An invented ceramic/enamel collectible, not an antique or botanical replica.

## Research
NParks Flora & Fauna Web, https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/2/5/2539 (retrieved for this brief): Papilionanthe Miss Joaquim, commonly Vanda Miss Joaquim, was chosen as Singapore's national flower in 1981. Its flower has three sepals, two petals and a central lip; upper sepal/petals are light pink, lower sepals white, lip pink-purple with an orange center. The interpretation below uses that differentiated anatomy and palette, not third-party imagery. Celadon body and brass mounts are invented complementary design choices, not claims of a uniquely Singaporean craft.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide box: lid 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 units, center z=1.828; hollow liner and original family pivots unchanged. Body height .10–1.56 with continuous ceramic exterior panels. Pale celadon glaze, fine mottled procedural color/roughness/normal maps (256px), not wood grain.
- Dark jade narrow seam, maximum height .108; modest warm brass corner caps, front staged latch and side ring. Four dark jade feet. No copied country landscape.
- Three shallow sculptural orchid enamel blossoms on the lid, centers (-.80,-.18), (.06,.26), (.87,.00). Each has five distinct cupped lobes (top/pair pink, lower pair ivory), purple lip and amber throat. Petal extent .32–.45; relief above lid <.35. Green stems/leaves connect the arrangement. All floral objects parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Gold slim frame encloses the orchid composition; leaves dark jade. Light pink target #e8a9c8; ivory #eee8da; lip #a83a8b; celadon #83b5a7 (procedural variation allowed). Materials deliberately stylized glazed enamel, not living flowers or photoreal plants.
- Family mechanics: 80° latch release in first quarter, then 105° lid opening. Same pivot transforms; every moving mesh participates in 81-pose collision sampling, with only named mating bearings exempted. Hollow floor and hinge attachment tests retained.
- Editable packed Blender source, exported GLB, true source-render poster. Offline embedded viewer strictly <5,000,000 bytes. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// checks, reduced motion, open/close, drag, reset and corrupt-model/no-JS fallback.
- Independent source reopen, source/export vertex parity <1e-5, theme geometry/palette/parent checks, decoded maps, exact SHA-256 delivery inventory. Technical pass is not a claim of botanical precision or physical-phone performance.
