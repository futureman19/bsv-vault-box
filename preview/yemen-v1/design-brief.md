# Yemen / Sana'a Gypsum — pre-model visual contract

Primary reference is this written brief, not a supplied illustration. UNESCO's [Old City of Sana'a](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/385) describes rammed-earth and burnt-brick tower houses on stone ground floors, geometric fired-brick and white-gypsum decoration, and ochre colors. [UNESCO heritage film description](https://unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-5432) corroborates these material associations. Research retrieved before authoring. No photographs or research imagery are used as textures.

## Interpretation and measurable contract
- Wide keepsake body 3.72 × 2.48 units, wall region z=.10–1.56; inherited hollow liner and brass mechanism. Terracotta/ochre procedural mineral maps, not cedar grain; RGB target approximately (155,83,49) with fine irregular grain.
- Pale ivory gypsum-inspired horizontal bands and raised diamond frieze across front and sides; invented simplified pattern, not a reproduction of a named artisan's work.
- Lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × .30, center z=1.828; dark warm narrow seam height .108. Satin brass corner fittings, latch and ring retain family language.
- Lid miniature: three separate tower-house forms arranged along X, width .46–.58, height .60–.92 above z=1.98; earth-colored walls, ivory horizontal courses, dark paired windows with white raised surrounds. Miniature towers remain below z=3.02. Not a scale reconstruction of any actual building or complete city.
- Front=-Y, hinge=+Y, right=+X; retain original hinge/latch pivots. Every tower and lid ornament is parented to Lid_Hinge. 80-degree latch release first, then 105-degree lid opening. Sample 81 poses, every moving mesh vs static and latch vs lid; only named mating bearings excluded.
- Source .blend plus portable GLB with packed procedural color, roughness and stone maps. No external runtime requests. Full offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes, actual source-render poster, native desktop/mobile controls and reduced motion.

## Acceptance
Independent saved-source reopen and geometry parity (<1e-5), hollow floor ray, hinge attachment checks, 81-pose collisions, tower counts/bounds/parentage and gypsum features, actual GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry, file:// desktop and touch emulation, drag/open/close/interrupted reset/reduced-motion/fallback, inventory SHA-256 and ZIP readback. Tests use assertions and a deliberate detached-tower negative control. Visual inspection reports stylization honestly, not photoreal equivalence or physical-phone performance. No chain operations, deployment, or shared-kit edits.
