# United States / Canyon & Copper — frozen pre-model visual contract

## Research and interpretation
Primary design reference is this written contract, not an approved illustration. NPS Geologic Formations https://nps.gov/grca/learn/nature/geologicformations.htm describes the Colorado River cutting nearly horizontal sedimentary rocks of the Colorado Plateau, resistant cliff layers and softer slopes. NPS Journey Through Time https://nps.gov/grca/planyourvisit/upload/life_geology.pdf describes the stepped canyon profile and iron-oxide colors. Sources retrieved for factual inspiration before authoring; no photographs or source text become textures.

This is an invented Grand-Canyon-inspired miniature, not a geographic reconstruction or a representation of all American cultures. No flags, tribal motifs, sacred imagery or assertions of traditional craftsmanship. Copper-toned hardware is an artistic family variation, not a historical claim.

## Measurable contract
- Preserve wide family silhouette: outer body about 3.72 x 2.38, body height about 1.9 units, hollow five-sided liner and visible narrow dark seam <=0.12 units.
- Body: warm iron-red sandstone with thin irregular ochre/cream geological strata and fine grain; not wood grain. Palette targets terracotta #A95D3A, ochre #C08D58, limestone #D8BE91, dark gorge #48382F. Procedurally generated portable basecolor/roughness maps <=1024 pixels per axis; no photographic scans.
- Lid: two opposing stepped, irregular canyon rims with at least three distinct cliff/slope levels each, relief between 0.18 and 0.65 above the lid. A connected sinuous blue-green river in the central open gorge; width 0.10–0.22, total length >=2.2 units. Rims must not cover the river centerline. All terrain and water parented under Lid_Hinge. Avoid a solitary mountain or disconnected islands.
- Copper/brass latch, medallion, corner fittings and physically attached rear hinges; inherited hardware is explicitly family-styled, not historically accurate. Front -Y, rear +Y, up +Z. Maintain inherited pivot matrices and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening.
- Actual-source poster, compact charcoal offline viewer with United States / Canyon & Copper copy, open/close/reset, drag/touch orbit and reduced-motion snap.
- Strict whole HTML <5,000,000 bytes. All runtime bytes embedded, no requests. Editable packed .blend, GLB, maps and reproducible scripts in this directory only.

## Acceptance gates
Independently reopen source; hollow-floor ray and pivot preservation; 81 sampled poses over all moving ornaments versus static geometry and latch versus lid, with only named mating bearings exempt. Actual GLTFLoader finite geometry/no degenerate triangles, texture variation in actual glTF channels, lid ancestry, source/export parity <=1e-5. Theme checks measure rim levels, terrain bounds, connected water and exposed centerline, and preserve a deliberate failing negative control. Browser desktop and 390x844 touch emulation over file://: native open/close, drag, reset during motion, reduced motion, actual corrupt-parser and no-JS poster fallback, zero network/errors/overflow. Inspect normal closed/open desktop/mobile screenshots. Hash/size inventory plus archive readback. These establish sampled technical acceptance, not photorealism, continuous collision certification or physical-phone performance.
