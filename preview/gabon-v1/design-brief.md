# Gabon v1 — Okoume & Loango

Written before modeling. A contemporary, landscape-inspired collectible: rose-brown okoume-like timber, a dark green coastal canopy and winding lagoon relief on the lid. Not a replica of a Gabonese ceremonial object, not harvested timber, and not a botanical or geographic scale survey. No sacred motifs or national claims.

## Research and interpretation
- USDA Forest Products Laboratory, Aucoumea klaineana: https://fpl.fs.usda.gov/documnts/TechSheets/Chudnoff/African/new_html_docs/aucoum1new.html — distribution includes Gabon; heartwood salmon pink to light pinkish brown, straight/shallowly interlocked/sometimes wavy grain. Search-retrieved source text informs authored color/grain, not copied imagery.
- Gabon biodiversity clearing-house: https://ga.chm-cbd.net/en/protected-areas/loango-national-park — search-index description identifies western Gabon coastal habitats and Iguela Lagoon. Direct retrieval returned 504; do not imply full-page access.
- Journeys by Design: https://journeysbydesign.com/destinations/gabon/loango-national-park — indexed description locates Loango between Nkomi and Ndogo lagoons and lists beaches, jungle, savanna and mangroves. Landscape palette inspiration, not exact mapping.
All textures and geometry independently procedural. No third-party photograph is embedded.

## Measurable visual contract
- Keep wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body/lid around 1.9 high, inherited hollow liner and mechanical structure. Front -Y, back +Y, right +X; exported glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body/lid rim warm salmon-brown timber, target sRGB midtones around #A56C55 with darker interlocked grain; compact generated color and roughness maps <=1024 per dimension. Not Japan's cedar or finger-joint/tree composition.
- Satin gold/brass family fittings with visible rivets, front articulated hasp, seated rear hinge leaves and bearings. Keep original Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot origins and staged 80-degree latch then 105-degree lid motion.
- Narrow dark-green seam <=0.10 height, interrupted around bearings/hasp; no floating or oversized lawn strip.
- Lid landscape: low asymmetric coastal forest bank occupying rear/side of lid, at least three visible broadleaf canopy groupings (not cedar needles), a continuous curved blue-green lagoon ribbon with a sand-colored bank. Relief entirely within lid footprint except natural canopy overhang <=0.12; ornament height <=0.85 above lid. Warm timber remains visible around landscape. Forest deep greens around #254F35/#467044; lagoon #245C61; sand #B8A47B. Colors are design targets, not measured local ecology.
- All landscape, canopy, lagoon, sand and lid fittings parent to moving lid. No national text/flag required. Interior/rear are family extrapolations.
- Actual saved-source render as poster. Whole offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes; embedded runtime/model/maps/poster, no network assets, native controls and reduced-motion behavior.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source reopening, hollow liner and seated hardware checks; evaluated source/export vertex parity within 0.00001 units; actual GLTFLoader finite vertices, nondegenerate triangles, map variation and lid ancestry. 81 staged motion samples covering every moving mesh vs static surfaces and latch vs lid; only explicitly named mating bearing contacts exempt, no blanket terrain exemption. Theme tests check dimensions, material variation, lagoon continuity and canopy count/placement against geometry. Offline file:// Edge desktop and touch-emulated 390x844 mobile: drag, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced motion, real corrupt-GLB poster fallback/no-JS fallback, no external requests/errors/overflow, open silhouette fits. Exact byte counts and SHA-256 inventory with required-file assertions. Tests assert and exit nonzero, retain genuine failures if encountered. Sampled intersections are not continuous physical certification; mobile emulation is not phone performance; no photorealism claim.
