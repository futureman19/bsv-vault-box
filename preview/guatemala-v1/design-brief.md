# Guatemala v1 — Atitlán & Woven Indigo

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this researched contract, not a supplied illustration. A stylized original collectible, not a reproduction of a community's garment or a geographic survey.

## Sources consulted
- Museo Ixchel, https://museoixchel.org/en/the-threads-tell-a-story/ — retrieved page describes Mayan weaving as a living, changing textile tradition fundamental to indigenous Guatemalan communities. Informational research only; no textile photographs or specific sacred/community motifs copied.
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, https://britannica.com/place/Lake-Atitlan — retrieved search text identifies the southwestern Guatemala highland lake and three border volcanoes Atitlán, Tolimán and San Pedro. Use lake/volcanic silhouettes as inspiration, not a scale map or geological claim.

## Frozen measurable design contract
- Retain the wide family keepsake silhouette: approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep, body around 1.9 tall, hollow five-sided interior. Front is Blender -Y; rear +Y, right +X. glTF coordinates (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: deep indigo woven-looking panels with visible narrow crimson, ochre and cream stripe accents. Colors target indigo #243555, crimson #a83e48, ochre #c39a50, cream #d8c9a1. This invented stripe rhythm is NOT an authentic huipil pattern. Portable generated color/roughness/normal maps, at most 1024 per axis, no photos.
- Lid: dark volcanic stone border and low asymmetric terrain framing a contiguous blue-green lake inset, visible from normal desktop and mobile views. Lake footprint at least 1.3 × 0.6 units. Three distinct irregular volcanic peaks on rear half, heights 0.30–0.75 above lid; no generic tree, glacier, temple or copied glyphs. Peaks differ in width/height; low front shore leaves lake visible. Entire landscape belongs to Lid_Hinge.
- Warm satin brass family latch and connected rear hinges, restrained corner fittings. Narrow dark reveal/gasket <0.12 high; preserve original bearing pivots and 80° latch release before 105° lid opening. No detached or floating terrain.
- All moving meshes sampled against static meshes, latch against lid, over 81 progress poses. Exempt only explicit mating bearings; include new landscape. Source/export vertex tolerance 1e-5; finite real-loader triangles, zero degenerates.
- Actual source poster, standalone embedded offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes, no runtime network requests. Rotation, staged opening/closing, reset, reduced motion, real corrupt-GLB fallback and no-JS fallback. Test file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile.
- Independent saved-source reopening, theme/material/ancestry checks, source/export parity, exact SHA-256 inventory and archive readback. Negative control(s) must demonstrate acceptance can fail. Preserve real failures and fixes.

## Limits
Procedural cloth and terrain are intentionally stylized, not woven-fiber simulation, photoreal equivalence, indigenous endorsement or accurate bathymetry. Sampled clearance is not continuous mechanical certification. Mobile emulation is not physical-phone performance. Cosmetic opening does not change custody or any on-chain state. No minting, hosting, network service, keys or chain queries.
