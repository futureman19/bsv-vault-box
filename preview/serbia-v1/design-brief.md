# Serbia — Pirot / Woven Crimson
Frozen before model authoring. Original geometric composition, not a reproduction of a museum textile or claim to represent all Serbian cultures.

## Research
- Serbia Intangible Cultural Heritage, https://nkns.etnografskimuzej.rs/en/popis-nkns/rug-making-pirot : Pirot wool tapestry woven on vertical looms; central fields and border systems; prevailing red in multiple shades.
- Ethnographic Museum Belgrade, https://etnografskimuzej.rs/en/zbirka/textile-furnishings/ : a Pirot 1792 kilim uses stepwise concentric diamonds. These facts inform invented shapes only. No museum image pixels or sacred/figural motifs are reproduced.

- Dark walnut edges, satin brass latch/corner protectors, four squat walnut feet. Muted dark seam height .09 (<.12); rear strip narrower for hinge clearance.
- Lid textile panel 3.10 × 1.78 × .024, center z 1.991. Three ivory stepped-diamond reliefs centered x -1,0,1, y 0; footprint .68 × .86; relief z 2.004–2.034, indigo smaller centers z 2.034–2.046. All six relief meshes and panel are Lid_Hinge children. Relief intentionally decorative inlay rather than literal woven wool geometry.
- Same original rear lid pivot and latch pivot. Front -Y, rear +Y. Latch releases 80 degrees during first quarter; lid subsequently opens 105 degrees. All new moving geometry included in 81-pose surface-collision sampling; only named mating bearing contacts exempted.
- Portable generated maps <=512 per dimension, nonzero decoded variation. No photographic-scan or traditional-pattern-authenticity claim.
- All-runtime-embedded offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes; actual-source-render poster. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// interaction, reset, reduced motion, corruption and no-JS fallbacks.
