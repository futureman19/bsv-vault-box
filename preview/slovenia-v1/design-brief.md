# Slovenia — Soča & Alpine Limestone

Written before model authoring. Primary design reference is this research-backed contract, not a supplied illustration. A stylized landscape jewel, not a geographic replica or traditional craft attribution.

## Research
- Slovenian Tourist Board, https://www.slovenia.info/en/places-to-go/attractions/soca-valley : emerald-green Soča, rocky gorges, pools and Julian Alps.
- Slovenian Tourist Board, https://www.slovenia.info/uploads/publikacije/en/julijske_alpe_2014_-_ang.pdf : Julian Alps formed from Mesozoic marine sediments, fractured and folded into peaks; Soča rises on their Primorska side.
- Triglav National Park, https://www.tnp.si/en/park/nature-and-culture/geology : grey limestone mountain groups, sharp ridges, marine limestone deposition, tectonic folding and water erosion. Sources retrieved through web search; the separate extraction backend was unavailable. No source photographs, logos, heraldry or text will be embedded as art.

## Frozen visual contract
- Family wide box: 3.72 X by 2.38 Y lid, slab bottom 1.678/top 1.978; body exterior approximately 3.72 by 2.48, floor-to-rim 1.56. Hollow inherited five-sided liner. Front -Y, rear +Y, up Z.
- Pale warm-grey limestone-inspired body, irregular fine fracture map, not wood courses or finger joints. Target generated albedo RGB approximately (169–213,173–217,164–208), restrained roughness variation. Dark jade reveal under 0.11 high. Four irregular stone feet.
- Satin antique brass inherited latch, medallion, side ring and corner fittings. Retain original pivot matrices and bearing seats; latch 80 degrees releases before 105 degree lid opening.
- Lid is a low miniature gorge: continuous sinuous emerald channel spanning X -1.4 to +1.4, width .16–.26. Two irregular pale rock banks with unequal jagged ridges, maximum top below 2.85. Channel low and visible from hero/mobile camera, green bank accents subordinate to stone. All landscape meshes parented to Lid_Hinge; not an exact model of Triglav or the actual Soča route.
- No tree, cedar fingers, national flags, text decals or fabricated cultural inscriptions. River is opaque polished turquoise/emerald mineral inlay, not physical water simulation.
- Source-render poster only, standalone embedded offline viewer <5,000,000 bytes, desktop/mobile native controls, drag, reset, reduced motion, corrupt-model and no-JS fallbacks.
