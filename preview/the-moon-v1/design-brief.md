## Frozen visual contract
- Theme: **Regolith & Impact**. Wide keepsake body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body top near 1.9–2.1 Z using inherited mechanism dimensions. Hollow five-sided inner shell, continuous floor. Gray regolith/breccia body with fine irregular mottling, no wood grain or foliage.
- Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, up +Z. Keep inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot transforms exactly. Gold/brass articulated hasp, medallion, seated rear hinges and compact corner hardware are collection fantasy, not lunar artifacts.
- Narrow dark seam, under 0.12 units high. Pale neutral-gray highland lid relief enclosing one dark mare-like floor and at least three individually readable bowl craters of unequal size. Dominant crater diameter 0.8–1.2 units; its rim must rise at least 0.06 above its depressed floor. At least one smaller crater diameter 0.25–0.55. Raised rims must be terrain geometry, not painted circles or separate floating torus ornaments. Relief stays within lid footprint and below 0.5 units above lid top; all relief belongs to moving lid.
- Body/surface sRGB target neutral gray approximately #777875 to #b4b4ac; dark mare #34383b to #55595c; subdued warm brass contrast. Variation is generated dust/rock texture, not terrestrial strata, a scan or photographic realism. Compact portable base color and roughness maps no larger than 512 per dimension; optional normal map equally bounded.
- Staged animation: latch releases 80 degrees in first quarter, then lid opens 105 degrees. Reverse sequence closes correctly. All moving geometry included in 81-pose surface-collision sampling against static parts and latch-versus-lid; only explicit inherited mating bearings exempt. No continuous collision/manufacturing claim.
- Viewer includes rotation, open/close, reset, reduced-motion, real-source poster fallback, and no external assets. Entire HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes.
