# Mali v1 — Bandiagara Ochre

Written before modeling. A wide ochre sandstone collectible with an asymmetric miniature escarpment rising from its lid. This is a landscape interpretation, not a scale monument or representation of all Malian cultures. No sacred symbols, masks, shrines or invented inscriptions. No supplied primary artwork exists for Mali; the following original geometric contract is the reference.

## Research and provenance
- UNESCO, Cliff of Bandiagara (Land of the Dogons), https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/516/ describes sandstone plateau, escarpment and plains, with cliffs, rock shelters and erosion features. Retrieved through indexed search; direct HTTP retrieval returned 403. This grounds the plateau-and-cliff silhouette, not a claim of geological accuracy.
- Archnet, Djenne, https://archnet.org/sites/272/media_contents/50596 describes banco earth construction and its plastic forms. Retrieved search excerpt. This provides contextual warm-earth material inspiration only; no mosque or building is reproduced.
All geometry and maps are original procedural work. No source photographs, text or cultural artifacts are embedded. Hardware is a BoxSV family extrapolation, not traditional Malian metalwork.

## Measurable visual contract
- Retain wide family silhouette approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y with body height approximately 1.9. Front latch -Y, rear hinges +Y, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Ochre body: dominant warm ochre/sand base-color map target mean RGB in ranges R 130–205, G 75–150, B 35–100. Fine irregular pores and broken sediment variation, not Morocco's pink banding or Japanese wood. Portable generated color and roughness maps at most 512 square per image; hollow continuous interior liner remains.
- Warm dark bronze/brass family fittings, inherited functional ring/clasp and connected rear hinges. Narrow dark-brown seam under 0.12 height. No bright vegetation strip.
- Low sandstone lid with an asymmetric escarpment along its rear half, approximately 2.5–3.1 wide, 0.65–1.2 deep, 0.45–0.85 high above the lid. At least three unequal-height plateau shoulders, irregular ledges and recessed gullies; no single cone or rectangular pedestal. Broad flat-ish upper strata and steep broken front faces should read at mobile scale. Terrain belongs to Lid_Hinge and travels as one rigid assembly. No trees/glaciers/buildings.
- Preserve existing pivot coordinates and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Sample at least 81 poses including ALL moving geometry against static geometry and latch against lid; only specifically named actual mating bearings exempt. Do not waive ornament collisions.
- Actual Blender-rendered poster, editable packed .blend, GLB, maps, reproducible local scripts. Offline file:// HTML with bundled runtime, rotation/open/close/reset and reduced-motion support; total HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes.

Stylized procedural miniature, not photorealism, continuous collision certification, physical-phone testing, launch approval or on-chain storage.
