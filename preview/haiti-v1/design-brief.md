# Haiti — Cut Steel & Garden

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this written contract, not a supplied illustration.

## Research / interpretation
SFO Museum, The Enduring Spirit of Haitian Metal Sculpture, https://sfomuseum.org/exhibitions/haitian-metal-sculpture describes Georges Liautaud and the 1950s Croix-des-Bouquets cut-metal tradition, flattened recycled oil drums, hand tools, embossing and bending, and natural as well as religious subjects. Singing Rooster, https://singingrooster.org/about-haitian-art describes hammer/chisel cutting and stippling. Retrieved for this task via web search. These inform material and process inspiration only. No artist's composition, sacred symbol, photograph or signature is reproduced. This digitally authored leafy medallion is an invented garden design, not Haitian artisan work or a claim to represent all Haiti.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth 2.48, skin from z .10 to 1.56; continuous hollow liner. Four satin steel feet. Dark blue-charcoal hammered steel, approximate sRGB base (55,69,79), subtle compact procedural stippling, warm gray metal cut edges. No painted wood grain.
- Four exterior steel panels, a narrow dark seam at z 1.618 under .11 high, continuous rounded lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centered z 1.828. Gold/brass collection latch, side ring, small corner caps; fixed rear mounts physically meet liner and moving leaves meet lid.
- A low horizontal openwork garden medallion on the lid: ellipse span about 2.65 x 1.65, thin steel stem with six branching pairs and twelve pointed solid-metal leaves. Real gaps between leaves, embossed midribs; central stem and border physically sit just above the lid. All parts lid-parented. Ornament maximum z below 2.20; no landmark or botanical fidelity claim.
- Brass rims highlight warm cut edges; dark steel on body/lid, brighter satin gray foliage makes silhouette legible at normal mobile size. Procedural color/roughness/normal maps <=512 per dimension.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X; exported glTF (X,Z,-Y). Original Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot positions unchanged. 80-degree latch release in first quarter, then 105-degree lid opening. Every moving mesh collision sampled across 81 poses, only explicit mating bearings excluded.
- Offline embedded runtime, GLB and actual source-render poster; HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes. No external requests. Open/close, drag, reset interruption, reduced motion and corrupt-GLB/no-JS poster acceptance on desktop and touch-emulated mobile file://.

## Acceptance / limits
Independently reopen source, compare exported geometry within 1e-5, check real loader degeneracy/finite data, texture variation and ornament ancestry/count/bounds. Include an intentionally detached leaf negative control. Check source-render pixels and inspect desktop/mobile actual screenshots. Sampled collisions are not continuous engineering certification; no phone hardware performance or photorealism claim. Pure cosmetic local art; no chain operations, issuance, deployment or live vault behavior.
