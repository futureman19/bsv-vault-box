# Marshall Islands / Pandanus & Swell

Brief frozen before authoring. Original contemporary collectible design, not a replica of a named chart or traditional clothing mat. No supplied concept artwork. Primary reference is this written visual contract.

## Research
- Waan Aelõñ in Majel (WAM), local canoe-building and training organization: https://canoesmarshallislands.com/author/adm-wam-clem/page/2 describes navigation teaching charts, coconut midribs/pandanus roots, coconut-sennit bindings, shells or coral markers and curved sticks indicating wave patterns. It also describes preparing pandanus strips for woven mats. Charts were memorized rather than taken to sea.
- Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History: https://naturalhistory.si.edu/research/anthropology/programs/recovering-voices/outreach/news-research-updates/canoe-collections-pacific-islands describes Marshallese charts as material records of Indigenous ocean-wave navigation knowledge, using pandanus roots and marine shells.
Research inspires materials and the idea of wave learning, not a functional navigational diagram. No historic artifact pixels, exact traditional designs, sacred signs or third-party textures are copied. The decorative curve arrangement and atoll silhouette are invented; neither is a geographic map.

## Honesty limits
Stylized miniature craft/landscape tribute, not photorealism, not artisan-made, not an accurate traditional teaching chart, not geography or nautical guidance. Mobile emulation is not physical-phone performance. Motion tests are sampled surface-intersection tests, not continuous collision/manufacturing certification. Cosmetic animation changes no custody state. No deployment, chain access or minting.
