# Tanzania / Kilimanjaro & Ochre — frozen before modeling

## Research and interpretation
Primary factual reference: UNESCO, Kilimanjaro National Park, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/403/ (read in browser 2026-11-04 after direct HTTP returned 403). UNESCO describes an isolated volcanic massif above savannah, a snowy summit, a surrounding montane forest, and three peaks Kibo, Mawenzi and Shira. Its conservation text explicitly warns of glacier retreat. This miniature is an invented landscape-inspired collectible, not an accurate topographic model or a statement of present snow coverage. Ochre earth, charcoal volcanic slopes, olive foothills and an ivory summit are a designed palette, not sampled geology. No third-party images/textures or sacred/cultural patterns are copied. The written contract is the primary design reference; there is no supplied Tanzania concept image.
