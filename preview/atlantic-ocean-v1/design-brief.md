## Research
NOAA Ocean Exploration, https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/mid-ocean-ridge/ describes the slowly spreading Mid-Atlantic Ridge, basalt volcanism, steep irregular relief and central rift valley. NOAA https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/news/noaa-dives-mid-atlantic-ridge/ describes its north–south Atlantic extent and hydrothermal sites. These facts inspire a split submarine ridge, not a geographically accurate bathymetric map. No research images are embedded or traced. No cultural craft tradition is asserted for an ocean.
