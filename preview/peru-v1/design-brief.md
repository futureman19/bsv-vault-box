# Peru v1 — Andean Stone & Terraces

Frozen before modeling. Primary reference is this written contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research
UNESCO Historic Sanctuary of Machu Picchu, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/274/ (retrieved via web search): describes stone walls, terraces and ramps integrated with rock escarpments, on the eastern slopes of the Peruvian Andes at the meeting with the Amazon Basin. This supports a stone-and-green-terrace landscape miniature. No source photographs or text are embedded as textures. This is an invented landscape composition, not a reconstruction of Machu Picchu, a sacred symbol, or a claim to represent all Peru.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep × 1.9 high; hollow five-sided liner. Family brass fittings and stone feet.
- Body: warm grey stone (#898377 target midtone), dark narrow irregular masonry joints, subtle mineral grain. Real geometric inset block boundaries, not only painted joints; at least three staggered courses on each visible long wall. No cedar grain or Iceland glacier.
- Lid: warm stone rim with narrow dark green seam <0.12 high. Main landscape contained within x ±1.55, y ±0.9, rising no more than 1.1 above lid. At least five visibly stepped green terraces ascending toward an asymmetric rear rocky ridge. Terrace stone risers and muted green tops distinct; no copied archaeological architecture or invented inscriptions.
- Brass hardware target #b58c48, moss/terrace greens #4e6540 and #778257, stone palette warm grey with darker crevices. Procedural portable color/roughness maps, ≤1024 pixels per dimension; no scans.
- Coordinates front=-Y, rear=+Y, right=+X; glTF (X,Z,-Y). All terrace/ridge details descend from Lid_Hinge and travel with it.
- Preserve original pivots and staged latch release 80° then lid opening 105°. Sample 81 poses, moving/static and latch/lid intersections, only explicit mating bearings exempt.
- Offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes including bundled runtime, GLB and actual-source poster. Drag/touch, Open/Close, Reset and reduced-motion supported.

## Acceptance
Independent source reopening, hollow floor ray, pivot preservation, full moving-part collision samples, source/export parity, actual GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry, texture variation in correct channels, terrace count/ancestry/bounds, file:// desktop/mobile native controls, no external requests/errors/overflow, corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster fallback, exact SHA-256 inventory and archive readback. Preserve any genuine failure and repair; include a deliberate negative control. Visual QA at desktop/mobile scale remains separate from technical gates. This is stylized authored geometry, not photoreal equivalence or physical-phone testing.
