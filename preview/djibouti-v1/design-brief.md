# Djibouti / Assal Salt Rift — brief-driven v1

Written before authoring. An original procedural landscape interpretation, not a replica of an existing artwork or all Djiboutian culture.

## Research
UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Djibouti's tentative submission, Le Lac Assal: https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5959 (accessed 2026-11-02). Describes an endorheic salt lake in the Afar depression, a rift graben, surrounding black volcanic lava and traditional salt commerce. This is a tentative-list submission, NOT inscription as World Heritage. No salinity superlative is adopted.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Assal_(Djibouti) describes basalt formations and bands of salt/white gypsum; used as secondary context only. No third-party photographs or text are embedded as art.

- Lid: a low irregular white salt shelf encircling a clearly visible turquoise/teal lake inset (#268f98 to #63b9b9). Real raised salt rim and modest asymmetric basalt escarpment, not merely a lake painted on a flat lid. Terrain below 0.65 units above lid. No claim of geographic scale/cartographic accuracy.
