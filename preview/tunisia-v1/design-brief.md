# Tunisia / Sejnane Clay — frozen before modeling

Research: UNESCO https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/01406 and committee decision https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/13.COM/10.b.38 describe women of Sejnane shaping terracotta utensils and animal figurines, decorated with two-tone geometric patterns. AFP/France24 https://france24.com/en/20190516-mother-daughter-tunisia-potters-pass-ancestral-know-how describes cream surfaces with black and red motifs and white clay slip. These are factual inspiration, not copied images, artisan endorsement or a claim to reproduce traditional symbolic patterns.

Primary design reference is this written contract, not a supplied artwork. Invented abstract chevrons and a shallow circular dish recall material/craft; they carry no claimed traditional meaning. One regional tradition is not all Tunisia.
