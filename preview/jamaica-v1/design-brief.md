# Jamaica / Blue & John Crow — frozen before modeling

## Research and interpretation
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1356/ : rugged, densely forested southeastern Jamaican mountains, upper montane communities, and the landscape's importance as a Maroon refuge. Search-retrieved description consulted for this brief. No religious or Maroon symbols are reproduced.
- UNEP-WCMC World Heritage datasheet, http://world-heritage-datasheets.unep-wcmc.org/datasheet/output/site/blue-and-john-crow-mountains/ : montane forest over shale in the Blue Mountains versus wet limestone forest in the John Crow Mountains.
These inform an invented paired-landscape keepsake, not a mapped miniature, stone specimen, traditional craft reconstruction or comprehensive representation of Jamaica. No source photographs/textures are embedded. A pale limestone body supports a darker blue-green forest ridge: the title refers to the two mountain ranges, not blue pigment in natural vegetation.
