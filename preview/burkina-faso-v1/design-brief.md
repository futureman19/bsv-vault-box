# Burkina Faso — Earthen Courtyard

Design contract written before modeling. This is an original architectural/material interpretation, not a replica, traditional artifact, or community-endorsed design.

## Research
- UNESCO, Royal Court of Tiébélé: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1713 — earthen architectural complex, enclosed compounds, distinct concessions and passageways; Kasena cultural context. Women preserve the knowledge of the symbolic mural decoration.
- UNESCO inscription decision: https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/8600 — earth, wood, cow dung and straw construction; decorated architecture is a living tradition.
- ICOMOS evaluation: https://whc.unesco.org/document/208499 — low earthen structures and banco walls, within a tradition spanning southern Burkina Faso and northern Ghana.

Use warm earth and low enclosed architectural forms as inspiration, not a claim to represent all Burkina Faso cultures. Do not copy sacred/symbolic mural motifs, people, inscriptions or third-party photographs. All maps generated locally. Abstract cream/charcoal lines are newly designed framing, not authenticated Kasena signs. No promotional reference supplied; the brief is the primary visual reference.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height approximately 1.9; preserve inherited hollow liner and mechanism anchors. Front = -Y, rear hinges = +Y, up = +Z; glTF = (X,Z,-Y).
- Rounded warm ochre/terracotta earthen walls, generated fine plaster grain, restrained dark and cream linear decorative borders. Palette targets: terracotta #A95E39, ochre #CB9254, cream #E6D0A0, charcoal #302720. Material roughness 0.65–0.95; no glossy plastic body.
- Dark narrow reveal/seam <=0.12 high, inset clear of hinges. Small antique brass corner fittings and inherited articulated brass clasp; no giant armor plates. Four low earthen/stone feet.
- Lid contains a compact abstract earthen courtyard: low connected enclosure walls around an open space, at least three low rounded architectural volumes, varied roof heights 0.18–0.55 above lid. Entire ornament within X +/-1.55 and Y +/-0.9, seated on lid. No tree/glacier or foreign landmark. All ornament parented to Lid_Hinge and moves rigidly with it.
- Preserve exact inherited pivots, 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening; sample 81 poses including every moving ornament against static meshes and latch against lid. Exempt only explicitly named intentional bearing contacts.
- Portable generated base-color, normal and roughness maps, preferably 512px or smaller. Actual source-render poster, never AI artwork.
- Standalone offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes, all runtime embedded, native open/close/reset/drag, reduced motion, actual corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source reopen, source/export vertex parity <=1e-5, hollow floor and hinge attachment checks, all-moving-part sampled collisions; real GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry and theme parentage; desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// checks without network or page errors; exact byte/hash inventory and delivery validation. Capture closed/open screenshots and inspect the actual visuals. Tests must fail on violated assertions; record any real failed runs rather than erase them. Mobile emulation is not phone-hardware validation; sampled collision checks are not continuous engineering certification. Technical acceptance does not imply photorealism, cultural authenticity, minting, hosting or user visual approval.
