## Materials and acceptance
Warm rose/ochre smooth dressed stone with undulating horizontal bands, not basalt. Thick dark polished cedar lid. Individual closed ceramic prisms in repeating green octagons, ivory angular/star accents and near-black details; fine visible joints, not a random pixel texture. A thin raised star inlay is a stylization rather than a replica of any particular historic tile panel. Restrained brass crown/corner wraps/clasp/solid medallion/ring. Reddish Atlas ridges only: no tree, moss, glacier or river inherited.
All maps are generated compact UV images, not scans or photographs. Stone species cannot be identified from the concept; do not claim the object depicts a verified local quarry. The miniature is a fictional Atlas-inspired massif, not surveyed Toubkal.
