# Turkey / Iznik Tulip — pre-model visual contract

This written brief is the primary reference; no supplied concept illustration exists. An original, stylized ceramic keepsake inspired by Iznik floral pottery, not an antique replica or a claim to represent all Turkish cultures.

## Research
- Metropolitan Museum of Art, Dish Depicting Two Birds among Flowering Plants: https://metmuseum.org/toah/works-of-art/59.69.1 — curator discussion identifies cobalt blue, turquoise, tomato red and green on white, with stylized tulips, roses and hyacinths.
- Museum With No Frontiers, Iznik dish, Stockholm NM 0046/1899: https://islamicart.museumwnf.org/export.php?id=object;ISL;se;Mus01;17;en — ceramic painted/glazed, later sixteenth-century floral repertoire and curved stems. Sources consulted as text only; no museum imagery embedded or copied.

## Contract before authoring
- Wide box: body width 3.72, depth 2.48, exterior from z .10 to 1.56; continuous hollow inherited liner. Lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centered at z 1.828. No wooden finger joints or Japan tree.
- Ivory glazed body with repeating original cobalt/turquoise floral medallions in a compact 512px procedural map; independent nonconstant roughness map. Dominant ivory approximately #e6dfcc, cobalt #173e83, turquoise #288d99, red #bb3931. Finishes are stylized, not measured historical pigments.
- Deep cobalt lid and four cobalt feet. Warm satin brass hinges, long family latch, ring and corner protectors. Narrow dark blue seam under .12 high, rear narrowed to clear hinges.
- Ivory inset ceramic lid panel, 3.20 x 1.86, with three raised red tulips on curved green stems, paired green leaves; original symmetric floral composition, no religious text or copied emblem. Relief mounted to the lid, within x +/-1.6, y +/- .93, z 1.98–2.13. This relief reinterpretation is not a claim that historic painted dishes were sculpted this way.
- Coordinate convention front -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z. Preserve inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot matrices; staged 80-degree latch then 105-degree lid. Include all moving decoration in 81-pose collision sampling, only explicit original mating bearings excepted.
- Actual-source poster; standalone embedded offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes. No network requests, native desktop/mobile-emulated controls, reduced motion, reset and corrupt-model/no-JS fallback.

## Acceptance
Saved source reopening, named ornament counts/bounds/ancestry, packed map variation, source/export nearest vertex parity <1e-5, independent baseline pivot comparison, hollow floor and hinge attachment checks, all-part motion sampling, actual browser geometry and viewport gates, inventory SHA-256/ZIP readback. Negative control removes one tulip from lid ownership and must fail. Browser emulation is not physical-phone testing. No photorealism, historical replica, minting, hosting or custody claim.
