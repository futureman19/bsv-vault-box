# China / Cinnabar & Peony

Frozen before modeling. Original digital interpretation of Chinese carved red lacquer, not a historical replica or a claim to represent all Chinese craft.

## Research
The Metropolitan Museum's [Cinnabar exhibition](https://metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2009/cinnabar/photo-gallery) describes Southern Song red lacquer boxes carved with peonies and other flowers; late-Ming square boxes combine floral cartouches and dense geometric side designs. Its [Xuande covered box](https://metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/748352) is catalogued as carved red lacquer. Only factual text informs this invented arrangement; no museum images or textures copied.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide body 3.72 x 2.48 units, outer wall z .10–1.56; rounded lid 3.72 x2.38 x.30 centered z1.828. Dark hollow liner retained; four dark-red feet.
- Dominant deep cinnabar red (map R 110–175, G 18–48, B 15–35), oxblood recesses; fine layered variation, no wood grain. Portable 256px color, roughness and normal maps; roughness .23–.40. Satin brass family hardware and medallion, original hinge pivots.
- Continuous ceramic-like lacquer skins butt at corners, no overlapping outer coplanar skins. Original geometric side-band lozenges are a contemporary interpretation, not an authenticated pattern.
- Three original peony-inspired rosettes, each 16 smoothly rounded raised petals in two rings; petal relief above lid no higher than .30 units. Winding red stems and six pointed leaves, all directly lid-parented; recessed dark red cartouche supports the relief. Main flower centered (-.65,0), secondary (.65,.35), small (.75,-.5). Ornament fits inside x +/-1.58 y +/-1.0. No copied writing, dragons, sacred symbols or historical marks.
- Narrow dark seam .108 high. Brass corner caps retained. 80-degree latch release first then 105-degree lid motion on unchanged pivots. 81-pose sampled checks include all ornaments and hardware; only original named mating bearings excluded.
- Offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes with embedded GLB/runtime and actual source-render poster. Desktop plus touch-emulated mobile file:// controls, reset, reduced motion, corrupt-data and no-JS fallbacks. No network.

## Limits
Script-authored stylized relief, not hand-carved lacquer, exact botanical anatomy, photorealism, physical-phone performance or continuous mechanical proof. Cosmetic opening only; no blockchain work or deployment.
