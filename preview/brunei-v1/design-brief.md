Primary reference is this written brief, not a supplied concept image. Research consulted before authoring:
- Brunei Tourism, Arts and Handicraft Training Centre: https://www.bruneitourism.com/places/brunei-arts-and-handicraft-training-centre/ — kain tenunan brocades use shimmering gold and silver threads; the centre sustains traditional crafts.

Interpretation: a burgundy textile-faced keepsake box with gold geometric thread relief. The invented diamond lattice is NOT a copied or certified traditional Jongsarat motif. No sacred/royal insignia, photographs or third-party texture pixels. This is one craft-inspired interpretation, not all Brunei culture.
