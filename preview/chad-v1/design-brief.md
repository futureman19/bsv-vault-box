# Chad / Ennedi Windstone — v1 visual contract

Written before modeling. One landscape-inspired collectible, not a representation of all Chadian cultures and not a literal scale replica of a named arch.

## Research and original design
UNESCO's Ennedi Massif listing https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1475 identifies northeastern Chad's sandstone plateau, sculpted by water and wind into cliffs, natural arches and pitons. IUCN https://worldheritageoutlook.iucn.org/node/1188 corroborates that landscape and the importance of permanent canyon water. Research retrieved by web search for this brief; no photographs, rock-art motifs, sacred signs or source prose are embedded in the art. The design is an original procedural interpretation, not a scan. No supplied Chad reference image exists.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: stone body width 3.72, depth 2.38; floor/base near z=.1, wall top 1.56. Continuous inherited hollow liner behind four ochre sandstone exterior panels. Front -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X; export (X,Z,-Y).
- Sandstone palette: ochre, burnt orange, muted cream; generated 512px color map with irregular thin strata and subordinate pores; separate roughness and mineral-grain map. No cedar, foliage or glacier forms.
- Satin sandstone lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centered z=1.828. Dark brown narrow .108 seam, rear narrowed to avoid hinge leaves. Restrained brass corner caps, family latch/hinges; four dark stone feet.
- Landmark: an asymmetric freestanding eroded arch across the lid, centered near y=.28; outer span about 2.1, raised height .8 above lid; genuine open negative space at least .7 wide and .35 high, not a painted arch. Angular buttresses and small wind-carved pitons, all attached to Lid_Hinge. Warm stratified stone extends across body and lid.
- Preserve mechanism source pivots, 80-degree latch release first, then 105-degree lid opening. Sample 81 poses against every static/moving mesh and latch/lid pair, only explicitly named bearing contacts exempted.
- Poster from actual Blender source. Standalone embedded viewer <5,000,000 bytes; no runtime network; desktop and emulated mobile file:// controls, reset, reduced motion, real parser-corruption fallback, no-JS fallback.
- Independent saved-source reopening, hollow floor ray, attachment intersections, source/export nearest-vertex parity <1e-5, actual-loader zero nonfinite/degenerate triangles; explicit theme geometry/map checks and inventory SHA-256/bytes. Negative control must demonstrate theme test rejects missing landmark.

Technical passing does not establish photorealism, geological accuracy, engineering certification or physical-phone performance. No chain operations, site changes, deployment or merge. Human per-asset review waived for this batch; visual limitations recorded honestly.
