# Saint Lucia / Pitons & Caribbean — pre-model visual contract

## Research and interpretation
Primary design reference is this written brief, not a supplied illustration. UNESCO's [Pitons Management Area](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1161/) identifies two adjacent forest-clad volcanic spires above the sea, linked by the Piton Mitan ridge, within the Soufriere Volcanic Centre. Its [IUCN evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/151917) describes degraded dacitic lava-dome cores and contrasting broad Gros Piton / narrower Petit Piton. Sources retrieved through web search before authoring. No source photos or text are embedded in maps. This is a stylized landscape tribute, not measured terrain, a geological sample, or a traditional craft replica.

## Frozen measurable design
- Wide family keepsake silhouette: exterior width 3.72, depth 2.38 (up to 2.48 at body), wall top 1.56; rounded lid centered z=1.828, thickness .30. Five-sided hollow interior; four dark stone feet.
- Body: weathered volcanic gray-green stone, target sRGB approximately #56635d with fine light/dark mineral speckle; roughness .65–.9. Continuous real walls rather than wood finger courses. Generated 256px stone color/roughness and forest color maps; no scans.
- Narrow dark jade seam centered z=1.618, height <=.108, rear narrowed to avoid hinge leaves. Satin warm brass family latch, side ring, medallion and small wrapped corner caps. Hardware is invented collection language, not Saint Lucian historical hardware.
- Lid: deep Caribbean teal polished facing (linear RGB .015,.15,.18), with a low irregular green coastal landform. Two independently named asymmetrical, fluted forest-clad peaks: broad peak centered (-.62,.27), height .80–1.00 above the lid; narrower peak centered (.58,.35), height .70–.95. Both rise from a connected low ridge, leaving visible teal water along the front. Neither peak is a smooth cone. Landform fits within x +/-1.5 and y +/- .95. All terrain/peaks are lid-owned and travel together.
- Forest greens target #385e32 through #79925a, with occasional muted exposed rock facets. Colors are artistic, not calibrated site measurements. No palm trees, flag stamps, sacred motifs or imported glacier.
- Coordinate convention: front -Y, rear hinges +Y, head-on right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Preserve exact inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot transforms: staged 80 degree latch release over first quarter then 105 degree lid lift. Collision sample all moving geometry vs static and latch vs lid at 81 poses, exclude only explicit mating bearings. Independently compare pivots to mechanism source.
