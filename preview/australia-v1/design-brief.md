## Measurable visual contract, written before modeling
- Wide family silhouette: exterior width 3.72, depth 2.38; body z .10–1.56, lid center 1.828 and thickness .30. Continuous five-sided hollow liner inherited from the trimmed Japan mechanism.
- Body: warm ochre sandstone with irregular orange/dark horizontal surface bands, subtle granular normal and roughness maps. Target generated sRGB orange about (175,91,43), charcoal about (57,53,45); orange remains dominant. No wood joints, cedar, moss, glacier, national flag or pasted photo.
- Three compact 512×512 procedural stone maps: color, roughness, normal. Project by dominant face normal with shared world-space coordinates so side faces do not stretch into wood-like grain.
- Lid: same banded sandstone slab, restrained gold corner caps, dark narrow mineral reveal at z 1.618; rear strip narrowed to .08 depth to preserve hinge clearance. Four low stone feet.
- Five separately modeled asymmetrical beehive domes on the lid, from z 1.97 to no higher than 2.90. Cluster offset toward rear (+Y), maximum footprint x ±1.50, y -.65 to .90; tallest rear-central dome. Each has rounded narrowing top, irregular radial fluting and horizontally registered orange/charcoal bands. Gaps separate the towers. No photographic or exact geographic claim.
- Brass family latch/medallion, side ring, seated rear hinge leaves. All lid ornament and caps parented to Lid_Hinge. Front is -Y, rear +Y, right +X. Same inherited Lid_Hinge/Latch_Pivot matrices; release latch 80° first, then lid 105°.
- Source and GLB actual geometry parity under 1e-5 units; finite vertices and zero degenerate triangles. 81 collision samples across every moving/static part and latch/lid, exempt only named original mating bearings. Same-group dome gaps checked separately.
- Standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, actual-source-render poster, embedded runtime/GLB/maps, zero external requests. Desktop and 390×844 mobile touch emulation over offline file://: rotation, open/close, reset, reduced motion, corrupt GLB poster and no-JS poster.
- Technical tests can fail and include deliberate negative controls. Visual review of real model screenshots is separate from technical acceptance. No physical-phone performance or photoreal-equivalence claim. At most two focused corrections before final honest limitations.
