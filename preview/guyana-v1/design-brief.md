# Guyana v1 — Kaieteur / Rainforest & Sandstone

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced brief, not an existing concept illustration. A stylized landscape miniature, not a geographic replica, traditional craft reproduction, or exhaustive representation of Guyana.

## Research
- Guyana Tourism Authority, https://guyanatourism.com/wheretogo/mountainsandrainforests/kaieteurnational-park : coffee-coloured Potaro River, 741-foot Kaieteur drop and surrounding rainforest. This informs amber water upstream, pale falling water and deep greens.
- Guyana Tourism Authority, https://guyanatourism.com/kaieteur-falls : brown escarpments and a curtain of water amid rainforest.
- https://tour.gy/destinations/kaieteur-national-park : sandstone plateau and rainforest. Used for material interpretation only; no ranking/superlative inscription.
Research informs original procedural geometry/maps; no third-party pixels embedded.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake body approximately 3.72 x 2.38 units, inherited mechanism envelope; brown layered sandstone body (target sRGB brown #79513b, ochre #ad8258), continuous hollow liner and restrained brass family fittings.
- Dark forest-green satin lid, narrow seam below 0.12 units; gold latch and connected rear hinges. Front is Blender -Y, back +Y, right +X, up +Z.
- A low irregular sandstone plateau on lid, footprint at most 2.8 x 1.65, rises 0.45–0.85 above lid. A clearly visible pale vertical waterfall at least 0.35 units tall connects an amber upper river to a lower pool, wholly inside the lid footprint. No cascade dangling into body sweep. Place exposed waterfall toward front-left, plateau toward back-right; avoid a generic mountain cone.
- Clustered deep-green canopy relief flanks water, at least 8 irregular canopy forms. Keep river/curtain visibly exposed from hero/mobile camera. Green targets #244e35 and #416c3f; water tan/amber above and ivory falling curtain. Geological strata portable procedural color/normal/roughness maps; not wood grain.
- All terrain, water, canopy and fittings travel with lid. Preserve inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot matrices and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Include every moving mesh in 81-pose collision sampling; only explicit inherited mating bearings exempt.
- Editable packed Blender source, GLB, generated maps, actual-source render poster, embedded offline viewer strictly <5,000,000 bytes. Drag, open/close, reset, reduced motion and genuine fallback.
