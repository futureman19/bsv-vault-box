# Lithuania / Curonian Dune & Lagoon

## Research and primary design reference
This written contract is the primary reference, frozen before authoring; no supplied concept image exists for this asset.
UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/994 describes the Curonian Spit as a sandy and wooded cultural landscape, shaped by wind, sea and human stabilization/reforestation. It is shared by Lithuania and the Russian Federation, not exclusively Lithuanian.
The IUCN evaluation https://whc.unesco.org/document/154788 describes the linear dune formations, lagoon margins and pine/spruce woodland mosaic. These sources inform a Lithuanian coastal interpretation, not a geographic scale model. No research images or text are embedded as artwork.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake body: 3.72 X by approximately 2.48 Y; exterior z .10–1.56. Warm pale sand body with fine irregular wind-ripple maps, muted dark lagoon-green seam under .12 high; satin brass inherited latch and hinges.
- Lid 3.72 by 2.38 by .30, centred z 1.828; thin blue-green lagoon inset covering 3.16 by 1.80, at z 1.99. Palette: sand tan/cream, lagoon teal, pine green, warm brass. Not amber gemstone or wood grain.
- A continuous curved sandy ridge traverses lid X from -1.40 to +1.40, width .38–.65; relief top z 2.04–2.48, with smooth asymmetric dune crests. Its closed perimeter extends to the inset, never a floating surface sheet. Lagoon remains exposed on both sides. A small coherent dark-green vegetation band on one ridge shoulder suggests stabilization, not a botanical survey.
- All inset/dune/vegetation geometry is owned by Lid_Hinge. Front -Y, hinge +Y, right +X; glTF maps (X,Z,-Y).
- Reuse original mechanism pivots and 80-degree latch then 105-degree lid choreography. Five-sided hollow interior, attached hinges; collision sample all moving meshes at 81 poses, only explicit inherited mating bearings exempt.
- Procedural compact maps (512px maximum); real editable .blend and GLB. Source-render poster, never AI promotional imagery.
- Offline standalone HTML <5,000,000 bytes, embedded assets, desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// controls, rotation/reset, reduced motion, actual corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback, no network or overflow.
- Independent source/GLB parity tolerance 1e-5; finite and nondegenerate GLTFLoader geometry; ridge bounds/parentage, map variation and source-render nonempty probes. Exact-byte SHA-256 inventory and archive readback.

## Limits
Invented landscape arrangement and family hardware, not a historic Lithuanian chest, exact coast map or traditional craft reproduction. Sampled collision is not engineering certification. Mobile emulation is not physical-phone performance. No blockchain or deployment work. Autonomous technical acceptance per task; visual judgement remains available for full-collection review.
