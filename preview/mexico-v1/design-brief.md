# Mexico / Talavera & Agave — v1 visual contract

Written before authoring. Primary reference is this brief, not an existing approved illustration. This is a stylized combination of two distinct regional traditions, not a claim that all Mexico has one visual identity.

## Research
- UNESCO, Artisanal talavera of Puebla and Tlaxcala (Mexico) and ceramics of Talavera de la Reina and El Puente del Arzobispo (Spain) making process: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/01462 . The entry describes clay preparation, enameling, decoration and firing, workshop-specific identities, and domestic/decorative/architectural uses. Inscribed tradition is shared with Spain. Our original cobalt/ivory floral repeat is an invented design, not a copied workshop motif or certified Talavera product.
- UNESCO, Agave Landscape and Ancient Industrial Facilities of Tequila: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1209/ . Blue agave fields and their cultivated landscape in Jalisco inform a compact sculptural rosette. This is not a botanical survey, a scale replica, or an endorsement of alcohol.
Research retrieved during this run. No photographs, sacred motifs, logos or third-party textures are embedded.

## Measurable contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body 3.72 X by 2.48 Y, exterior from z .10 to 1.56. Lid 3.72 by 2.38 by .30 centered z 1.828. Hollow inherited five-sided interior.
- Ivory glazed ceramic body with original cobalt floral repeat, separated into 8 front/rear by 3 courses and 5 side by 3 courses. Fine dark grout gaps, real beveled tile boundaries. 512-square color map and variable roughness map; ivory target #e6ddc4 and cobalt #173b75 with restrained ochre accents. Texture is independently drawn, not scanned.
- Terracotta lid slab and four squat volcanic-dark feet. Inset cobalt/ivory patterned ceramic top panel; satin brass corner plates and family latch. Narrow dark blue seam at z 1.618, max height .108, rear narrowed to avoid hinge sweep.
- Raised terracotta oval soil island centered (0.25,0.22) with radius about .85 X / .63 Y; attached at z 1.98. One blue-green agave rosette of 24 individually modeled tapered, ridged leaves rooted at (0.25,0.22,2.10). Extents stay inside lid; top below z 3.15. Original sculptural interpretation, not botanical precision. All soil and leaves parent to Lid_Hinge.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X; glTF maps (X,Z,-Y). Original hinge/latch pivot matrices preserved. 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Sample 81 poses of every moving mesh against every static mesh and latch against lid; exclude only named concentric bearings.
- Standalone file:// HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes; model, runtime, generated maps and source-rendered poster embedded. Drag/touch rotate, open/close/reset, reduced-motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster fallbacks.
