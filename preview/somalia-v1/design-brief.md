# Somalia / Merka Dunes & Indian Ocean

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced written contract, not an approved illustration. A stylized landscape collectible, not a survey or a claim that one landscape represents every Somali community.

## Research
- Carbone & Accordi (2000), The Indian Ocean Coast of Somalia, Nairobi Convention: https://nairobiconvention.org/clearinghouse/node/399 describes skeletal sands, coral build-ups, eolian sands and paleosols along the southern coast, and the ancient Merka red dune ridge complex separating the coastal belt from the Shabeelle alluvial plain.
- CNR publication record: https://iris.cnr.it/handle/20.500.14243/241160 independently hosts the abstract of the same paper.
- Library of Congress country study, Terrain, Vegetation and Drainage: https://countrystudies.us/somalia/35.htm describes coastal dunes along the Indian Ocean from Mereeg toward Chisimayu, with scattered scrub and grass.
No research photographs or text are used as textures. No exact coastline, artifact, religious motif or traditional craft reproduction is claimed.
