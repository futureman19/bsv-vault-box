# Syria v1 — Damascus / Walnut & Pearl

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced written contract, not a supplied marketing image.

Research: World Crafts Council Asia-Pacific Encyclopedia, Jean Bachoura, https://encyclocraftsapr.com/damascus-mother-of-pearl-inlay describes Damascus walnut furniture decorated with mother-of-pearl, silver thread and geometric or plant motifs. Enab Baladi, https://english.enabbaladi.net/archives/2022/02/syrian-mosaic-suffers-state-neglect-despite-public-interest describes ornate wooden boxes and the use of walnut, lemon and olive woods. Sources consulted for craft context only; no photographs or patterns copied.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide walnut box: 3.72 wide, about 2.48 deep, body z=.10–1.56; lid 3.72×2.38×.30 centered z=1.828. Hollow inherited liner.
- Dark warm walnut procedural grain: base RGB around (96,53,30), fine variable roughness. Three compact maps, maximum dimension 512. Dark brown seam, height .108, rear narrowed to avoid hinge.
- Original invented geometric pearl composition: three eight-point rosettes across lid at x=-1,0,1, y=0. Each consists of eight separate cream lozenges, with warm wood centers. Two rows of small pearl diamonds form lid borders; body front has two lower horizontal diamond bands clear of the clasp. These are shallow applied inlay-like geometry, not authentic joinery or a reproduction of a historical pattern.
- Cream pearl accents and restrained satin brass family fittings. Pearl is opaque satin approximating nacre, not spectral iridescence. No flags, inscriptions, sacred symbols or war imagery.
- All lid decoration parented to Lid_Hinge; upper ornament z <=2.08. Front -Y, rear +Y. Retain exact mechanism pivots, 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid swing. Every moving ornament included in 81 sampled collision poses.
- Small dark stone feet. No cedar tree, moss or landscape terrain.
- Actual saved-source poster, full offline embedded HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes; desktop/mobile file:// interaction, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback checks. Source/export vertex tolerance 1e-5. Nonfinite/degenerate geometry fails. Explicit ornament counts/parentage and map variation checks.

Interpretation: Syria has multiple living craft traditions; this is one Damascus-inspired digital study, not artisan-made furniture, authentic nacre, a cultural survey or photoreal equivalence. No minting, network deployment or blockchain operations.
