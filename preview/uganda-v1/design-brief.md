# Uganda v1 — Mutuba / Beaten Barkcloth

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this written contract, not an invented previously approved image.

## Research
UNESCO, Barkcloth making in Uganda: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/barkcloth-making-in-uganda-00139 describes the Baganda craft in southern Uganda, inner bark of Mutuba (Ficus natalensis), repeated wooden-mallet beating and an even terracotta color. It explicitly distinguishes the process from weaving. Research consulted during this build; no source photography/textures copied.

This design celebrates one specific Ugandan tradition, not every Ugandan culture. It is a procedural material interpretation, not artisan-made barkcloth, ritual dress, royal insignia or an ethnographic reproduction. The folded cloth display is invented product ornament.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake body: approximately 3.72 X × 2.48 Y; outer skins z .10–1.56. Continuous hollow liner retained. Front -Y; rear hinge +Y; glTF X,Z,-Y.
- Terracotta nonwoven fibrous body panels and lid, base-color target around sRGB #A85D39 with irregular low-contrast mottling and short broken fibers; no woven checker or long wood-growth rings. Compact 512px procedural color/roughness maps; dark warm interior and dark stone feet.
- Continuous front/rear and side skins instead of Japan finger courses. Restrained satin brass corner caps, existing brass clasp, medallion and side ring. Hardware is family extrapolation, not claimed Ugandan traditional metalwork.
- Dark brown reveal seam under .12 high, retained hinge clearance. Lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 centered z1.828.
- Lid ornament: a single folded, rippled barkcloth sheet, x ±1.30, y ±.72, top z2.04–2.18; closed solid skirt down to lid top z1.978. Three shallow folds run along X; warm terracotta face and darker edge. No floating cloth. Ornament parent Lid_Hinge; entire ornament travels with lid.
- Same inherited pivot positions; staged 80° latch release then 105° lid opening. All moving/static surfaces and latch/lid surfaces sampled in 81 poses; only explicit mating bearings excluded.
- Actual source render poster. Offline embedded HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes. Desktop and 390×844 touch-emulated file:// open/close, rotation, reset, reduced motion, failure/no-JS poster and zero network requests.
- Saved-source reopening, source/export vertex parity <1e-5, finite nondegenerate GLTFLoader mesh, map variation, independent cloth bounds/parent/normal/attachment checks, exact byte/hash inventory and ZIP readback. Negative control must genuinely fail.

## Honesty boundary
A stylized study of beaten barkcloth, not photoreal cloth simulation or proof of traditional manufacturing. BVH samples are not continuous collision certification. Browser mobile is emulation, not physical-phone performance. Local collectible artwork only; no chain, wallet, deployment, mint or custody claims.
