Written before geometry authoring. Primary reference is this research-backed written contract, not an existing illustration. This is an invented landscape miniature, not a geographic or botanical replica or a traditional craft claim.

## Research
- UNESCO World Heritage, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/159 : Darien includes sandy beaches, rocky shores, mangroves, wetlands, rivers and lowland/upland tropical forests.
- UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, https://unesco.org/en/mab/darien?hub=200805 : coastal mangroves, freshwater marshes, palm-forest swamps and moist tropical forests bridge the continents.
These sources were retrieved before authoring. Only ecological ideas are used; no photographs or third-party texture pixels. Jade is an artistic green mineral palette, NOT a claim of Panamanian jade deposits. No indigenous motifs or sacred symbols are appropriated.
