# Austria / Adnet & Alpine Crown

Written before model generation. The primary design reference is this research-backed contract, not an existing approved illustration. A procedurally authored, stylized collectible, not a stone scan or geographically surveyed miniature.

## Research
- IUGS Heritage Stone, https://iugs-geoheritage.org/geoheritage_stones/adnet-marble : Adnet near Salzburg supplies mostly reddish decorative limestone, including red breccias with white cement rims. Commercially called marble, it is limestone, not metamorphic marble. Its historical architectural and sculptural use inspires the polished box shell.
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/bf00260440 : the Adneter Scheck breccia consists of red limestone clasts and white sparry calcite, historically used as decorative stone.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highest_mountain_in_Austria : Grossglockner belongs to the Hohe Tauern and has a prominent double summit. The lid will carry an invented multi-peak Alpine crown, NOT a scale replica or a claim that Adnet stone is the summit's bedrock.
No research photographs or text are embedded in the collectible maps. All maps are generated from seeded math.
