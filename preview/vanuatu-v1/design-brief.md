# Vanuatu / Yasur — Ash & Ember

Written before authoring. Primary reference is this research-led contract, not a supplied or previously approved illustration.

## Research and interpretation
- Vanuatu Tourism Office, https://vanuatu.travel/en/things-to-do/volcanos — Yasur on Tanna has a broad crater with visible lava; Vanuatu's volcanic landscapes include ash plains. Retrieved for this brief.
- Vanuatu Tourism Office, https://vanuatu.travel/au/plan/where-to-go/tanna/guide-to-tanna — Tanna's black volcanic sand, forest and Mt Yasur inform the dark mineral shell and restrained warm crater interior.
- VTO Tanna guide, https://vanuatu.travel/images/documents/VTO0064_Brochure_Tanna%20Guide_A4_v14.pdf — ash plain and crater-rim experience support a broad ash cone rather than a tall alpine peak.

This is an invented volcanic keepsake, not a geographic reconstruction, eruption simulation, traditional craft replica or claim to represent every ni-Vanuatu community. No sacred motifs, names of communities, flags or decorative pseudo-writing. All pixels and geometry are procedural; no third-party photographs/textures embedded.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Wide rectangular shell: 3.72 X by approximately 2.48 Y; body skins z .10–1.56. Hollow inherited liner and four dark volcanic feet. Four broad monolithic ash-stone walls rather than cedar finger joints. Fine pitting/noise, charcoal/brown ash target sRGB around #393736 to #625b53; no glacier, tree, temple or ocean motif.
- Satin dark volcanic lid: 3.72 by 2.38 by .30, centered z 1.828. Narrow oxidized dark rust seam z 1.618 height .108, with rear strip narrowed to keep hinge clearance. Front is -Y, rear +Y, physical right +X. glTF coordinates (X,Z,-Y).
- Low broad asymmetrical crater relief on lid: center approximately (0,.12), outer radius 1.30 X by .83 Y, basal z 1.985, irregular summit rim about z 2.55–2.70. A real recessed bowl, not a solid cone or flat decal: interior descends to z 2.13 with a dark orange ember basin radius about .30 by .22. Entire relief and basin parented to Lid_Hinge. Warm interior target #b34c16–#e58232, restrained constant warm emission, not a claim of actual lava lighting. Bowl visibly open from elevated normal camera.
- Antique satin brass inherited latch, medallion, ring and small corner fittings. Four compact feet, continuous liner, attached rear hinge bridges and leaves. Same inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot transforms. Staged 80° latch release then 105° lid opening, reverse close order.
- Procedural portable maps max 512 px, color/roughness/normal ash textures. No remote runtime assets. Editable .blend and exported GLB, actual-source poster.
- Single-file offline viewer strictly below 5,000,000 bytes; drag/touch orbit, open/close/reset, reduced-motion and real parse-error/no-JS poster fallback.
