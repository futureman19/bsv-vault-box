# Ecuador / Toquilla & Volcanic Isles

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this research-backed written contract, not an existing approved illustration. A deliberately invented collectible combining mainland Ecuadorian fibre craft with its Pacific volcanic landscape; not an authentic traditional chest or a geographic replica.

## Sources
- UNESCO, Traditional weaving of the Ecuadorian toquilla straw hat: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-weaving-of-the-ecuadorian-toquilla-straw-hat-00729 . Describes coastal Ecuador fibre cultivation, processing, crown/brim weaving and intergenerational craft transmission. This supports fine pale woven surfaces, not a claim that this box is a traditional hat or indigenous motif.
- Galápagos Conservancy, History of Galápagos: https://www.galapagos.org/about_galapagos/history/ . Describes volcanic island formation and major island volcanoes. This supports a low volcanic-island lid miniature, not surveyed island outlines.
Research text and photographs are not embedded as textures. All maps and geometry are independently procedural.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide family keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, height approximately 1.9; retain proven hollow liner, rear hinges, latch and pivots from Japan mechanism. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Warm pale straw woven body and lid perimeter: cream/sand base with interlacing diagonal over-under cues, not cedar grain. Compact portable base-color, normal and varying roughness maps (maximum 1024 per side). Palette target straw RGB approximately (193,164,111) to (228,207,162); recesses darker. Decorative weave is an invented approximation, not a copied named traditional pattern.
- Dark graphite lid inset with low teal Pacific water area and three irregular charcoal volcanic islets; one prominent broad shallow caldera, two smaller irregular islets. No snow, cedar, temple or copied glacier. Caldera must have a real depressed center and raised rim, terrain relief 0.15–0.55 above lid. Three island footprints fit inside lid, avoid brass fixtures, and all belong to Lid_Hinge. No literal geographic scale claimed.
- Restrained antique-gold hardware, inherited proven hinge attachment; dark narrow seam less than 0.12 high. No flags, sacred symbols, factual inscriptions or wildlife figurines.
- 80-degree latch release first, then 105-degree lid opening, unchanged mechanical pivots. All moving meshes including islands are collision sampled against static surfaces and latch against lid. Only individually named mating bearing contacts may be excluded. At least 81 samples; sampled surface evidence, not continuous physics certification.
- Actual source-render poster; offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes including runtime, GLB, maps and poster. No external requests. Source blend and maps outside viewing payload also supplied.
