# Albania / Stone City — brief frozen before modeling

## Research and primary reference
This written contract is the primary design reference; there is no supplied concept image and no claim of prior visual approval.
- Albanian National Tourism Agency, https://akt.gov.al/en/gjirokastra : Gjirokastra's Ottoman-style tower houses use stone roofs, stone walls (sometimes whitewashed), and wooden balconies. The city's hillside architecture is the inspiration.
- University of Florence / ISPRS architectural survey, https://flore.unifi.it/retrieve/e398c380-35f2-179a-e053-3705fe0a4cff/isprs-archives-XLIV-M-1-2020-33-2020.pdf : local limestone masonry, grey stone roof slabs, timber upper structures, and sloping urban context.
No research photographs or text are used as textures. The three-house miniature is invented, not a survey or replica of a named monument; it represents one regional tradition, not all Albania.
