# Solomon Islands / Ebony & Shell — frozen visual contract

Written before modeling. This is an original brief-driven collectible, not a reproduction of an approved illustration or a traditional sacred object.

## Research
- WWF Coral Triangle, **Talented carvers of Marovo Lagoon, Solomon Islands**: https://coraltriangle.blogs.panda.org/coral-triangle/talented-carvers-of-marovo-lagoon-solomon-islands — describes king ebony, streaked queen ebony and other carving woods, and nautilus-shell pieces fitted into carved grooves. Search-retrieved text; direct extraction unavailable in this environment.
- Metropolitan Museum, **Nguzunguzu (canoe prow ornament)**, object 1976.351: https://metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/309868 — identifies contrasting luminous shell inlay and stained black wood in western Solomon Islands art, while explaining the ritual/ancestral context of figureheads.
- National Museums Scotland, **Material Culture of the Solomon Islands**: https://files.nms.ac.uk/production/Documents/Our-Impact/Collections-reviews/Pacific-collections/Pacific%20Region/27-material-culture-of-the-solomon-islands.pdf?dm=1736177620 — describes pearl shell inlay as a distinctive western Solomon Islands technique.

These sources support a material contrast, not a claim that one object represents every island/community. Do not reproduce figureheads, faces, ritual emblems or museum imagery. All textures and simple geometric shell motifs are original procedural inventions; no harvested wood/shell, scans or copied photographs.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height approximately 1.9; preserve inherited hollow liner and mechanically attached brass fittings.
- Body and lid: dark warm ebony-like wood with subtle brown lengthwise grain. Color map predominantly near-black brown (sRGB approximately #211711 to #493021); satin rather than mirror finish. Use compact <=512px generated color/roughness maps, not unportable shader-only effects.
- Main identity: cream shell-inspired segmented geometric inlays on the front and lid. At least 20 distinct visible inlay pieces total. Invented staggered diamonds/lozenges, not claimed traditional pattern. Shell target ivory #e9e1c8 with restrained warm/cool shifts.
- Lid ornament: a low sculptural asymmetric shell-inlay medallion / curved band, no tree, glacier, temple, or sacred figure. Raised relief <=0.18 above lid. Every lid decoration is parented under Lid_Hinge.
- Hardware: satin antique brass latch and connected rear hinges; retain family pivot positions, 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. Front = -Y, rear = +Y, right = +X in Blender. Fine dark seam, no inherited moss or stone terrain.
- Preserve contact seating, hollow interior, and sample all moving-vs-static and latch-vs-lid meshes over 81 poses. Only explicit bearing exclusions allowed; disclose sampled not continuous proof.
- Readable contrasting inlay at desktop and 390x844 mobile emulation. Actual-model source render poster, no AI substitute. Visual QA separate from tests; no photoreal-equivalence claim.
- Standalone HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes with all runtime assets embedded, offline file:// interaction, reduced-motion, genuine corruption fallback and no-JS poster.
