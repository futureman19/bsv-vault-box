# Egypt v1 — Alabaster & Pyramid Fields

Written before modeling. Original brief-driven design, not a reconstruction of a supplied illustration. This represents one material/landscape thread, not all of modern Egypt.

## Research
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Egyptian Stone Vessels: Khian through Tuthmosis IV: https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/egyptian-stone-vessels-khian-through-tuthmosis-iv documents Egyptian stone vessels and travertine (Egyptian alabaster).
- UNESCO, Memphis and its Necropolis: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/86 describes the pyramid fields, including the three pyramids of Giza and limestone Step Pyramid of Djoser.
These support warm banded calcite/travertine-inspired stone and a miniature pyramid-field landscape. No museum photos or archaeological ornament copied; no sacred symbols or invented hieroglyphs. Not an accurate scale reconstruction, historical chest replica, scan or archaeological claim.

## Pre-model measurable contract
- Inherit wide keepsake proportions approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body from z≈0.3 to 2.2; hollow continuous five-sided liner. Front -Y, rear hinge +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Warm ivory/honey alabaster-inspired body: procedural portable color and roughness maps, cream #ddcba5 and honey #a77b42 target palette, irregular narrow mineral bands rather than cedar grain or Morocco salmon. Opaque satin approximation, not physical translucency. Map dimensions at most 1024.
- Shallow lid in matching stone with dark navy recessed accent, narrow dark seam under 0.12 units, brass family corner fittings/latch/medallion. No copied tree, glacier or moss. Keep base pivots and connected hinge seats.
- Three raised four-sided limestone-inspired pyramids on a low irregular sand-colored plateau on lid. Footprints of descending sizes roughly 0.9, 0.65, 0.4 units; tallest 0.65–0.85 units. Entire landscape parented to Lid_Hinge; confined within lid footprint and away from rear mechanical sweep. Subtle geometric course detailing is allowed; do not make tall temple towers.
- Gold/brass hardware, restrained blue accent, warm cream stone dominate; no text inscriptions pretending historical authenticity. Source render is the only poster.
- Unchanged staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Independently reopen saved source; sample 81 poses across entire motion, all moving/static meshes and latch/lid pairs, narrowly enumerated mating-bearing exceptions only. No ornament collision blanket exemptions.
- Source/export nearest world vertex error <0.00001 units; actual GLTFLoader finite vertices/bounds and no degenerate triangles. Verify three pyramids, portable map variation, source dimensions and moving ancestry.
- Offline self-contained HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, complete GLB/JS/poster embedded. Desktop and 390x844 touch-emulated mobile file://: open/close, rotate, interrupted reset, reduced motion, parser-error/no-JS fallback, no external requests, no overflow; full-open silhouette fits reset camera.
- Evidence JSONs must derive from actual measurements and fail on violated assertions. Preserve any real failures and fixes. SHA-256/byte inventory and delivery check; no claims of photorealism, exact historical equivalence, continuous collision proof or physical-phone performance.
