# Malta — Honey Limestone & Harbour

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced written contract; no supplied concept illustration or photographic texture.

## Research
- IUGS Globigerina Limestone: https://iugs-geoheritage.org/geoheritage_stones/globigerina-limestone — identifies Maltese stone as pale yellow to cream, used for building/sculpture and fortifications. Inspiration for porous honey-cream masonry, not scanned stone.
- UNESCO Valletta conservation record: https://whc.unesco.org/en/soc/1638 — describes fortified town and globigerina limestone as its basic building material, weathered by maritime humidity. Informs softly worn block edges.
- NASA Earth Observatory, Malta's Landscape of Limestone: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/maltas-landscape-of-limestone-150742 — describes Valletta peninsula between two harbours. Factual inspiration only; no NASA pixels used.

## Frozen visual contract
Wide family keepsake box approximately 3.72 X 2.38, body height approximately 1.9 units. Latch front -Y; hinge rear +Y; up +Z. Warm pale honey limestone body with visible offset ashlar joints and fine irregular pores; cream/honey palette approximately #d8ba78 to #ead6a5 with restrained darker joints. Not wood grain, dunes or volcanic basalt. Compact generated maps at <=1024 maximum dimension. Continuous hollow liner and floor retained.

Lid: warm stone frame around a deep blue/teal harbour-inspired recessed inlay. A low irregular limestone peninsula relief with angular bastion outline occupies the centre, leaving blue water readable on two sides. Stylized invented harbour plan, NOT an exact Valletta map or scale heritage reconstruction. At least three distinct angular bastion projections, overall relief height <=0.55 above lid; no tower skyline or religious emblem. Blue target #246b79, warm brass #b78b43. Fine ashlar lines should read on body at normal desktop size; large water/stone separation should read on mobile.

Satin brass family latch, corner fittings, feet and connected rear hinge assemblies. Narrow dark teal seam <0.12 height. Preserve existing 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening, original pivots. All inlay and peninsula geometry belongs to lid. Source render poster must show actual final geometry.
