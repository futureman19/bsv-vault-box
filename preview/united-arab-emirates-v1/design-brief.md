# United Arab Emirates — Desert Loom

## Research and authorship
Primary reference is this written contract, frozen before modeling; no supplied concept image. UNESCO's UAE-specific Al Sadu entry (https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/02223) documents traditional weaving, its women knowledge-bearers, safeguarding and intergenerational transmission. This supports a textile-inspired collectible, not a claim of artisan manufacture. The geometric stripes/diamonds below are invented, not a copied named tribal motif. No source photos or UNESCO media enter the asset.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide box, 3.72 units across, roughly 2.48 deep; body from .10 to 1.56; lid centered 1.828 with .30 thickness. Genuine five-sided hollow liner.
- Deep madder-red textile-inspired body and lid with ivory, charcoal and warm red stripes; compact 512-square procedural woven color and roughness maps. Dark brown end accents and dark seam <= .108 high.
- Lid carries a raised rectangular woven panel (2.70 x 1.60) and seven ivory diamond outlines centered along X, each .30 wide x .62 deep; brass border rails. This is a rigid decorative textile relief, not simulated cloth.
- Brass family latch, medallion, side ring and small corner fittings; four dark stone feet. No flag, writing, sacred emblem or skyline miniature.
- All raised panel geometry belongs to Lid_Hinge; back +Y, front clasp -Y. Preserve baseline pivot matrices, staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening.
- Independently reopen saved source; check seven diamonds (28 segments), parentage and bounds. Sample all moving/static meshes and latch/lid at 81 poses, exempting only named mating bearings. Check hollow floor and hinge attachment.
- Actual GLTFLoader finite/degenerate checks, source/export tolerance 1e-5; offline desktop/mobile file:// native controls, reduced motion, fallback, no external requests. HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes. Byte inventory and archive readback.

## Limits
Stylized script-authored craft homage; not woven by an Emirati artisan, not historical reconstruction, not photoreal equivalence. Mobile testing is emulation. Art only; no chain operation, deployment or custody claim.
