# Moldova v1 — Woven Garden

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this research-backed contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research and interpretation
UNESCO's Traditional wall-carpet craftsmanship in Romania and the Republic of Moldova (2016 inscription) describes woven decorative/insulating carpets, diverse motifs, identity, and contemporary artistic use: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-wall-carpet-craftsmanship-in-romania-and-the-republic-of-moldova-01167 . Research retrieved for this task. This is a shared tradition, not exclusive to Moldova. No source photographs or historic patterns are copied. The geometric garden motif below is a contemporary invented interpretation, not an authenticated regional weave or sacred emblem.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette about 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height about 1.9; hollow five-sided interior. Front -Y, rear hinge +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Warm dark walnut-toned structural rim and corners; deep charcoal/burgundy woven panels on four outer walls. Generated compact color/normal/roughness maps with visible small cross-thread grain and an ivory/ochre stepped-diamond border. Main palette: charcoal #292622, burgundy #702b39, ivory #d4bd8f, muted green #64724b, antique brass #aa793e. Palette is artistic, not a historic dye claim.
- Lid: inset rectangular woven garden panel enclosed by walnut rim. Three raised geometric eight-petal floral medallions along X, with burgundy petals, ivory accents and green stepped leaves. Each flower diameter 0.55–0.85 units; relief at most 0.12 above lid. All relief belongs to Lid_Hinge. No inherited cedar, glacier, Moroccan terrain or other country imagery.
- Fine dark seam under 0.10; family antique brass hinges, front latch and side ring. Keep original mechanical pivot matrices and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Include all moving ornaments in 81-pose collision tests; exclude only named mating bearings.
- Real editable Blender source and embedded-map GLB; source-rendered poster, no AI replacement. Offline embedded HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes.

## Acceptance gates (capable of failing)
Independent saved-source hollow/pivots/attachment checks and 81 sampled motion poses; source/export vertex parity within 1e-5; actual GLTFLoader finite and nondegenerate geometry; procedural map variation and three floral medallions with full lid ancestry; desktop and 390x844 touch-emulated mobile file:// open/close/drag/reset, reduced motion, corrupt-input and no-JS posters, zero external requests and overflow; exact bytes/SHA-256 inventory and archive readback. Retain failures and fixes. Review source poster and actual browser screenshots for motif visibility. These are stylized craft-inspired art checks, not geographic completeness, photorealism, physical-phone performance or manufacturing certification.
