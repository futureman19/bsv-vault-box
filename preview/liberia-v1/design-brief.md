# Liberia — Sapo Rainforest & River

Written before modeling. Landscape-inspired miniature, not a park map or a representation of all Liberian cultures. No third-party images/textures, sacred motifs or botanical accuracy claims.

## Research
- Liberia Forestry Development Authority, Sapo assessment: https://www.fda.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/sapoimet.pdf describes permanently humid tropical lowland rainforest, swampy flatlands and rugged Putu Hills.
- Pygmy Hippo Foundation: https://www.pygmyhippofoundation.org/about/sapo-national-park/ describes Sapo's Upper Guinean forest ecosystem and lowland swamp/rainforest habitat.
These inform a dark timber box carrying broadleaf canopy islands and a winding river. Timber is an artistic material, not a claim of harvested protected wood.

## Measurable contract
- Wide 3.72 x 2.38 lid; body height approximately 1.9 including lid. Dark warm brown procedural wood grain (map target RGB 112/66/36), green seam below .12 height, satin brass inherited family latch/hinges.
- Hollow continuous five-sided liner. Same rear X-axis hinge at y=1.32 z=1.668; original latch pivot. Staged 80-degree latch followed by 105-degree lid. No on-chain action.
- Green inset lid terrain with three asymmetric broadleaf tree groups, branching trunks, flattened irregular green crowns (not conifer needles); top under z=2.9. River ribbon snakes front-to-back through canopy islands; teal material, no claim of simulated fluid.
- All terrain, river and foliage parented to lid. Every moving mesh included in 81-pose collision tests, only explicitly named mating bearings exempt.
- Compact portable procedural wood/color/roughness/stone maps. Actual source render poster only. Entire embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes.
- Independent source reopening, source/export vertex parity <1e-5, GLTFLoader finite/triangle checks, offline file:// desktop/mobile interaction/reduced motion/corrupt-input fallback/no network; exact byte/hash inventory.

Technical passes do not establish photorealism, continuous collision safety, real-phone performance, minting or storage guarantees. At most two bounded visual corrections; autonomous v1 delivery per task instruction.
