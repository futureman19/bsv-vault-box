## Research and interpretation
UNESCO, Traditional craftsmanship of the Mongol Ger and its associated customs (2013), https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-craftsmanship-of-the-mongol-ger-and-its-associated-customs-00872 describes round wooden frames, peaked roofs, white felt/canvas covers and tightening ropes, plus communal felt making, stitching and painted woodwork. Retrieved via web search; direct extraction backend was unavailable. This supports the material/craft theme, not an exact architectural reconstruction. No research photos are copied. The rectangular felt-clad box, ochre-red trim, blue seam and stylized green steppe are original design extrapolations; no sacred or purported traditional emblem.
