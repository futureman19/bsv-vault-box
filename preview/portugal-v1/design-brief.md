# Portugal — Azulejo / Atlantic Blue
Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this contract, not an existing illustration.

## Research
- American Ceramic Society: https://ceramics.org/ceramic-tech-today/azulejos-a-portuguese-tradition-influenced-by-globalization — Portugal's blue-white architectural tiles and their multicultural influences.
- Portugal Visitor: https://portugalvisitor.com/portugal-culture/azulejos-tiles — glazed decorative architectural tiles in Portugal.
These support the material tradition, not the authenticity of our invented floral repeat or sculptural waves. No third-party pixels are embedded.

## Frozen visual contract
Wide keepsake silhouette: body 3.72 x 2.48, skins z=.10–1.56; lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centered z=1.828. Hollow inherited liner. Ivory ceramic (#eee8d7 target) with cobalt (#194780 target) original four-petal rosettes and curved corner flourishes, narrow cream grout. 512px portable color, normal and roughness maps. Real panel skin geometry rather than painted wood. Satin brass corner caps, medallion, ring and inherited articulated hasp. Dark cobalt seam width under .12; rear strip narrowed for hinge clearance.
Three distinct raised wave ribbons run along X at y=-.58,0,.58, width span 2.7, height .08–.24 above lid. Glazed cobalt/ivory alternate, rooted on lid. This is decorative Atlantic imagery, not a geographic sea reconstruction. Four ceramic feet.
Front -Y, rear +Y; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Retain original Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot matrices, 80-degree release then 105-degree opening. Every wave travels with lid. All moving surfaces sampled at 81 poses against fixed surfaces and latch versus lid; only explicit mating bearings exempt.

Actual source render and browser captures inspected separately from numeric checks. Stylized procedural work; no claim of photorealism, historic-pattern fidelity, physical-phone performance or continuous collision proof.
