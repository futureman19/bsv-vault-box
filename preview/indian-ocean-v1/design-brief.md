# Indian Ocean / Atoll & Abyss

Frozen before modeling. This is an invented ocean-landscape collectible, not a geographic replica, traditional craft attribution or harvested-coral product.

## Research
UNESCO's Aldabra description https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/185 identifies an Indian Ocean raised coral atoll: four coral-limestone islands enclosing a shallow lagoon, separated by narrow passes and surrounded by a reef. Its IUCN evaluation https://whc.unesco.org/archive/advisory_body_evaluation/185.pdf describes weathered limestone holes/pits and rugged relief. Retrieved via web search; direct extraction unavailable. These facts inform the ivory broken reef ring and turquoise lagoon against deep ocean blue. No source imagery is copied; no biological or bathymetric accuracy is claimed.

- Keep family wide box dimensions near 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height near 1.9; hollow continuous liner and gold/brass fittings. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: deep midnight/navy and petrol-blue procedural mineral/water-inspired marbling, restrained fine pale current lines. Target sRGB palette #123647, #20566A, #378B94, not cedar grain or basalt/glacier. Compact generated base-color, normal and roughness maps.
- Lid: satin deep-teal ocean platform; thin turquoise seam <0.12 tall. Centered stylized low irregular elliptical reef, footprint approximately 2.6 by 1.45, above lid by 0.12–0.35. Four separate ivory coral-limestone arc islands with visible narrow water passes; no mountain, tree or glacier. Open center contains lower bright turquoise lagoon; retain unobstructed center view at normal/mobile framing. Ivory roughly #D5C9A6; lagoon #30ADB1. Reef relief is geometry, not an image plane. No literal coral harvesting implied.
- A deeper ocean border surrounds the reef. Ring roughness and island heights vary asymmetrically. Small weathered rock protrusions permitted within reef footprint; all lid ornament inherits Lid_Hinge. No sacred imagery or invented inscriptions.
- Preserve proven rear hinges, latch pivots and family brass hardware. Latch release exactly 80 degrees first, then lid opens 105 degrees. Sample all moving meshes against all static meshes and latch against lid at 81 poses; only explicit existing mating bearings exempt. Same-pivot baseline comparison required.
- Standalone offline HTML below 5,000,000 bytes with embedded actual GLB, runtime and source-rendered poster; native controls, drag, reset, reduced motion and real corrupt-input/no-JS fallback.
