# Trinidad and Tobago / Scarlet Rhythm

## Research and interpretation — frozen before modeling
- Ministry of Foreign and CARICOM Affairs, National Instrument: https://foreign.gov.tt/about/trinidad-tobago/national-symbols/national-instrument — steelpan developed locally in the 1930s/40s from steel drums/containers; playing surface divided into tuned convex sections.
- National Musical Instrument Act 2024, Act 13: https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/a2024-13g.pdf — legally designates steelpan; describes a concave playing surface divided into convex note sections.
These factual sources inform an invented steelpan-inspired collectible, not a playable instrument, authentic tuning layout, historical replica, or comprehensive representation of both islands. No source photographs or text are incorporated into maps. No supplied primary artwork exists: this written contract is the reference.
