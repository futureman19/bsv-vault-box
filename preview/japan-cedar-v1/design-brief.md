# Japan / Cedar & Moss — one review pilot

Primary: original japan.png (hash in preserved-inputs.json), not a newly invented temple or Iceland reskin. Procedurally authored geometry and generated portable maps; no scans or claim of hand sculpture. No mint/deployment.

## Evidence and interpretation
- UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/662 identifies Yakushima's ancient sugi, Cryptomeria japonica, and humid montane forest.
- IUCN evaluation https://whc.unesco.org/archive/advisory_body_evaluation/662.pdf identifies predominant granite bedrock.
- Japan National Tourism Organization https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/517/ describes Shiratani Unsuikyo's ancient cedars and moss-covered landscape.
These inform material/ecology only; this miniature is not a botanical survey or a scale model of a named tree. Research text/images are not copied into textures. Original concept remains separate.

## Measurable visual contract (written before model)
- Wide warm red-brown body, width 3.72, depth 2.38, wood height approximately 1.9. Fine horizontal grain on front/back, along depth on sides, along X on lid. Portable 512px authored wood atlas, darker exposed finger ends. No 2K maps.
- Four real alternating corner finger joints per corner; no painted-on joint illusion. Rounded satin lid, not obsidian. Four irregular granite feet. Hollow five-sided liner.
- Narrow muted moss-green seam (under 0.12 height), interrupted at corner wood and latch; no bright continuous lawn.
- Front latch is -Y; rear hinges +Y; head-on right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Reference tree base estimated 65–70% across and 85–90% from front. Target trunk (0.62,0.78), granite footprint approx 0.9 by 0.65; tree height approx 0.85 above terrain. Rear-right is a plan-view decision, not screen-right.
- Small bent cedar trunk, visible branching and fine irregular needle sprays; avoid conical pine primitives. Granite/moss terrain must sit on lid and entire tree hierarchy travel with lid.
- Refined brass latch/medallion and left ring. Small corner fittings, not heavy Iceland wraps. Unexplained front hole omitted intentionally (no imaginary function). Rear hinges/interior are extrapolations.
- Keep base staged 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening, same pivots. Collision sampling includes every new moving ornament and all static/hardware surfaces; only named mating bearings exempt.
- Normal-size desktop/mobile closed/open evidence; same orientation and equal-width reference comparison without anisotropic image scaling. Up to two focused correction passes, then honest review limits.
- Standalone offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including real GLB/runtime/poster; editable source and reproducible scripts separate.
