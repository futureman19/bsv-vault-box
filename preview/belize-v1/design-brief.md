# Belize / Blue Hole & Reef — frozen pre-model contract

## Research and interpretation
UNESCO's Belize Barrier Reef Reserve System description (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/764, retrieved for this task) identifies offshore atolls, the Blue Hole, white sand cayes and green mangrove cayes contrasting with azure water. Its component map (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/764/maps) identifies Blue Hole Natural Monument. These support a reef/karst landscape interpretation, not a scale geographic reconstruction or a claim that a motif represents all Belizean cultures. No research imagery is copied. All maps and geometry are authored procedurally.

## Visual contract written before authoring
- Theme: Blue Hole & Reef. Wide keepsake silhouette approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height approximately 1.9, inherited hollow liner and family hardware.
- Pale warm carbonate/reef-stone body (sand/ivory #c6b48c–#eee0b8), subtle irregular pores and mineral speckles, NOT cedar grain. Compact portable generated color/normal/roughness maps, at most 512px per axis.
- Thin turquoise mineral seam under 0.12 high; satin deep-teal lid with a shallow raised reef platform. Brass latch, medallion, corner fittings and attached rear hinges retained from proven mechanism.
- Lid centerpiece: large visibly circular navy Blue Hole depression/inset, radius 0.45–0.65 units, centered slightly left of middle, surrounded by an irregular turquoise shoal and pale broken reef ring. Circle is an artistic symbol, not a bathymetric survey. A darker floor sits below the raised rim: real relief, not merely a painted disk.
- Secondary low white-sand caye on right with restrained green mangrove-inspired clusters; all relief remains within lid footprint and no more than 0.65 above lid. All terrain must parent to Lid_Hinge and move with it.
- Front is -Y, hinge rear +Y, right +X. Preserve existing pivot matrices, 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. No alterations to shared sources.
- Real source render poster, no AI promotional substitute. Normal desktop/mobile visuals should show the navy circle, pale reef and turquoise surface clearly.
