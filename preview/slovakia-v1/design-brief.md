# Slovakia v1 — Čičmany / Lime & Timber

Frozen before modeling. This brief is the primary design reference; no supplied concept image exists. Procedurally authored miniature, not a replica of a particular protected house or a claim to represent all Slovak culture.

## Research
Slovakia's official tourism portal, https://slovakia.travel/en/cicmany (retrieved during this task), describes dark log houses with white geometric ornament, traditionally applied first to corners with mud and later lime, extending across facades since the nineteenth century. Geometric motifs also appear in local embroidery. https://slovakia.travel/en/fotogenickym-miestam-na-stope/2-cast independently describes the white geometric patterns on dark wood and architectural reserve. These factual material/craft associations inform original geometry and maps; no third-party photographs, patterns or text are embedded as artwork.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body width approximately 3.72, depth 2.38 and wood height approximately 1.9 units, inherited proven hollow liner and mechanical dimensions. Front is Blender -Y, hinges +Y, right +X; glTF conversion (X,Z,-Y).
- Dark umber timber body and satin lid: target base RGB around #38251d to #654333 with visible grain. Warm lime-white #e5dfc9 original geometric chevron/diamond/short-stroke decoration in at least two rows on front and side faces. Motifs are invented contemporary abstractions inspired by the white-on-dark contrast, not exact documented traditional patterns.
- Narrow dark seam under 0.12 units. Retain gold/brass family latch, ring and corner details, rear hinge seating and hollow interior.
- Lid ornament: a low timber-inspired geometric relief panel with raised lime-white diamond/chevron bands. At least five distinct geometric repeats, relief approximately 0.025–0.09 units above lid. The design is craft-led, not a transplanted Iceland glacier or Japan tree. Every relief part is owned by Lid_Hinge and travels with it.
- Preserve inherited pivots exactly: latch releases 80 degrees in first quarter, followed by lid opening 105 degrees. Sample at least 81 poses of all moving versus static parts and latch versus lid; only inherited explicitly named mating bearings exempt.
- Portable generated base-color/roughness/normal maps, no scans; compact maps <=1024 per dimension. Complete offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes, runtime/GLB/actual source-render poster embedded.
