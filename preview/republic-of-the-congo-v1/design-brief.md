# Republic of the Congo — Odzala Bai / Forest & Wetland

Design contract frozen before modeling. Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville), NOT Democratic Republic of the Congo. Landscape interpretation, not traditional craft attribution or geographic scale model.

## Research / provenance
- UNESCO, Forest Massif of Odzala-Kokoua, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/692/ : forest recolonization of savanna ecosystems and large-scale forest landscape.
- Harvard Davies Lab, Odzala-Kokoua National Park, Republic of Congo, https://davieslab.oeb.harvard.edu/odzala-kokoua-national-park-republic-congo : dense lowland tropical rainforest, forest islands, and nutrient-rich hydrologically associated clearings called bais.
Search-retrieved source text informs the concept; no third-party image pixels or textures are copied. These ecological sources do not establish a specific wood species, indigenous craft tradition, or accurate aerial map. The body and hardware are artistic extrapolations.

## Measurable visual contract
- Family wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth 2.38, overall body/lid approximately 2.1 high; preserve inherited mechanical dimensions and hollow liner.
- Body: dark umber forest-floor/wood-inspired finish with fine irregular vertical organic grain; portable generated maps <=512px each. Color family dark brown (#423125), dark forest green (#244b36), wetland ochre (#a48c4e), restrained teal-brown water (#396b62). Not a harvested rare-timber claim.
- Lid: low relief wetland diorama with a clearly open irregular central bai, shallow reflective water inset, surrounding asymmetric green broadleaf canopy clusters and visible brown trunks. Canopy must occupy the outer/back parts rather than filling the clearing. No conical cedar tree or Iceland glacier. Relief <=0.65 above lid, clearing >=0.65 wide, >=3 distinct canopy groups. All attached to Lid_Hinge.
- Gold/brass family hardware, corner fittings, front hasp/medallion and rear connected hinges. Narrow dark green seam <=0.10 high; preserve clearances. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Same base pivots and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Sample >=81 poses, every moving mesh against static and latch against lid, exempt only named mating bearings. Hollow floor ray and source/export <=1e-5 world-vertex parity.
- Offline embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes including runtime, GLB, maps and actual Blender-render poster. Desktop and emulated-touch mobile file://: open/close, rotation, reset, reduced motion, real corrupt-GLB fallback, no JS fallback, no external requests/errors/overflow.
- Tests assert feature geometry, hierarchy, finite/nondegenerate triangles, decoded map variation, exact byte counts and SHA-256 inventory. Capture actual desktop/mobile closed/open views. Technical pass is not photorealism, physical-phone performance, continuous collision proof, or user visual approval.
