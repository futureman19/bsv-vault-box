# Bangladesh v1 — Jamdani & Tidal Delta

Design contract written before authoring. This is a new original composition, not a reconstruction of an existing marketing image. Only this new folder is writable. No chain, wallet, mint, site, server or shared-kit work.

## Research and interpretation
- UNESCO, Traditional art of Jamdani weaving: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-art-of-jamdani-weaving-00879 — describes vividly patterned sheer cotton cloth woven on handlooms by craftspeople and apprentices around Dhaka; the craft transmits motifs and weaving knowledge. This informs the fine woven ground and spaced contrasting ornamental repeats, not a claim that a rigid box is authentic Jamdani fabric.
- UNESCO, The Sundarbans (Bangladesh): https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/798 — describes the Ganges/Brahmaputra/Meghna delta, tidal waterways, mudflats, small islands and salt-tolerant mangroves. This informs a low-relief branching waterway and irregular green islands, not a geographic map or a botanical species reconstruction.
Both sources were retrieved during this task. No third-party photographs or UNESCO imagery are embedded. Motifs, maps and geometry are independently procedural. This composition combines two inspirations; it does not represent every Bangladeshi cultural tradition. No sacred symbols, flags, factual inscriptions or harvested rare timber claims.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide inherited keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep, body height approximately 1.9; use unchanged existing mechanism pivots and hollow five-sided liner. Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: deep teal/indigo textile-inspired lacquer ground (target sRGB roughly #164D50), warm ivory (#E6D5AD) spaced original stepped diamond/floral weaving-inspired motifs and subtle two-axis thread texture. No cedar, glacier, sandstone strata or generic flag wrap. Materials are a fictional textile-inlay finish on a rigid box, not a literal load-bearing fabric container.
- Satin dark teal lid frame, warm brass fitted corner accents/hinges/latch and restrained ivory ornamental border. Hardware inherits the tested family language. Narrow muted dark seam under 0.12 high; maintain rear hinge clearance.
- Lid centerpiece: a shallow miniature tidal delta in an inset field, with at least three visibly separate irregular green land islands and one connected branching blue-green water network. Relief about 0.06–0.25 above lid; tiny stylized vegetation is optional, but no tall conifer or mountain. Keep all geometry within lid footprint and parent all islands, water and trim under Lid_Hinge. Low tidal relief, not a detached landscape plinth.
- Preserve staged 80-degree latch release, then 105-degree lid opening and reverse closure, with unchanged pivots. Every moving mesh participates in 81 or more sampled collision poses versus static geometry, and latch versus non-latch lid geometry. Only explicit original bearing pairs can be exempt; do not exempt decorative terrain.
- Maps compact (prefer 512px or smaller, up to 1024px if budget permits), portable embedded PBR base/roughness/normal maps as applicable. Real geometric relief for lid landforms; weave motifs may be procedural maps.
- Actual-model source-render poster, clean desktop and mobile offline viewer. Open/close, rotation, reset, interrupted reset, reduced-motion, no-JS and actual corrupted-GLB fallback must work. Full open silhouette must fit at reset/default view. Entire HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, including runtime/GLB/poster.
