# Dominican Republic — Larimar / Caribbean Blue

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced visual contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research and interpretation
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larimar describes larimar as blue/blue-green pectolite associated with Barahona, Dominican Republic. Retrieved search evidence identifies the Bahoruco area and the material's regional association.
- https://www.dominican-larimar.com/index.php/en/the-story-of-larimar/ describes the Barahona origin. Commercial jewelry source, not independent mineral certification.
These support the material connection only. No third-party photographs or textures are copied. This is a procedural stone-inspired sculpture, not real mined stone, an authentic traditional craft pattern, or a geographic reconstruction.
