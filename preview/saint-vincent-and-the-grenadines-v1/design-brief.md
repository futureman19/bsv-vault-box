# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines — Soufrière & Cays

Written before modeling. Original brief-driven miniature, no supplied reference artwork. Geography inspires materials and forms, not a scale map or a traditional craft attribution.

## Research
- Government Ministry of Tourism, Tobago Cays: https://tourism.gov.vc/tourism/index.php/national-sites-of-svg/tobago-cays describes five islets, Horseshoe Reef, white sand and blue water. Accessed for factual inspiration; no photography/textures copied.
- Government Ministry of Tourism, La Soufriere: https://tourism.gov.vc/tourism/index.php/national-sites-of-svg/la-soufriere describes steep volcanic ridges and tropical vegetation. Its last-eruption statement is outdated and is NOT used here.
- Government national sites: https://tourism.gov.vc/tourism/index.php/national-sites-of-svg describes black-sand shores and the volcanic origin of Owia coastal formations.

## Frozen visual contract
- Family-wide keepsake proportions: approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep, body about 1.9 high. Genuine hollow shell and fitted lid. Front is Blender -Y, hinge +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: dark charcoal volcanic stone, generated fine mottling and subtle porous normal variation, not wood stripes. Color targets charcoal #343d3c with restrained ash gray flecks; compact <=512px procedural maps. Gold/brass hardware inherited for family coherence, never coral harvested from nature.
- Lid: dark rim enclosing a turquoise sea field (#288c99), five distinct low irregular sand-edged green cay reliefs toward front/right and one taller irregular crater/ridge toward rear/left. Geography is intentionally compressed into an invented composite; volcano is not located in the Tobago Cays. Five cays distinguish the reef cluster from a generic island.
- Raised volcanic crown: approximate x=-0.65, y=0.30, footprint <=1.25 × 1.05, height 0.35–0.65 above lid; visible crater depression >=0.10 deep. Forest-green flanks #345c3e, ash-charcoal crown. Cays each 0.20–0.45 across with pale sand perimeter, max height 0.16 above water. Sea and every miniature part belong to Lid_Hinge and travel with it.
- Narrow dark mineral-green seam <=0.10 high with safe rear hinge clearance. Retain connected gold hinge leaves/barrels, latch and feet, no floating disconnected hardware.
- Preserve inherited exact pivots: staged latch 80 degrees, then lid 105 degrees. Independently sample 81 poses across the full sequence, all moving meshes included; exempt only named intentional bearing contacts.
- Offline HTML includes actual GLB, bundled runtime and source-render poster, strictly <5,000,000 bytes. Drag, open/close, reset and reduced-motion; desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// checks, no network requests, corrupt-GLB and no-JS fallback.
- Source reopen, source/export parity within 1e-5 world units, finite nondegenerate exported triangles, decoded texture variation, five cays and crater depth/ancestry assertions, exact byte/hash inventory and ZIP readback. Include deliberate negative control to demonstrate theme gate rejects a detached ornament.
