# Netherlands — Delft & Polder

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this research-backed visual contract, not an approved illustration. Original procedural motifs, not reproductions of a historic ceramic or a geographical survey.

## Sources
- Dutch Delftware museum resource: https://delftsaardewerk.nl/en/discover/what-is-delftware — historic Delftware is tin-glazed earthenware, often blue and white, with opaque white glaze over yellow-firing clay. Our ceramic-inspired finish makes no authentic manufacture claim.
- UNESCO: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/899/ — Beemster was drained in 1612; its rational geometric landscape comprises fields, roads, canals, dykes and settlements. This informs an abstract gridded miniature, not an exact Beemster model.
- Royal Delft Museum: https://museum.royaldelft.com/en — documents the continuing Delft Blue pottery craft. No logo, maker mark, museum photograph or copied pattern is used.
Research retrieved through search excerpts; direct extraction backend was unavailable. No source imagery is embedded.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake box approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y; body height about 1.9. Front -Y, rear +Y, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body and lid rim: warm ivory ceramic (#e7e5d6 target) with cobalt (#174780 target) invented floral/scroll medallion repeats and fine blue border lines. Pattern must visibly occupy broad body faces, not merely edge piping. Compact procedural color and roughness maps <=1024 each; ceramic roughness 0.22–0.45 with nonconstant portable texture.
- Brushed warm brass existing family latch, side ring and rear connected hinges. Retain inherited hollow five-sided liner, same lid/latch pivots and staged 80-degree release then 105-degree lid opening.
- Narrow dark-blue seam <=0.10 height, trimmed around bearing contacts. No moss, cedar branches, glaciers or inherited country ornaments.
- Lid miniature: low rectangular polder patch, approximately 2.7 by 1.55, with at least six distinct raised muted-green/ochre field parcels separated by two perpendicular visible blue watercourses, plus a surrounding dyke outline. Relief <=0.28 above lid except one small invented mill.
- One restrained dark timber windmill with four distinguishable static sails, toward rear/right (+X,+Y); total height <=1.1 above lid. Sails are decorative, not independently animated. Base physically rests on the lid landscape. Every landscape and mill part belongs to Lid_Hinge and follows it.
- Keep sculptural landmarks subordinate to the broad ceramic box silhouette. Real geometry for fields, canals, dyke and mill; no poster planes masquerading as model.
