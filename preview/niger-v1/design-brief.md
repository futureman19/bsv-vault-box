# Niger v1 — Aïr & Ténéré

## Research and interpretation (before modeling)
UNESCO, Air and Ténéré Natural Reserves, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/573 identifies the volcanic rock mass of Aïr within the Saharan desert of Ténéré in Niger. Retrieved through web search for this brief. This contrast of dark rock and warm desert drives the design, not a claim to reproduce a surveyed location or represent all Nigerien cultures. No source photos or sacred/cultural symbols are copied. All maps and geometry are independently procedural.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body about 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height about 1.9; inherited hollow liner and exact established mechanism pivots. Front is -Y, rear +Y, up +Z.
- Warm ochre/sand body (target sRGB midtones #B77B3C to #D9AC68), fine irregular wind-swept texture, no cedar grain, greenery or glacier. Dark umber volcanic feet and a narrow dark seam <=0.12 high.
- Lid miniature: asymmetric low golden dune crests occupying most of its area, with a compact dark angular Aïr-inspired rocky outcrop near rear-right. Relief maximum 0.65 above lid; not a flat printed landscape. Both dunes and rocks are actual geometry and children of Lid_Hinge. Avoid tall landmarks or copied Morocco ridge.
- Restrained satin brass family latch/corners and physically attached rear hinges. Brass is a collection-design extrapolation, not a claim of local historic craft. No flags, inscriptions or religious symbols.
- Preserve 80-degree staged latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening and reverse close, drag/touch rotation, reset and reduced motion.
- Portable procedural base-color/roughness/normal maps <=512 per dimension unless justified by measured byte budget. Keep meaningful terrain silhouette over redundant detail.

## Gates fixed before implementation
1. Reopen saved Blender source, verify hollow floor and exact pivots; all moving meshes including terrain participate in 81-pose sampled surface collision checks with only explicit mating-bearing exclusions.
2. Independently compare saved source and exported geometry at <=1e-5 world vertex tolerance. Actual GLTFLoader reports no nonfinite vertices or degenerate triangles.
3. Assert palette/texture variation, dune and rock geometry, lid ancestry and relief bounds; test a deliberately invalid input to show acceptance can fail.
4. Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile: native open/close, drag, interrupted reset, reduced motion, no external requests/overflow/unhandled errors; real corrupt GLB and no-JS poster fallbacks.
5. Standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes; embedded runtime/GLB/poster rendered from actual source. SHA-256 and exact-byte inventory. Source, maps, scripts, checks and honest README in this directory only.
6. Inspect actual closed/open desktop/mobile renders separately from numerical gates. Stylized procedural collectible, not photoreal equivalence or physical-phone performance. No human per-asset review required for this collection batch.
