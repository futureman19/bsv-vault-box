# Iraq v1 — Babylon / Cobalt & Ochre

Written before authoring. A contemporary collectible inspired by Babylon's glazed brick craft, not an archaeological reconstruction or a representation of all Iraqi cultures.

## Research
- UNESCO Babylon: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/278/ — ancient city in present-day Iraq, architectural heritage context.
- World Monuments Fund, Heritage from Home: Ishtar Gate, Babylon: https://www.wmf.org/videos/heritage-home-ishtar-gate-babylon — describes once cobalt-blue bricks and yellow-glazed relief animals. Search retrieved this wording during design research.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishtar_Gate — supplemental design account describes blue glazed bricks and black/white/gold borders and rosettes. Inspiration only; no third-party imagery or text incorporated into maps.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide family keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth approximately 2.48, exterior z .10–1.56; lid 3.72 by 2.38 by .30 centered z 1.828. Retain hollow liner and seated inherited hardware.
- Main identity: cobalt glazed brick courses with warm ochre inset borders. Portable generated color/roughness maps, maximum 512 square per map. Target cobalt sRGB roughly #17468b with distinct darker mortar and irregular blue glaze, ochre #c69542. Subtle surface mottling rather than stone strata or wood grain.
- Genuine shallow geometric brick courses, offset seams, restrained warm brass family latch and corner fittings. Dark narrow seam beneath lid; no green moss or Japanese tree.
- Lid: shallow stepped architectural relief in warm fired-clay/ochre on cobalt field, three decreasing rectangular tiers (maximum height .50 above lid), plus compact geometric rosette accents. Stylized abstract Babylon architecture, not a measured ziggurat reconstruction; no sacred figures or invented inscriptions. Every lid ornament belongs to Lid_Hinge.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Preserve Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot positions from trimmed Japan mechanism, 80-degree latch release in first quarter followed by 105-degree lid opening. Retain physical attachments and sample all moving meshes, including ornament.
- Geometry bevel widths below half minimum dimension. Four low feet. No floating hinge or exposed shell holes.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source reopen, source/export parity <1e-5 units, genuine hollow floor ray, explicit bearing-only contact exclusions and 81 motion samples with no unintended surface intersections. Actual GLTFLoader finite geometry and nondegenerate triangles. Theme checks assert cobalt map variation, tiers, lid ancestry, dimensions and absence of inherited forest motifs. Preserve real failure evidence if encountered.
Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile: open/close, drag, interrupted reset, reduced motion, actual corrupted-GLB fallback, no-JS poster, no external requests/errors/overflow. Actual source-render poster, no AI substitute. Standalone embedded HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes. Hash/byte inventory and additive-only scoped git check. Mobile emulation does not establish phone performance; sampled surface collision is not continuous certification; procedural miniature is not photorealism.
