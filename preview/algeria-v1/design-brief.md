# Algeria v1 — Tassili Rock Forest

## Research and scope (written before modeling)
Primary factual reference: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Tassili n'Ajjer, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/179/ (retrieved via web search). UNESCO describes south-east Algeria's plateau, eroded sandstone 'rock forests', and sedimentary sandstone successions. Gallery context: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/179/gallery/ . This design interprets the geological landscape, not a specific monument or the site's culturally significant rock art. No ancient drawings, religious symbols, flags, photographs or third-party textures are copied. One landscape cannot represent all Algerian cultures.

## Measurable visual contract
- Theme: Tassili Rock Forest. Wide ochre sandstone keepsake box with wind-eroded vertical channels and an asymmetric miniature sandstone rock forest attached to its lid. Distinct from Morocco's fine colored strata and Japan's wood/tree.
- Body approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep, top near z=2.0; retain the proven mechanism/interior dimensions. Wide silhouette, not a cube. Continuous hollow five-sided interior, four feet.
- Procedural stone color target ochre (#b77d43), amber/sand highlights (#dbb77c), restrained umber recesses (#62462e). Palette values are artistic targets, not geologically measured samples. Compact generated color/roughness maps (512px baseline), no external textures or scans.
- Body features real shallow irregular vertical erosion channels/ribs around a continuous inner shell. No copied cedar finger joints, moss, glacier or ceramic motif. Narrow dark umber seam (height <=0.10), clear of rear hinge travel.
- Lid: satin dark sandstone slab with low irregular sandy apron and 5–9 separately legible, uneven eroded sandstone towers, height 0.25–0.80 above lid. Towers have nonuniform ring profiles, weathered caps and varied heights, not regular cones or temple spires. Whole forest remains within lid plan footprint (approximately |x|<1.65, |y|<0.95). Highest forms offset to rear/right, lower foreground for normal-view visibility.
- Retain restrained warm brass family latch/medallion, rear hinges and small corner caps. Hardware and interior are artistic extrapolations, not a claim about Algerian chest-making traditions.
- Coordinates: front = Blender -Y, rear = +Y, head-on right = +X, up = +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Preserve existing named pivots and staged 80° latch release then 105° lid opening. All apron/towers parented to moving lid; collision-sample every moving mesh against static meshes and latch versus lid with only explicit mating bearings excluded.
- Standalone offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes including runtime, real GLB, textures and actual-source poster. Native rotate/open/close/reset, interrupted reset, reduced motion and real parser-failure poster.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source reopen, hollow floor ray, source/export vertex parity <0.00001 units, actual GLTFLoader finite vertices/no degenerate triangles, expected texture variation and lid ancestry, at least 81 motion samples including every ornament. Offline file:// desktop and mobile touch emulation: native open/close/rotate/reset/reduced motion, no network requests/runtime errors/overflow, framed open silhouette, corrupt-input and no-JS fallback. Theme assertions count towers and enforce spatial/palette/material limits. Exact SHA-256 and byte inventory with explicit required files. Real failures must be preserved, not waived. Actual source/browser images inspected separately from technical checks. Stylized landscape interpretation, not photorealism, continuous collision proof or physical-phone performance. Per-asset human review is waived for this batch; no publication, blockchain operations or integration authorized.
