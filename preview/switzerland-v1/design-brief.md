Frozen before modeling. Original brief-led procedural collectible; no supplied concept image or geographic accuracy claim.

## Research
- Switzerland Tourism, Matterhorn: https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-us/destinations/nature/mountains/mountains-4000-peak-search/-/zermatt/ identifies the 4,478 m peak and characteristic pyramid silhouette (retrieved search excerpt).
- Switzerland Tourism, Matterhorn Lodge: https://myswitzerland.com/en-ch/accommodations/hotel-matterhorn-lodge describes natural stone and old wood integrated into alpine-style building in Zermatt (retrieved search excerpt).
These support an Alpine wood-and-stone interpretation, not a claim walnut is unique to Switzerland. Walnut finish, gold fittings and snow distribution are invented design choices. No third-party imagery is embedded.

- Wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, body height approximately 1.9; retain inherited hollow liner and seated hinges.
- Dark warm walnut-brown body with visible fine irregular grain, compact procedural base-color, normal and roughness maps (at most 1024 per side). Target brown RGB family approximately #68432c; gold/brass hardware, not a silver industrial case.
- Thin dark green-grey seam no more than 0.12 high; satin wood lid with a stone relief inset.
- Lid bears an asymmetric four-sided horn-like mountain miniature, grey faceted rock with broken pale snow upper faces. Peak offset toward rear/right (+X,+Y), height 0.65–1.15 above lid, footprint contained on lid; not a smooth cone, flag or copied Iceland glacier. Lower foothills at its base; all terrain travels with lid.
- Front is -Y, rear +Y, up +Z. Keep exact inherited rear lid and front latch pivots, staged 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree opening. Decorative hardware may be inherited; mechanical geometry is not redesigned.
- Gold family latch, ring and corner hardware; continuous hollow shell and real rear hinge attachments.
- Acceptance: reopen saved blend; portable GLB parse/finite/degenerate checks and source parity; quantify mountain bounds, snow/rock materials and lid ancestry; sample 81 motion poses including every moving ornament versus static and latch/lid. Exempt only explicit inherited mating bearings.
- Real source-render poster; offline embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file://: rotate, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster fallback, no external requests/errors/overflow. Exact byte/SHA-256 inventory.
- Technical success is not photorealism, accurate mountain survey, physical-phone performance or continuous mechanical certification. No chain interaction, deployment or minting. Bound art correction to two passes.
