## Research and interpretation
- UNESCO tentative listing, https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5317/ describes Qatar's southeast Khor Al-Adaid: mobile barchan/parabolic dunes adjoining a tidal embayment, sabkha and limestone outcrops. This is a tentative listing, not a claim of World Heritage inscription.
- Qatar Tourism, https://visitqatar.com/intl-en/things-to-do/beach-holiday/khor-al-adaid-beach describes a lagoon encircled by sand dunes.
Retrieved search excerpts support these factual inspirations; direct UNESCO retrieval returned HTTP 403. No third-party photos, maps or textures are copied. Geometry is a stylized invented composition, not a geographical survey, craft-authentic object or geological material sample.
