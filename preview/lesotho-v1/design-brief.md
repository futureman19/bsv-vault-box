# Lesotho v1 — Maloti Basalt & Woven Indigo

Brief frozen before modeling. A new landscape-led BoxSV collectible, not a copy of Japan or a scale reconstruction of a named summit. No supplied primary artwork exists for this country: this written contract is the design reference. All geometry and maps are procedural original work; no source photographs, textile designs, national insignia or sacred motifs are copied.

## Research and interpretation
- Lesotho Tourism Development Corporation, **About Lesotho**, https://www.visitlesotho.org.ls/about-lesotho : describes lowland sandstone hills, highland basalt cliffs, and the cool blue appearance of distant mountains. Retrieved through web search; direct HTTP retrieval failed DNS in this worker environment.
- Government of Lesotho, **Tourism**, https://www.gov.ls/about-lesotho/tourism/ : describes the Maluti mountain plateau, valleys, rivers and volcanic soils; identifies the mokorotlo as a national symbol. Retrieved through web search. The hat is deliberately not used as a generic conical ornament.
- LTDC, **Travel & Investor Guide**, https://www.visitlesotho.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/LesothoTravelInvestorGuide-compressed.pdf : describes mountain scenery and Basotho blankets worn as cloaks with cultural significance. Search-indexed text, not a complete PDF review.

Landscape supplies the main identity: a weathered blue-charcoal basalt body and an asymmetric miniature Maloti ridge on its lid. A restrained indigo/ivory/rust woven panel refers broadly to textile craft; it is an invented geometric stripe pattern, NOT a reproduction of any named Basotho blanket, brand or ceremonial design. This stylized interpretation cannot represent all Basotho culture.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve the family wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth about 2.38, lid top about z=1.98; no tall cube. Front = Blender -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X. glTF maps (x,z,-y).
- Continuous hollow five-sided inner shell, blue-charcoal basalt exterior with irregular relief/panel courses rather than cedar joints. Exterior approximate sRGB targets charcoal #35444c, muted blue #506372, warm mineral #8a8171; generated compact varied stone color/roughness maps (each <=512 pixels per side).
- Four low stone feet; satin aged-brass hardware, dark interior. Retain the proven lid and latch parent/pivot mechanics. Gold is subordinate framing, not a giant ornamental cage.
- Narrow dark seam, height <=0.09. Preserve real negative clearance at rear hinges rather than covering bearings with the seam.
- Lid: inset woven indigo panel with restrained ivory/rust stripes, generated weave map <=512 pixels per side. Terrain is actual geometry, not a picture. At least three unequal rounded/angular basalt peaks, continuous connected ridge footprint across rear-middle lid; footprint stays inside x +/-1.45 and y +/-0.90, peak height 0.35–0.70 above lid. Subdued olive highland grass patches accent sheltered ridge bases; no glacier, cedar tree, waterfall or borrowed Moroccan tilework.
- Ridge, panel, grass and all lid fittings belong to Lid_Hinge and travel with it. Keep all ornaments away from the front latch sweep; whole silhouette must fit desktop/mobile closed and fully open reset views.
- Mechanism: latch releases 80 degrees during first quarter; lid then opens 105 degrees around inherited rear pivot. Preserve reverse-close, interrupted reset, drag/touch rotation and reduced-motion snapping.
- Sample 81 poses using evaluated saved-source mesh BVHs: every moving mesh vs static, plus latch vs lid; exempt only named intentional bearings. Report sampled surface intersection evidence, not continuous or manufacturing certification.
- Offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, containing actual GLB, bundled runtime and source-render poster; no network downloads. Deliver .blend, .glb, maps, generator, viewer/build/test scripts and evidence separately.

## Acceptance gates
1. Independently reopen packed source: expected structure, hollow-floor ray, exact inherited pivots, lid ornament ancestry, dimensions, 81 collision samples without unintended intersections.
2. Source/export parity in world coordinates <=1e-5 units; actual GLTFLoader finite geometry and no degenerate triangles; decode maps and confirm real pixel variation.
3. Theme checks enforce named geometry/material, peak count/height/footprint, woven panel and texture bounds, ancestry and seam thickness. Real negative control must fail rather than trusting hard-coded PASS flags.
4. Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile: actual Open/Close, drag, interrupted Reset, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB poster and no-JS poster; no external requests, runtime errors or horizontal overflow. Save normal-size closed/open screenshots.
5. Build byte count, SHA-256 inventory and delivery readback. Additive-only under this directory. No blockchain, keys, nodes, production site, VPS, deployment or external messaging.

Visual review is autonomous for v1 per task direction. Passing gates is not photoreal equivalence, physical-phone performance, authenticity/custody proof or user aesthetic approval.
