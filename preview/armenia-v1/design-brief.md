# Armenia v1 / Rose Tuff Arcade

Frozen before modeling. Primary design reference is this written contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research and interpretation
- Smithsonian, **How Ancient Volcanoes Created Armenia's Pink City**: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/travel/yerevan-armenias-pink-city-180961506/ identifies Yerevan's pink building stone as tuff formed from compacted volcanic ash.
- Yerevan's official tourism site, **Building of the RA Cadastre Committee**: https://www.visityerevan.am/places/details/587/en describes finely dressed pink tuff facades and a unified arcade of columns and arches.
These support a rose-tuff body and an architectural arcade miniature. No photographs or source pixels are used. The three-arch composition is invented, not a replica of that building, a sacred ornament, or a claim to represent every Armenian tradition.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth 2.48; exterior z=.10 to 1.56. Four continuous rose-tuff skins with subtle pores, not cedar joints or glacier terrain. Front is -Y, rear hinge +Y, right +X; export glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body procedural 256px pink/salmon map: mean red > green > blue, visible nonzero variation. Dark volcanic feet and muted charcoal seam. Narrow seam z=1.564..1.672, recessed/narrowed near rear hinge.
- Lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30, centered z=1.828. Warm tuff face, inherited brass corners/latch/ring. Hollow five-sided liner and attached rear hinges; no claim of physically engineered stone hardware.
- Three separate rose-tuff semicircular arches across X (centers -.95,0,.95), centered y=.35. Inner radius .29, outer radius .39; spring z=2.32, tops below 2.75. Piers rooted at lid z=1.978; depth .20. Three open apertures must ray-clear at z=2.43 along Y; crown rays must hit stone. A thin dark platform is allowed only below the arch roots. All ornaments are lid children.
- Inherited exact lid/latch pivots and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid rotation. Collision-sample every moving mesh against static parts and latch against lid at 81 poses; only explicit inherited mating bearings exempted.
- Real-source poster. Offline embedded viewer <5,000,000 bytes; desktop and 390x844 touch emulation over file://, rotation, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-input fallback and no-JS poster.
- Independently reopen saved source, compare exported vertices within 1e-5, verify packed maps, arch bounds/apertures/ancestry, finite/nondegenerate actual-loader geometry. Exact byte/SHA-256 inventory and archive readback.
