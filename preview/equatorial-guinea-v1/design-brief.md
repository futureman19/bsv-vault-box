# Equatorial Guinea v1 — Bioko basalt & rainforest

Written before authoring. An original landscape-inspired collectible, not a replica of a supplied image. This is one regional interpretation, not a claim to represent all Equatoguinean cultures.

## Research and interpretation
- Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program, Pico Basilé: https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=224004 describes the largest/highest of three basaltic shield volcanoes forming Bioko and basalt/picro-basalt rock types.
- Smithsonian GVP, Gran Caldera de Luba: https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=224002 describes a basaltic shield with a broad summit caldera on southwest Bioko.
- Bioko geography/ecology overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioko describes tropical rainforest and montane forest. Used for broad ecological context, not species identification.

No source images/textures are reproduced. The forested shield and hollow rim are a deliberately compressed landscape synthesis, NOT a topographic map or exact Luba model. The dark central depression has no invented erupting lava or glacier. Brass is the collection's fictional hardware language, not a claim of traditional local metalworking. No sacred/ethnic symbols or unsupported inscriptions.

## Measurable visual contract
- Keep wide box silhouette: exterior width 3.72, depth 2.38, main stone from z .10 to 1.56, lid .30 thick centered at z 1.828. Charcoal basalt body; four subtle vertically segmented panels per long face, asymmetric fine mineral mottling. Five-sided continuous hollow liner retained.
- Dark warm charcoal base map RGB roughly 38–85, compact 512x256 color/roughness/normal maps; polished lid satin charcoal, brass hardware warm gold. Body roughness .65–.85. No external textures or 2K maps.
- Narrow muted green seam centered z 1.618, height .108; rear seam narrowed to .08 to clear mechanism. Lid corners capped with restrained brass.
- Lid relief: broad asymmetric shield footprint around 2.5 by 1.55, height under .65 above lid; distinct depressed caldera center at approximately (-.35,.10), raised irregular rim; main shield toward (+.45,+.15). No needle-shaped volcano, no ice. Forest: at least 40 irregular flattened broadleaf canopy clusters in 3 green tones across shoulders, no conical pines. Geometry stays within lid edges. All relief/canopy meshes parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Front=-Y; rear=+Y; right=+X; glTF=(X,Z,-Y). Reuse the Japan trimmed mechanical baseline exactly: rear hinge pivot (0,1.32,1.668), staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid lift. Confirm actual pivot values from source rather than guessing latch transform. Hollow floor and ten mechanical attachment pairs must pass.
- Sample 81 poses of every moving part against every static collectible mesh, plus latch against lid parts; only named mating bearings exempt. This is sampled surface-intersection evidence, not continuous manufacturing certification.
- Standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, embedded GLB/runtime/actual source-render poster, no runtime network. Keep editable .blend and generated maps separate.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source checks, actual GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry, bidirectional source/export vertex tolerance 1e-5, portable-map variation, explicit relief/forest hierarchy, caldera depression and dimensions. Offline file:// desktop and emulated mobile native controls, touch rotation, interrupted reset, reduced motion, full-open framing, parser corruption and no-JS poster fallback, no external requests/errors/overflow. Exact SHA-256 inventory and byte counts. Preserve genuine failures; never manufacture PASS. Visual inspection is separate from numerical acceptance. No photorealism, exact geography, physical-phone performance, minting or custody proof claimed.
