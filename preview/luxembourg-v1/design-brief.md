# Luxembourg — Mullerthal / Sandstone & Fern

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this researched visual contract, not a supplied illustration. This is a stylized landscape keepsake, not a geographically accurate miniature or a claim to represent all Luxembourg.

## Research
- Luxembourg tourism, Berdorf: https://visitluxembourg.com/place/berdorf — sandstone formations, narrow crevices, caves and extensive forests; Huel Lee bears traces of historic millstone extraction.
- Mullerthal regional tourism: https://mullerthal.lu — eroded sandstone landscape, forest valleys and streams; Natur- & Geopark Mëllerdall entered the UNESCO Global Geoparks network in 2022.
- Luxembourg tourism, Black Ernz valley: https://visitluxembourg.com/natura-2000-area-valley-of-the-black-ernz — sandstone cliffs, beech forests, ferns and streams.
Research informs shapes/materials only. No third-party imagery is embedded. No sacred/heritage pattern or landmark replica is asserted.

## Measurable visual contract
- Retain wide family box approximately 3.72 wide by 2.38 deep, hollow five-sided interior; inherited lid and latch pivots unchanged. Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Main body pale buff/ochre sandstone, target sRGB #B79B71 to #D1BC91 with subordinate dark pores, irregular fine sediment layers and visible edge breaks. Avoid Morocco's salmon palette and Japan's wood grain. Procedural portable color/roughness/normal maps at 512px or smaller preferred.
- Satin muted brass hardware, inherited physically attached rear hinges and front hasp. Dark olive reveal/seam under 0.12 units; no vegetation obstructs bearings.
- Lid miniature: two or more irregular sandstone outcrops, maximum relief 0.8 units above lid, separated by a visibly open narrow ravine, plus restrained dark forest-green fern/groundcover patches. No generic glacier, cedar tree or monumental castle. All lid terrain belongs to Lid_Hinge and travels with it. A small blue-green stream may occupy the low ravine, bounded entirely on the lid.
- Relief occupies at most 2.8 by 1.65 units in plan; geometry rather than painted mountains. Co-moving outcrop/ravine separation independently checked. Buff stone dominates, olive green secondary, brass punctuation.
- Staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening; preserve exact inherited pivots. Sample every moving mesh against static geometry and latch versus lid for 81 poses, only explicit mating bearing exceptions.
- Offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes with embedded GLB, runtime and actual-source poster. Drag, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced-motion, true corrupt-GLB fallback and no-JS poster.
