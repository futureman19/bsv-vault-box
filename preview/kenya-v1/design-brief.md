# Kenya v1 — Rift Escarpment & Soda Lakes

Written before modeling. This original landscape-inspired collectible is not a replica of a traditional Kenyan craft object and does not claim to represent all Kenyan cultures. No external imagery is embedded.

## Research
UNESCO's Kenya Lake System description (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1060/) identifies Bogoria, Nakuru and Elementaita as shallow alkaline lakes on the Great Rift Valley floor, surrounded by steep escarpments and volcanic outcrops. Retrieved via web search for this brief. These are the primary factual inspiration: a sculpted volcanic body and three inset lake forms, not a literal map or hydrological model.
UNESCO Mount Kenya (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/800/) describes an extinct volcano and the transition to semi-arid savanna grasslands. This supports the wider volcanic/grassland palette, not an assertion that Mount Kenya sits beside the three miniature lakes. No mountain replica, sacred motif, national emblem or borrowed photo is used.

## Measurable visual contract
- Family-wide keepsake silhouette: approximately 3.72 wide by 2.38 deep; body height about 1.9 units. Hollow five-sided liner and inherited feet/hardware; no tall cube.
- Primary body: dark warm volcanic stone, charcoal-brown target sRGB #514335 with subordinate ochre pores and broken irregular relief. Compact procedural color and roughness maps, maximum 512 square each; authored imagery, not scans.
- Lid: earth/ochre escarpment relief with irregular low ridges flanking a lower basin. Three distinct small blue-green soda-lake inlays, not a continuous river; target #538c82, cream shoreline #d0bf8e and dry grass #9b874e. Geographic composition is artistic, not to scale. Relief remains below 0.7 above lid to preserve box silhouette.
- Warm muted brass hardware; retain proven family hinge and latch connectivity rather than inventing a mechanism. Narrow dark seam, <=0.10 high. No dense symbol or beadwork imitations.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y). All terrain, shorelines and lakes must descend from Lid_Hinge. Keep exact inherited pivot positions and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening.
- Independent saved-source reopening; exported geometry parity <=1e-5; actual loader finite/degenerate checks; full moving-vs-static and latch-vs-lid sampled collision testing over 81 poses with only specifically named bearing contacts exempt.
- Actual source render poster; self-contained file:// HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including runtime/model/poster. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile controls, rotation, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-model and no-JS fallback, zero network requests and overflow.
- Theme tests must check three lake meshes, lid ancestry, relief bounds and real map variation. Preserve real failure evidence if encountered; negative controls must demonstrate checks can reject broken inputs.
