## Frozen visual contract
- Wide family keepsake silhouette: body nominal width 3.72, depth 2.38 and height approximately 1.9 units, inherited hollow inner shell. Front -Y, hinge rear +Y, right +X; glTF mapping (X,Z,-Y).
- Warm chalk/ochre plaster-inspired body (sRGB approximate #cdb990), dark oxblood timber cornice (#752e27), golden ochre (#c49a49) and subdued teal (#39706a) painted accents, dark brown lid wood (#593c2b). Brass mechanical hardware remains the collection language, not a historical nail-free joinery claim.
- Actual raised narrow timber ribs/framed panels around the body and a stepped timber lid cornice. Keep a narrow dark seam under 0.12 units. Compact procedural 256/512px color/roughness maps, not scans.
- Lid centerpiece: an abstract low asymmetric mountain relief, approximately 2.2 x 1.0 footprint, no more than 0.6 units above lid, flanked by a restrained painted geometric border. Not a named mountain, monastery replica or sacred diagram. The ridge and every border/cornice part belong to the lid hierarchy.
- Preserve exact inherited pivot locations and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. No ornaments inside hinge sweep or latch path. All moving meshes included in 81-pose surface collision sampling; only specific inherited mating bearings may be exempt.
- Poster must be rendered from the saved source. Review actual desktop/mobile closed/open views; prioritize the pale body, red timber cornice, painted accents and low ridge silhouette. Stylized craft interpretation, not photorealistic equivalence.

## Acceptance gates
1. Independently reopen saved blend; hollow shell, pivot positions/parentage, all themed moving parts and 81 sampled poses pass. No continuous collision/manufacturing claim.
2. Actual GLTFLoader finite geometry, nondegenerate triangles, embedded textured materials; source/export world vertices within 1e-5.
3. Theme tests enforce body proportions, named geometry counts, palette/maps and lid ancestry, with a deliberate negative control or preserved real failure.
4. File:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile: load, drag, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced motion, real corrupt-GLB fallback, no-JS poster, no external HTTP requests, no overflow or unexpected JS errors. Emulation is not phone hardware.
5. Whole standalone HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes; exact HTML/GLB bytes and SHA-256 inventory; build and checks reproducible. Additive scope only preview/bhutan-v1, shared mechanics read-only.
