# Belgium / Petit Granit — pre-model visual contract

Written before authoring. This is a new written-reference design, not a reconstruction of supplied concept artwork. Belgium is represented through one regional stone tradition, not a claim to summarize all Belgian cultures.

## Research
- IUGS Geoheritage, https://iugs-geoheritage.org/geoheritage_stones/petit-granit-pierre-bleue-de-belgique/ : Petit Granit is a crinoidal limestone from Wallonia (Hainaut and Condroz), grey to greyish blue and shiny black when polished; used in Belgian architecture, decoration and sculpture.
- Carrières du Hainaut, https://www.carrieresduhainaut.com/en/quarry/belgian-bluestone : compact blue-grey limestone containing calcite-cemented crinoid fragments, also shells and other fossils; weathered stone develops a light grey patina.
- Belgian Blue Stone, https://www.pierrebleuebelge.be/en/belgian-blue-stone/ : Soignies quarrying and worked finishes; occasional calcite veins.
Sources consulted as text, not copied texture imagery. Maps and fossil-inspired relief are invented procedural artwork, not geological specimens, scans or artisan-authored sculpture.

## Measurable design contract
- Preserve the collection wide keepsake silhouette: outer stone shell approximately 3.72 wide, 2.48 deep, body from z .10 to 1.56. Continuous hollow five-sided liner; no simulated solid box interior.
- Four continuous blue-grey limestone exterior panels rather than inherited cedar finger courses. Matched, nonoverlapping corner joints. Base-color atlas 512px or smaller, dark blue-grey matrix (approx RGB 45–95, 55–105, 65–115) with irregular pale crinoid-inspired rings/fragments and subordinate sparse calcite streaks. No wood grain, trees, flags or food stereotypes.
- Polished charcoal-blue lid slab centered z 1.828, dimensions 3.72 x 2.38 x .30; lower roughness than honed body. Four short stone feet. Narrow dark slate seam maximum .108 high; rear strip kept clear of inherited hinges.
- Lid ornament: low raised fossil-inspired pale calcite rosette, centered around (0,0,2.00), six to ten radially arranged segmented curved arms with open gaps; footprint within x +/-1.35 and y +/- .82. Highest ornament below z 2.35. This is decorative abstraction of crinoid remains, not an anatomically correct fossil or a named traditional motif. All ornament is lid-parented and seated on the slab, not floating. Keep enough dark lid exposed for high contrast on mobile.
- Restrained warm satin brass corner plates, left side ring, front medallion and inherited latch. No copied wood end-grain accents. Hardware is collection design language, not a historical Belgian chest reconstruction.
- Coordinates: front -Y, back +Y, right +X, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Exact inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot; 80-degree latch release first quarter, then 105-degree lid opening. Ornament travels with lid. Test all moving mesh surfaces against static parts and latch versus lid, 81 poses, only explicitly named bearing exclusions.
- Real Blender source poster, not promotional AI substitute. Offline all-embedded HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, native Open/Close/Reset, drag rotation, reduced-motion endpoint snapping.

## Acceptance gates
Independent saved-source reopen, hollow floor ray, hinge attachments, all-part sampled collisions; source/GLB vertex parity within 1e-5; real GLTFLoader finite vertices/nondegenerate triangles/maps; source theme tests for stone panels, palette/roughness variation, ornament footprint/height/ancestry/seating and open gaps; unchanged mechanical pivots; desktop and emulated touch mobile file:// controls, reduced-motion, no network/no overflow/errors, corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster fallbacks; exact size/hash delivery inventory and archive readback. Negative controls must fail without weakening criteria. Report visual limits independently of technical checks. No claim of photorealism, physical-device performance, continuous collision proof, minting or hosting.
