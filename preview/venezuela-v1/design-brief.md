# Venezuela — Tepui / Cloud & Cascade

Written before modeling. Original brief-led miniature, no supplied illustration or approved reference. The primary design reference is this contract.

## Research
- UNESCO Canaima listing https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/701/ (direct retrieval was challenge-blocked; not claimed read).
- UNEP-WCMC World Heritage datasheet http://world-heritage-datasheets.unep-wcmc.org/datasheet/output/site/canaima-national-park — retrieved search excerpt describes isolated flat-topped, vertical-sided sandstone table mountains, weathered summits and waterfalls. Multicolored sandstone, quartzite and erosion inform the stone palette and relief.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaima_National_Park — retrieved search excerpt corroborates sandstone tepuis, near-flat tops and vertical walls, and Auyantepui's association with Angel Falls.
Research is factual inspiration only; no third-party images/textures incorporated. This invented composition is not a scale replica of Angel Falls, not a geological survey or an indigenous craft/sacred motif. Cloud refers to pale mist-toned water, not simulated cloud geometry.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Broad keepsake body 3.72 wide by approximately 2.48 deep. Body stone rises z=.10 to 1.56; lid 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centered z=1.828. Hollow retained liner and four stone feet.
- Warm rust/sandstone portable 512px color and roughness maps with irregular strata and subordinate grain. Base sRGB target approximately (150,85,58); dark stone seams, muted olive vegetation, pale blue-white cascade and satin brass fittings. No copied cedar grain/finger joints.
- One irregular polygonal tepui, footprint about 1.8 x 1.2 on lid, summit z=2.85 (relief about .87). Near-vertical layered cliffs and flat top; miniature summit vegetation. Separate slim continuous cascade on the front cliff to a small basin on lid. All terrain, water and vegetation parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Brass family medallion, ring and small corner protectors; existing precise hinge and clasp seats unchanged. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Same inherited pivots, staged 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. Full 81-pose moving/static plus latch/lid collision sampling with only named bearing exclusions; hollow floor and attached hinges verified.
- Actual Blender source-render poster. Self-contained offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes. Rotation, open/close, reset, reduced motion; no runtime network.
