# France v1 — Burgundy Glazed Tile

## Research and interpretation (frozen before modeling)
Primary design reference is this written contract, not a supplied illustration. Beaune regional tourism describes polychrome glazed flat-tile roofs as a Burgundian architectural tradition and identifies Hôtel-Dieu, Santenay and Corton André as examples:
https://www.beaune-tourism.com/explore/discover-the-hospices-de-beaune/visit-the-hotel-dieu-museum-hospices-de-beaune/the-art-of-glazed-tiles/
Its Hospices overview distinguishes the exterior slate roof from the courtyard glazed decoration:
https://www.beaune-tourism.com/explore/discover-the-hospices-de-beaune/
Retrieved search text describes colorful patterns, prestige, and historical local tile manufacture. No research photographs or text are used as texture assets. This is a regional French inspiration, not a claim to represent all France or a replica of the hospital. The cream stone body and brass fittings are invented complementary design choices; tile layout is an original geometric abstraction, not a traced historical pattern.
