# Azerbaijan v1 — Lahij Copper

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced visual contract, not an existing illustration.

## Research and boundaries
UNESCO, Copper craftsmanship of Lahij: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL?RL=00675 describes hammered thin copper plates, polishing and engraved decoration with environmental themes in the Lahij community of Azerbaijan. UNESCO decision https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/10.COM/10.b.7 records its 2015 inscription and apprenticeship tradition. Retrieved through web search for this brief.
Interpretation: a warm hammered-copper keepsake vault with a low raised ornamental medallion, concentric rings and eight abstract leaf forms. These are newly invented botanical geometry, NOT a reproduction of a named historic pattern, artisan work or sacred lettering. No third-party imagery embedded.
