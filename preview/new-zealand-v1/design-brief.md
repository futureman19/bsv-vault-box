Written before modeling. This is an invented landscape miniature, not a surveyed replica, traditional carving, or depiction of all New Zealand cultures.

- New Zealand Department of Conservation, [Fiordland nature and conservation](https://www.doc.govt.nz/parks-and-recreation/places-to-go/fiordland/places/fiordland-national-park/about-fiordland-national-park/nature-and-conservation): hard crystalline gneiss, schist and granite; glaciation created U-shaped valleys and fiords.

Research informs materials and shapes only; no source photographs or textures copied. No sacred/cultural motifs or claims of traditional craft authenticity.
