## Research

The range is shared by Liechtenstein, Austria and Switzerland; the motif is not exclusive to one country. [The Three Sisters](https://tourismus.li/en/activities/art-culture/fables-from-liechtenstein/the-three-sisters) documents the local legend. Only the three-rock silhouette is interpreted; no religious scene is reproduced. Retrieved search evidence 2026-11-04. This is not a surveyed mountain model or traditional stone craft claim.
