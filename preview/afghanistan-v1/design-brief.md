# Afghanistan — Lapis & Hindu Kush

Frozen before modeling. Primary reference is this written, researched contract; no supplied or claimed approved illustration. A regional mineral/landscape interpretation, not a representation of every Afghan tradition.

## Research
- GIA, Wyart/Bariand/Filippi, Lapis-Lazuli from Sar-E-Sang, Badakhshan, Afghanistan (1981): https://gia.edu/doc/Lapis-Lazuli-from-Sar-E-Sang-Badakhshan-Afghanistan.pdf — identifies Hindu Kush deposits, blue lazurite, associated calcite/dolomite and pyrite.
- IUGS geological heritage: https://iugs-geoheritage.org/geoheritage_sites/the-sar-e-sang-lapis-lazuli-deposit — locates Sar-e-Sang in northeastern Afghanistan and describes blue mineral lenses in marble with calcite and pyrite.
Research supports palette/material association only. All maps are original procedural paintings, not mined material, photographs or scans. No religious or political symbols. Relief is invented, not surveyed topography.
