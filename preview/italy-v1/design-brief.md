# Italy / Florentine Stone — frozen v1 contract

Written before authoring. Primary design reference is this brief, not an existing illustration. A regional craft-inspired interpretation of Italy, not a claim to represent all Italian traditions.

## Research
The Italian Ministry of Culture's Opificio delle Pietre Dure describes the Florence workshop founded in 1588 and its commesso (Florentine mosaic) process using colored stones: https://opificiodellepietredure.cultura.gov.it/en . Its specialist restoration department documents mosaics and Florentine commesso: https://opificiodellepietredure.cultura.gov.it/en/area/restoration-department-mosaics-and-florentine-commesso/ . Research informs material contrasts and fitted stone decoration only; no museum photos, patterns or textures are copied. The radial flower and leaves below are original geometric inventions, not replicas or an attribution of artisan workmanship.
