# Micronesia / Basalt & Tidal Canals

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this written contract, not an approved illustration. Micronesia here means the Federated States of Micronesia; this interpretation focuses on Pohnpei and does not claim to represent all four states or every Micronesian culture.

## Research
- UNESCO, Nan Madol: Ceremonial Centre of Eastern Micronesia, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1503 : over 100 constructed islets off Pohnpei, basalt and coral boulders, monumental columnar-basalt header/stretcher construction, waterways. Retrieved via web search for this brief.
- McCoy, Alderson and Thompson, archaeological field survey, FSM archive: https://nach.gov.fm/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/mccoy-alderson-and-tohmpson-2015-a-new-archaeological-field-survey-of-the-site-of-nan-madol-pohnpei.pdf : columnar basalt, boulders and coral rubble fill in lagoon islets.
- CSRM Foundation, https://csrmfoundation.org/nan-madol-sustainable-conservation-plan : local stewardship/conservation and predominant columnar basalt construction.

Use material and waterway structure as inspiration, not replicas of tombs, sacred buildings or ceremonial symbols. No source photography, inscriptions or maps are copied. The ornamental layout is invented and not an archaeological reconstruction.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Wide family box, body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y and 1.9 high. Front is -Y, rear hinge +Y, up +Z. Hollow continuous five-sided liner.
- Charcoal to blue-gray basalt body: dominant color targets #333f43 through #62716d, subtle generated mineral grain/roughness. Real horizontal prism courses/end caps on exterior evoke stacked basalt, not wood or ice. Compact procedural maps no larger than 1024 per axis.
- Satin dark stone lid; narrow muted teal seam less than 0.12 high. Gold/brass family corners, feet, latch and rear hinge attachments. Hardware is contemporary collection fiction.
- Lid: shallow teal lagoon inlay within lid footprint, at least five distinct low pale stone islet platforms separated by visible connected channels; asymmetric arrangement. At least one low open rectangular stack of basalt prisms, not a tall temple. Relief height at most 0.65 above lid. No palm/tree/glacier inheritance.
- Stone platforms pale gray coral-limestone inspired, not harvested coral; canals target teal #176b70 to #43a5a0. Raised basalt miniature echoes body material. All relief is attached to and travels with Lid_Hinge.
- Retain original pivots and staged latch release 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees. Sample 81 poses with all moving parts against static surfaces and latch versus lid; only explicit original mating-bearing exemptions.
- Offline standalone viewer includes all assets and actual source-render poster, strictly less than 5,000,000 bytes. Desktop and emulated mobile file://, native controls, drag, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-model and no-JS fallback, no HTTP requests or runtime errors.
