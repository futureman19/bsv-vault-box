# El Salvador / Añil & Volcanic Lagoon

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced visual contract, not an existing illustration. A contemporary invented collectible combining two Salvadoran associations; not a traditional artifact or exact landscape model.

## Research
- Official tourism, [Learn more about Añil](https://elsalvador.travel/article/learn-more-about-anil/en): natural blue dye extracted from Xiquilite and Suchitoto's indigo workshops.
- Official tourism, [Arte Añil](https://elsalvador.travel/experience/arte-anil?lg=en): blue-dyed craft and visitor workshops in Suchitoto.
- Official tourism, [Santa Ana / Ilamatepec](http://elsalvador.travel/en/places/santa-ana-volcano-ilamatepec): volcanic summit with sulfur lagoon. No elevation, precise geography, sacred symbolism or historical pattern is reproduced.
Research informs color and landscape only. All pixels/geometry independently procedural, no third-party photos embedded.
