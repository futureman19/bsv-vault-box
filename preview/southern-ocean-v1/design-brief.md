# Southern Ocean — Pack Ice & Polar Blue

Frozen visual contract before modeling. Landscape-led, not a claimed regional craft or ethnographic motif. No supplied concept illustration; this brief is the primary design reference.

## Research
- British Antarctic Survey, Antarctica: https://www.bas.ac.uk/about/where-we-work/antarctica/ describes an ocean surrounding Antarctica, winter sea ice, ice shelves calving icebergs, and the Antarctic Circumpolar Current.
- British Antarctic Survey, The big breakup: https://www.bas.ac.uk/blogpost/the-big-break-up-why-do-scientists-love-enormous-icebergs/ describes flat-topped tabular bergs and smaller drifting fragments.
- BAS, Oceanography and sea ice in the Southern Ocean: https://www.bas.ac.uk/data/our-data/publication/oceanography-and-sea-ice-in-the-southern-ocean-2/ describes the coupled sea-ice/ocean system.
These text references inform independently generated geometry and maps only. No source photographs/textures are copied. The miniature is not geographic, scientific or climate-model output.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake shell approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep, inherited mechanism height, genuine hollow five-sided liner. Front -Y, rear hinge +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Deep polar navy/teal body (target sRGB #123849 to #27667a) with irregular pale blue flowing current streaks and subtle portable normal/roughness variation. Not wood grain or basalt columns.
- Satin dark-blue lid with a shallow inset sea field. Above it: one dominant irregular flat-topped tabular white/cyan iceberg, approx 1.1 × 0.65 footprint, 0.35–0.55 height; at least seven separate low angular white pack-ice floes with visible blue channels between them. Avoid mountain peak/glacier copied from Iceland. Berg situated rear-right but below 0.6 above lid. All ornament rooted on lid and parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Snow white #dcebf0, ice-shadow cyan #72b9c9, dark ocean channels. Generated small maps <=1024 pixels per dimension; no photo scans or external runtime assets.
- Brass family fittings, connected rear hinges and inherited front latch. Narrow dark/cyan seam under 0.12 height. No wildlife, flags, geographic labels or false historical symbols.
- Preserve exact baseline pivot positions and 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. Collision-test every moving part against static parts plus latch-versus-lid over 81 poses; exempt only explicitly named mating bearings.
- Standalone offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes including real source-render poster, runtime, embedded GLB/maps. Drag/touch rotate, Open/Close, interrupted Reset, reduced-motion snap, genuine corrupt-model and no-JS fallback.
- Independent saved-source reopen, geometry parity, actual GLTFLoader checks, desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// browser checks, exact-byte SHA-256 inventory and delivery readback. Tests must assert actual geometry/material/ancestry and retain failures. Mobile emulation is not phone-hardware certification.

## Honest visual boundary
Stylized premium miniature, not photoreal water or physically accurate translucent ice. Deep blue body is an artistic vessel finish, not a literal block of ocean. Hardware/interior are collection-family extrapolations. Source and render provenance are procedural Blender, not AI promotional artwork. No blockchain, deployment, wallet or custody claims.
