# Palestine / Crimson Stitch

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced written contract, not a supplied illustration.

UNESCO's 2021 inscription https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/the-art-of-embroidery-in-palestine-practices-skills-knowledge-and-rituals-01722 describes embroidery on linen, cotton and wool, floral/tree/bird symbols, and intergenerational transmission in Palestine and its diaspora. The community inventory https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/47594.pdf names cross stitch and red thread, and cautions that patterns carry specific regional meanings. This digital piece uses an invented geometric diamond arrangement, not a copied regional garment, artisan-made textile, or authentic traditional pattern. No source images are embedded.
