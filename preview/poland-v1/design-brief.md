# Poland / Cobalt & Cream — frozen pre-model brief

Primary design reference is this written contract, not an existing approved illustration. A ceramic keepsake vault inspired by Bolesławiec's cobalt-on-cream stoneware. One regional craft does not represent all Polish culture.

## Research
- https://zakladyboleslawiec.com/poleng/blog/post/a-beginners-guide-to-polish-pottery-patterns-and-history — manufacturer describes cream-white grounds, blue decoration and circular peacock-eye motifs, and hand-painted production.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boles%C5%82awiec_pottery — overview identifies Bolesławiec in southwestern Poland and characteristic cobalt decoration.
Research informs palette and craft only. No photographs, trademark patterns, makers' stamps or copied textures are used. This digitally procedural design must NOT say handmade in Poland, UNIKAT, or authentic pottery.

## Measurable visual contract, written before modeling
- Wide box body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y with body height approximately 1.9. Front is -Y; rear +Y; up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Cream glazed ceramic body (#e5dcc4 target), cobalt circular floral/eye-like repeats (#183b80 target) over all four walls. Small muted leaf-green accents permitted. Original invented repeated motifs, not a reproduction of a named workshop pattern. At least three readable rows on front; portable generated color and roughness maps <=1024px per dimension.
- Cobalt rounded lid frame and cream inset panel; raised geometric floral rosette centered on lid, 8 petals and central gold/cream boss, maximum relief 0.20 above panel. All ornaments belong to Lid_Hinge. No terrain miniature, temple or trees.
- Fine dark-blue seam below lid, <=0.08 height; hollow continuous liner/floor. Four compact feet. Inherited brass hardware, side ring, front medallion and connected rear hinges. No new semantic seal/custody claims.
- Use unchanged inherited articulation pivots: staged 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid lift. Sample 81 complete-sequence poses, every moving mesh against static meshes and latch against lid, only narrowly named mating-bearing contacts exempt.
- Source .blend with named editable parts, embedded portable maps, actual source-rendered poster, exported GLB. Surface is stylized generated ceramic, not a scan or photorealistic certification.
- Standalone offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes with embedded runtime, model and poster. Native open/close, drag, reset, reduced-motion, parser-failure and no-JS fallback.

## Acceptance
Independent saved-source reopening; source/export world-vertex parity <0.00001; portable maps/UV/finite geometry and no degenerate triangles through actual GLTFLoader; theme palette/rows/petals/lid ancestry; 81-pose sampled intersections and hollow floor; desktop plus 390x844 touch-emulated mobile file://, zero external requests/uncaught errors/overflow, visible closed/open silhouette; exact HTML/GLB byte count and SHA-256 inventory with archive readback. Preserve real failed assertions and fixes. These are sampled geometry/browser checks, not physical-phone testing, artisan authenticity, continuous collision proof or on-chain verification.
