# Montenegro / Durmitor & Tara

## Research and interpretation — frozen before model authoring
UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Durmitor National Park: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/100/ identifies northern Montenegro's limestone massif, glacial landforms, deep river canyons and dense forests. Its description specifically names the Tara gorge and glacial lakes. IUCN World Heritage Outlook https://worldheritageoutlook.iucn.org/node/985/pdf?year=2020 describes rugged peaks, limestone plateaus, canyons and caves. UNESCO text was retrieved through web search for this brief; no photographs or third-party textures are incorporated.

Primary art reference is this written contract, not an existing approved illustration. A miniature karst canyon cuts through a pale limestone lid, with a continuous blue-green river and dark forest patches. The body is weathered gray limestone with fine oblique fractures. This is an invented landscape composition, not a scale map of Tara, traditional craft reproduction, or literal stone chest construction.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake proportions: body width 3.72, depth approximately 2.48, exterior stone from z=.10 to 1.56; lid slab 3.72 x 2.38 x .30 centred z=1.828. Front is -Y, rear hinge +Y, right +X; export (X,Z,-Y).
- Four continuous limestone wall skins around the inherited hollow five-sided liner, four irregular stone feet. Pale gray/warm limestone map mean RGB near (160,163,157), fine gray fractures; no wood grain, cedar, glacier, or sandstone bands.
- Narrow dark forest-green seam height .108, rear strip narrowed to .08 for inherited hinge clearance. Satin antiqued brass clasp, medallion, side ring and small lid corner caps.
- Two independently meshed jagged karst banks run generally left-to-right over the lid, divided by a meandering open gorge. Canyon centre y=.12*sin(2.2*x); water half-width .11, bank inner edge offset .19 or greater. Low foreground bank tops around z=2.2–2.45, taller rear peaks reach z=2.65–2.95. Entire terrain stays inside |x|<1.55, |y|<.93. The water is a continuous opaque turquoise inlay at z=2.005, not a simulation or glass transmission claim.
- Small irregular dark green vegetation patches on outer bank slopes; not giant trees. All ornaments parent directly to Lid_Hinge and travel with lid.
- Retain original pivots and 80-degree latch release first, followed by 105-degree lid swing. Sample 81 poses, every moving mesh against every static mesh and latch against lid meshes. Only documented mating bearings exempt.
- Portable generated color/roughness maps at most 512px per side; physically oriented stone UVs, no photos/scans. Source .blend independently reopened; geometric export parity tolerance 1e-5.
- Offline embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes, source-rendered poster; desktop and 390x844 touch-emulated mobile file:// checks: drag, open/close, reset interruption, reduced motion, corrupt-GLB and no-JS poster, no HTTP requests/errors/overflow. Mobile emulation is not phone performance.

## Acceptance and bounded art decision
Source and export geometry, river continuity/open-channel rays, ornament parentage, map variation, palette, pivots, sampled collisions, actual browser interactions, exact bytes/hashes and ZIP readback must pass. Include deliberate invalid-input/ancestry controls to demonstrate rejection. Inspect normal-size source/browser closed/open images for readable canyon and river; at most two focused corrections. Technical PASS does not establish photorealism, geographic accuracy or human approval. Per task, no individual human review gate; no deployment, chain activity, mint or merge.
