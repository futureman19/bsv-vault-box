# Seychelles v1 — Granite & Lagoon

## Research and interpretation (before modeling)
Primary factual landscape reference: https://worlds50beaches.com/anse-source-dargent/ (retrieved successfully over HTTP 200 during authoring). Its description identifies the Seychelles beach's large granite boulders, shallow clear water and coastal setting. The quoted visitor description identifies turquoise water and palms. This is landscape inspiration, not a measured reconstruction of Anse Source d'Argent or a claim to represent every Seychelles island. No source photograph or third-party texture will be embedded. No invented indigenous craft claims.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y; body top about Z 2.1, lid about Z 2.3. Preserve inherited mechanical exact dimensions where required; document measured bounds.
- Main body warm pink-grey speckled granite (target sRGB #aa9287 with cream/dark mineral flecks); hollow five-sided interior. No basalt columns or Japanese wood joints.
- Lid landscape: a pale sand crescent and low turquoise lagoon inlay, plus at least three asymmetric rounded, vertically weathered granite boulders with different heights and visible grooves. Highest rock no more than 0.85 above lid. Lagoon target #36a9ab, sand #dbc9a0; clean brass hardware target #b28a44. These are authoring palette targets, not rendered pixel guarantees.
- Low, restrained dark teal seam under 0.12 high. Brass family latch, side ring and attached rear hinge hardware; no flags, inscriptions or generic tree copied from Japan. Boulders clustered rear/right (+X,+Y), lagoon foreground/left. Lid geometry and all landscape objects must travel with Lid_Hinge.
- Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, up +Z. Inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot exact pivots; latch release 80 degrees then lid 105 degrees. Preserve attachment and hollow liner. Collision sample all moving meshes against static meshes and latch versus lid at 81 poses; only explicitly justified mating bearings exempt.
- Compact independently generated color/roughness/normal maps; no 2K textures needed. Editable packed .blend, exported GLB and actual source-render poster.
- Standalone offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes, embedded runtime/model/poster, native rotation/open/close/reset and reduced motion. No network requests.
