# Ukraine / Petrykivka Bloom — brief frozen before modeling

## Research and interpretation
Primary factual reference: UNESCO, Petrykivka decorative painting as a phenomenon of Ukrainian ornamental folk art, https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/petrykivka-decorative-painting-as-a-phenomenon-of-the-ukrainian-ornamental-folk-art-00893 . Describes household decoration with fantastic flowers and natural elements informed by local flora/fauna, and intergenerational teaching. Ukraine Ministry of Culture inventory hosted by UNESCO: https://ich.unesco.org/doc/src/18937-EN.pdf describes flowerpot, bouquet, branch and frieze compositions, saturated red/green colors and under-lacquer painted wooden objects including boxes. Sources retrieved via web search for this brief; no source photographs or artist designs copied.

This is an original procedural floral interpretation inspired by that living tradition, NOT authentic artisan Petrykivka, an exact historical pattern, or a claim to represent all Ukraine. The written contract is the primary design reference; no supplied marketing illustration exists.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette approximately 3.72 wide × 2.38 deep; exterior body z .10–1.56 and lid slab z 1.678–1.978. Genuine hollow five-sided liner, inherited rear hinges, front clasp and brass feet or dark wood feet.
- Deep near-black lacquered wood body and lid: nominal RGB around #18231d, subtle procedural grain/roughness. Portable color/roughness maps at most 1024 pixels per axis. Warm satin brass inherited hardware, restrained small corner fittings; not an armored cube.
- Body carries original saturated red/coral and ochre flowers, green tapering leaves and branching stems, distributed as painted friezes on visible front/side surfaces. No flag-only reskin. Floral design cannot be merely a label on a blank box.
- Lid carries at least three clearly distinct raised red/coral floral heads with ochre centers, tapering leaves and connected curved stems. Spread across the top within x ±1.55, y ±.9; relief .02–.16 above lid, no tall terrain/tree. The raised relief is a deliberate invented translation of traditionally planar painted ornament.
- Thin dark seam under .12 height, continuous hollow shell behind exterior. No moss/glacier/cedar tree retained.
- Front −Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,−Y). Reuse exact Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot transforms from Japan mechanism, staged −80° latch release then −105° lid opening. Every lid ornament is descended from Lid_Hinge.
- Sample 81 poses against all moving/static meshes and latch-vs-lid; exemptions only explicit inherited mating bearings. Test hollow interior, source-export world positions with 1e-5 tolerance, geometry finite/nondegenerate, portable image variation using roughness GREEN channel.
- Offline self-contained HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes including actual GLB, bundled Three and actual source-render poster. No external network. Open/close, drag, reset, reduced motion and fallback.
- Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// checks; no overflow/errors/external requests. Real corrupt-GLB fallback and no-JS poster. Inspect actual screenshots, not just numeric gates. Emulation is not physical-phone testing; sampled clearance not engineering certification; no photorealism promise.
