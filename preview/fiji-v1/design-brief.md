# Fiji / Masi & Lagoon — pre-model visual contract

Written before modeling. This sourced written brief is the primary reference; no supplied concept illustration or prior art approval is implied.

## Research and interpretation
- Cambridge Museum of Archaeology and Anthropology, https://maa.cam.ac.uk/masi : masi is Fijian barkcloth made from inner paper-mulberry bark; decoration includes stencilling, with regional methods and black, white, red-brown cloth. This informs fibrous cream/umber surfaces, not claims to reproduce a particular community's pattern.
- Metropolitan Museum, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/316402 : Fijian masi kesa panel, rectilinear stencilled decoration, and significant ceremonial uses. Our simple rectangular/triangular border is invented: not a copied ceremonial motif, traditional craft product, or cultural authorization.
- Tourism Fiji, https://fiji.travel/things-to-do/snorkelling/the-ultimate-fiji-snorkelling-guide : fringing, barrier and patch reefs; lagoons separate barrier reefs from shores. This informs a turquoise miniature lagoon with pale rim and green islands, not a geographic map or reef-health claim.
No third-party photographs or museum pixels will enter textures. All maps and geometry procedurally authored.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide family box: lid 3.72 X by 2.38 Y by .30 Z, centered at z=1.828. Body between z=.10 and 1.56, four skins around inherited continuous hollow liner; no cedar tree/finger-joint theme.
- Ivory fibrous body map with dark umber rectilinear bands and russet accents: generated base RGB cream near (218,199,157), umber (57,38,25), russet (132,68,39). Portable 512px color/roughness maps; dark wood interior/edge map 256px; no scans.
- Fine dark reveal seam at z=1.618, height <=.108. Warm satin brass latch, medallion, side ring, small corner plates and connected rear hinges retained from family geometry.
- Lid holds one irregular pale reef/sand plinth, footprint inside X +/-1.5 and Y +/-.87; turquoise lagoon inset inside its boundary. Relief rests on lid z=1.978. Three non-overlapping green islands, heights <=.45 above lid; all carried by Lid_Hinge. Low relief leaves lagoon visible from standard desktop/mobile camera. Lagoon is opaque enamel-like inlay, not simulated transparent water.
- Front latch -Y, rear hinge +Y, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Reuse exact mechanism pivots. Staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Full 81-pose surface-intersection sampling includes every moving mesh against static and latch versus lid; only original named mating bearings excluded.
- Named editable packed .blend, portable GLB, actual-source poster. Offline HTML includes runtime/model/poster and is strictly <5,000,000 bytes; no network assets. Reduced-motion, open/close, drag/touch and reset.
