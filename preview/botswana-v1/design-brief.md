# Botswana v1 — Mokola & Delta

Written before model authoring. A contemporary BoxSV keepsake inspired by Botswana's palm basketry and Okavango inland delta; not an authentic basket, named traditional pattern, geographical survey or representation of every community.

## Researched foundations
- Botswana Tourism Organisation, https://www.botswanatourism.co.bw/arts-and-crafts : baskets use mokolwane palm (Hyphaene petersiana), earth-tone dyes and tight coils. Nature informs many patterns.
- WIPO, https://www.wipo.int/en/web/fit-japan/w/news/2019/news_0004 : Chobe weavers use young mokola palm leaves and native-plant dyes; skilled handwoven work. This digital sculpture is NOT made by those artisans and claims no affiliation.
- UNESCO, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1432 : Okavango is an inland delta with permanent marshes, seasonal floodplains and dry-season flooding.
Research consulted for factual inspiration only; no third-party image or texture copied.

## Measurable contract
- Retain wide family proportions: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, height approximately 1.9, five-sided hollow liner. Front -Y, rear hinge +Y, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Primary exterior: warm natural palm coils, straw #bfa16a, umber #503824, ivory #ddc99a. Procedural portable base-color/normal/roughness maps no larger than 1024 per dimension. A bespoke broken-chevron rhythm, not a copied named traditional motif. Readable fine transverse binding stitches and horizontal coil relief. No wood/granite/cedar legacy visible artwork.
- Body has actual narrow rounded coil relief or ribs in addition to maps; lid framed by a slender basket-inspired rim and brass fittings. Family gold hardware, restrained and satin, inherited proven mechanical anchors.
- Lid ornament: shallow sculpted inland-delta fan, maximum 0.38 above lid; at least three physically branching blue-green channels (#327c80) amidst muted reed/sand islands (#777a43 / #b6a16c). Channels are actual geometry, no flat poster. Not a cartographic model or sea delta. Every ornament descends from Lid_Hinge.
- Narrow dark umber seam less than 0.12 high, no green Iceland/Japan moss seam. Rear ornament bounded away from hinge bearing, front away from latch.
- Same rear lid and front latch pivots as reusable mechanism; latch releases 80 degrees during first quarter, then lid opens 105 degrees. Reverse ordering on closing. Full moving/static and latch/lid BVH sampling at 81 poses; only narrowly named mating bearings excluded. Ornamental overlaps within one rigid assembly are not inter-assembly collisions.
- Offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, including runtime, GLB, maps and actual-source-render JPEG poster. No network requests. Drag, zoom, open/close, reset, reduced motion and genuine broken-GLB/no-JS poster fallback.
- Independently reopen source; source/export world vertices tolerance 1e-5; real GLTFLoader finite triangles and zero degenerate triangles. Validate theme geometry/material/hierarchy, desktop and emulated-mobile file:// screenshots, exact SHA256 inventory.
- Technical gates are not photorealism, physical-phone performance or cultural endorsement. Up to two targeted visual corrections; honest stylization notes, no per-asset human review required.
