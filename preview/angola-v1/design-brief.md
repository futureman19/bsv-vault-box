# Angola v1 — Miradouro da Lua

Written before modeling. A landscape-inspired collectible, not a survey replica or a representation of every Angolan tradition.

## Research
- Atlas Obscura, Miradouro da Lua (Viewpoint of the Moon): https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/miradouro-da-lua-viewpoint-of-the-moon — retrieved search excerpt describes canyons and ridges carved by wind/rain erosion. Full-page extraction was unavailable; do not claim full-text review.
- Geological context lead: https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Location-map-of-the-studied-sections-in-Miradouro-da-Lua-illustrating-the-outcrops-in-3D_fig1_260395539 — retrieved excerpt links the cliffs to uplift of the late Neogene Palaeo-Cuanza delta. Informational lead, not a full-paper review.
No source photographs or textures are copied. Earth palette and miniature composition are artistic interpretation. No sacred or ethnic motifs, flag emblems, or unsupported inscriptions.

## Measurable contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette about 3.72 X by 2.38 Y; inherited hollow liner, rear +Y hinges, front -Y latch. Height around 1.9 excluding lid relief.
- Body: warm ochre/terracotta sediment-inspired stone with irregular cream horizons and darker erosion grooves. Generated portable color/roughness maps <=1024 pixels per dimension; no photographic scans. Target albedo colors terracotta #A85D3A, ochre #C58A53, cream #D8BA85, dark earth #543928; lighting may shift apparent color.
- Lid: three or more asymmetric, visibly eroded miniature cliff buttresses/ridges, real relief geometry not a flat photograph; maximum relief 0.65 units above lid. Distinct from Morocco's ceramic/wood treatment: no tiles, no cedar, no ice. Warm earth lid with branching gullies and pale caps, a landscape built into the box.
- Narrow dark-earth seam <=0.10 units; inherited restrained brass corner hardware, medallion and ring; all ornament is parented under Lid_Hinge. Rear/interior are authored extrapolations.
- Preserve exact baseline Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot positions. Latch releases 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees; reversible and resettable. Collision sample all moving meshes against static meshes and latch against lid across 81 poses; exempt only explicit inherited mating bearings.
- Whole offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including Three runtime, GLB, maps and actual-source-render poster. No runtime network requests; reduced motion and fallbacks.
