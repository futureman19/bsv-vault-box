# Kuwait v1 — Sadu & Sand

Written before modeling. Original procedural art direction; no supplied concept image, scan, photographic texture, tribal insignia or claim to reproduce an authentic woven artifact. This is one cosmetic inner collectible, not a chain operation.

## Research
- UNESCO, 2020 inscription decision for Saudi Arabia and Kuwait: https://ich.unesco.org/en/Decisions/15.COM/8.b.4 . Al Sadu is warp-faced plain weave made by Bedouin women on ground looms, with natural fibres, rhythmic geometric repetition and bright reds/oranges. This tradition is shared, not exclusive to Kuwait.
- UNESCO, Kuwait training programme case study: https://ich.unesco.org/en/individual-case-study-art18-01377?id=5 . Al Sadu Society and Kuwait's Ministry of Education support transmission through master-weaver-led teacher training and Sadu House activities.
These sources motivate a contemporary geometric textile-inspired surface. Our invented diamonds and bands do not claim traditional symbolic meanings. Sand-colored sculpted lid relief is an artistic landscape association, not a surveyed Kuwaiti landmark. No third-party pixels are embedded.

## Visual contract
- Preserve wide keepsake silhouette: outer body about 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, floor near Z .265, body top near Z 2.0; exact inherited mechanism dimensions take precedence for safe mating. Front is -Y, rear +Y, head-on right +X; glTF maps (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: recessed textile-inspired red/charcoal/ivory panels framed with dark warm brown wood and restrained brass corner pieces. Red #8f2925, charcoal #292322, ivory #d5bf92 target swatches; generated weave grain subordinate to legible horizontal bands and repeated diamonds. The pattern must read at mobile whole-box size. No flag, lettering, sacred or tribal insignia.
- Lid: dark wood edge, textile inset border and a low asymmetrical sand-colored dune relief (about 2.5 by 1.3 units; maximum height .35 above lid) with two or more curved crest lines, no tree or glacier. Sand #c6a574. Entire relief and inset travel with lid.
- Narrow dark seam under .10 units, hollow five-sided interior, small brass feet/corner fittings and visible inherited rear hinge attachments. Brass hardware is family interpretation, not historical Kuwaiti hardware.
- Inherited named Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot, same seated pivots and 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. Include every moving mesh in 81-pose moving/static and latch/lid collision sampling; only explicit inherited bearing contacts may be excluded.
- Compact generated color/roughness maps (512 or smaller preferred), finite UVs, no degenerate triangles. Save editable packed .blend and export actual GLB. Poster is actual source render.
- Standalone offline HTML strictly under 5,000,000 bytes, embedded GLB, Three/runtime and poster; drag/touch rotation, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced-motion and real parser-failure fallback.
