# Guinea-Bissau — Bijagós Tidal Mangrove

Written before modeling. A landscape-inspired collectible, not a replica of a cultural artifact or geographic survey. No source photographs or sacred motifs copied.

## Research
- UNESCO Biosphere programme: https://unesco.org/en/mab/bolama-bijagos describes the 88-island archipelago, delta origins, mangroves, mudflats and palm groves.
- UNESCO World Heritage https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1431 and committee decision https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/8940 describe active deltaic islands, sandbanks, intertidal habitats and the role of community conservation.
These factual landscape connections inform independently procedural geometry and textures, not an assertion that one design represents all Guinea-Bissau cultures. No mangrove harvesting or traditional box-making claim.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve broad family box silhouette approximately 3.72 wide x 2.38 deep, body about 1.9 high; hollow continuous floor and four walls.
- Body: dark warm estuarine brown (#573b29 target), fine irregular sediment/ripple texture; lighter sand-colored edge detailing. Not Japanese cedar finger joints or Morocco ceramic patterns.
- Lid: shallow sculptural delta lagoon inset, muted teal (#237d79), at least three asymmetric raised sandy islands (#bba36c) separated by visible branching tidal channels. This is an abstract archipelago, not a map of specific islands.
- One small mangrove grove on the rear/right portion (+X,+Y), at least three branching trunks, exposed curved prop roots and clustered olive foliage (#405d32). Total ornament height <=0.95 above lid. Roots meet island surface. All terrain, water, trees and leaves parent under Lid_Hinge.
- Narrow dark teal seam <=0.10 high, satin aged brass latch, rear hinges, small corner hardware; no flags or inscriptions. Existing family hardware is a contemporary extrapolation.
- Blender front -Y, rear +Y, right +X, Z up; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Preserve original family pivots, staged 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. Collision sample every moving mesh against static shell and latch against lid over 81 poses; only explicit mating bearing exemptions.
- Portable independently procedural maps <=512px each; real geometry islands, roots, trunks and foliage. Compact representation prioritized over microdetail.
- Standalone offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including bundled runtime, embedded GLB/maps and actual Blender-render poster. Drag/touch rotate, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced-motion support, corrupt-input and no-JS fallback.

## Acceptance and limits
Independently reopened source hierarchy, floor ray and full sampled motion; source/export world-vertex parity <=1e-5; actual GLTFLoader finite/degenerate tests; desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// interactions, zero external requests and successful-run JS errors, no overflow, open framing; exact byte/hash inventory and delivery check. Theme gates count islands/trees/roots and verify ancestry, bounds and palette. Negative controls must fail meaningfully. Visual assessment from actual model render and browser captures. Stylized miniature, not photorealism, continuous collision proof, physical-phone validation or art approval. No chain activity, deployment or production integration.
