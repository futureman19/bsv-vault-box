# Romania v1 — Maramureș / Carved Timber

Frozen before model authoring. Primary reference is this written contract, not a supplied marketing image. A stylized timber-craft collectible, not a replica church or an assertion that one region represents every Romanian tradition.

## Research
UNESCO, Wooden Churches of Maramureș, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/904/ describes narrow high timber constructions, tall slim towers, and shingle-covered single/double roofs. These construction silhouettes inform an invented secular architectural relief on the lid. Research is factual inspiration only; no third-party photographs or textures are embedded. The geometric diamond and braided border are original decorative interpretations, not authenticated traditional motifs.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide 3.72 x 2.38 lid, body walls approximately 1.46 high over inherited floor; closed lid top z=1.978. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF maps (X,Z,-Y).
- Dark warm brown timber with generated longitudinal grain; color atlas nominal warm range #62402b to #996b43 (lighting changes rendered colors). Compact 512px color/roughness and 256px end-grain/stone maps. Real alternating corner joints. No cedar tree or moss.
- Narrow near-black brown seam; subdued satin brass mechanical hardware and corner caps; four irregular gray stone feet. Genuine hollow five-sided interior. Hardware/rear/interior are family extrapolations.
- Lid carries a low elongated steep double-slope shingled roof relief plus one slim timber spire, all attached to the lid. At least 40 individual shingle plates, spire tip no higher than z=3.13 (1.15 above lid). No cross or religious icon. Original light-wood diamond/inlaid border with at least 12 diamonds. Each visible relief element travels with lid.
- Existing rear hinge and latch pivots preserved exactly: latch release 80 degrees in first quarter, then lid opens 105 degrees. 81 sampled moving/static and latch/lid intersection poses. Exempt only named mating bearings, never new ornaments.
- Actual-source poster, standalone offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes, embedded model/runtime/maps. Desktop and 390x844 touch emulation file://, open/close, drag, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt GLB fallback and no-JS poster.
- Independently reopen saved source; hollow-floor ray, hinge attachment, source/export nearest-vertex parity <1e-5, nonfinite/degenerate geometry rejection, theme counts/ancestry/material maps and exact byte/SHA256 archive readback.

Technical acceptance does not establish photorealism, architectural fidelity, continuous collision proof or physical-phone performance. At most two bounded corrections; no human review gate for this batch.
