# Rwanda v1 — Imigongo / Ochre Relief

## Research and interpretation (before modeling)
Primary design reference is this written contract, not an AI illustration. Rwanda's imigongo is raised geometric art, traditionally formed on wooden panels using dung and ash, dried and painted. Black, white, red and ochre are documented pigments. This digital collectible interprets its relief and palette; it is not an authentic artisan-made object, a copy of a particular artist's panel, or a claim about every Rwandan tradition.

Sources retrieved during design:
- The New Times, 19 May 2022, “Imigongo: The evolution of Rwanda's traditional designs”: https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/195963/News/imigongo-the-evolution-of-rwandaas-traditional-designs — reports Kayonza makers, relief process, geometric zigzags/diamonds and black/white/red. Distinguishes imigongo from unrelated basket motifs.
- AFAR, “From the Ashes: Rwanda’s Traditional Imigongo Art Is on the Rise”: https://afar.com/magazine/from-the-ashes-rwandas-traditional-imigongo-art-is-on-the-rise — wooden base, raised relief, kaolin white, soil red, ochre and black pigments.
No research photographs or artist pattern pixels will be embedded. No symbolism, royal origin or sacred meaning is asserted for our independently composed geometry.

- Warm red-earth/plaster body on continuous hollow wood liner. Compact generated pigment/grain maps, at most 1024 per dimension. Palette targets (sRGB approximate): terracotta #99452f, ivory #e7d7b8, charcoal #231d19, ochre #b98535. Lighting changes apparent colors.
- Body front and side carry actual shallow geometric relief bands, not only a flat texture. At least 12 independently countable raised motif elements; front clasp space remains readable. Ivory/charcoal diamonds and zigzags, composed anew rather than copied.
- Lid carries a raised geometric panel: nested diamond/chevron geometry in ivory and charcoal with restrained ochre accents, maximum ornament height .16 above lid. No inherited Japan tree, moss or Iceland glacier. Entire lid ornament is parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Satin warm brass hardware retains family language, existing pivot seating and rear hinge connections. Thin dark seam, no grass border. Four dark wooden/earth feet. Rear/interior are design extrapolations.
- Inherit trimmed mechanism only; staged latch rotates -80 degrees first, lid then -105 degrees. Sample at least 81 poses over complete motion, including all new moving motifs; only explicit existing bearing contact exceptions. Continuous analytical clearance is not claimed.
- Actual source-render poster, actual offline browser desktop/mobile screenshots. No promotional AI substitute. Stylistic craft-inspired digital art, not photoreal equivalence.
- Self-contained offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes; GLB, runtime and fallback embedded. Rotation, open/close, interrupted reset, reduced motion, corrupt-model and no-JS fallbacks; zero external requests and no overflow. Mobile is emulated, not physical-device performance.
