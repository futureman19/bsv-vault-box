# United Kingdom / Slate & Walled Meadows

## Research and scope — frozen before authoring
Primary reference is this written contract, not an existing approved illustration. UNESCO's Slate Landscape of Northwest Wales (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1633/) describes the slate industry and its transformation of mountain and valley landscapes. The Yorkshire Dales landscape archive (https://outofoblivion.org.uk/themes/agriculture/hedges-field-walls) describes dry-stone field boundaries, throughstones and coping without mortar. These inform a composed slate keepsake with a miniature walled green landscape. It is not a geographically accurate place, a historic construction replica or a claim to represent all four UK nations. No research photographs or third-party textures are embedded.

## Measurable visual contract
- Retain wide family silhouette: slate shell 3.72 wide, 2.38 deep; exterior courses from z .10 to 1.56; closed lid centre 1.828, thickness .30. Hollow continuous interior behind decorative courses.
- Six staggered real slate courses on each exterior side, varied stone lengths, narrow joints. Blue-charcoal/purple slate procedural color map (nominal sRGB 77,83,100), subtle grain; portable roughness map, not scans. Warm satin brass family latch, ring, medallion and four corner protectors.
- Dark green mineral seam at z 1.618, thickness .108; narrow rear strip avoiding inherited hinge attachments. Four stone feet.
- Lid meadow relief confined to x +/-1.45 and y +/-.85; undulating surface z 2.00–2.40. Four muted greens, not neon grass. Two intersecting low dry-stone boundaries composed of individual blocks and upright coping; they follow the terrain surface. Deliberately invented field layout, no flags, heraldry or sacred imagery.
- All terrain and field-wall objects parented to Lid_Hinge. Front is -Y, rear +Y, right +X. Preserve existing pivots, 80-degree latch release in first quarter, then 105-degree lid swing. All moving meshes included in 81 collision samples; only explicit mating bearing contacts excluded.
- Offline embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes; source-render poster, editable .blend, GLB and maps separate. Desktop and mobile file:// open/close/drag/reset/reduced-motion and real corrupt-GLB/no-JS fallback checks.
- Independently reopen source; source/export geometry error <1e-5, decoded color and roughness variation >0; terrain attachment and wall counts checked. Strict byte inventory/archive readback. Technical acceptance does not imply photorealism, continuous collision proof, or phone hardware performance.

## Acceptance and limits
Source, parity, theme, browser and delivery reports are executable assertions. Negative controls must demonstrate rejection. UK identity is a selective material/landscape interpretation rather than a complete national symbol. Local art only: no blockchain, keys, minting, website deployment or external messaging.
