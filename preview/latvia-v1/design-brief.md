# Latvia v1 — Linen & Amber

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced design contract, not a supplied or approved illustration.

## Research
Latvia's official tourism site describes wood and linen among the locally available materials used in Latvian craft and names loom-woven linen products: https://www.latvia.travel/en/latvian-crafts . Its Riga Christmas market listing includes amber jewellery and wooden craft: https://www.latvia.travel/en/event/rigas-christmas-market-2025 . Retrieved search excerpts support these limited factual connections; direct page extraction was unavailable. No photographs or third-party patterns are copied. This invented textile panel is not an ethnographically exact regional belt or sacred symbol.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide warm honey-brown wooden body, 3.72 wide, approximately 2.48 deep; body z .10–1.56. Eight alternating finger-jointed courses. Hollow inner shell and four small stone feet.
- Satin wooden lid 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 at z 1.828. Front is -Y, rear +Y. Retained brass hinge, staged latch and ring family hardware. Narrow dark brown seam no higher than .108; rear clearance strip .08 deep.
- A raised cream linen-inspired panel 2.95 × 1.65, centered on lid, bottom z 1.981, top 2.011. Fine procedural crosswoven 256px portable color and roughness maps. Cream target sRGB near (194,181,150), red bands near (112,30,36). Two red woven border bands along Y edges, visibly broad, not a flag claim.
- Three low amber-inspired smooth cabochons, along X at -.9,0,.9; each footprint approximately .36 × .27, rising .12 above its brass setting. Honey-orange opaque material, not physically accurate fossil resin/transmission. Brass settings sit on the panel. All panel/ornaments belong to Lid_Hinge.
- Ornamental gaps and low relief are deliberate; no copied Latvian folk symbols. Body grain is generated, not scanned wood. Shared mechanism only; no Japanese tree, moss or other country terrain.
- Latch releases 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees; preserve inherited pivot transforms. Sample 81 poses including every moving mesh against static meshes and latch versus lid. Only named concentric bearing contacts exempt.
- Actual source render poster; standalone offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes. Reduced motion, desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// interactions, no external runtime requests, parser-error and no-JS poster fallback.
