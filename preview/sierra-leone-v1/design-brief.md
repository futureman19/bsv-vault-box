# Sierra Leone v1 — Gola Canopy

Written before model authoring. A forest-earth keepsake box inspired by Sierra Leone's Gola rainforest, not a replica of a traditional craft object or a representation of every Sierra Leonean culture.

## Research and interpretation
- Trillion Trees, https://trilliontrees.org/project/gola-rainforest/ (retrieved 2026-11-02), identifies the Greater Gola Landscape spanning Sierra Leone and Liberia as one of the largest remaining Upper Guinean rainforest tracts; describes leafy shade trees and forest-friendly cocoa. Page text independently retrieved over HTTPS. This supports the broadleaf canopy theme, not any specific tree species reconstruction.
- Research correction on resumed production (visual contract unchanged): the earlier tentative-list citation was outdated. UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1746/ and decision https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/8936/ confirm the Gola-Tiwai Complex (Gola Rainforest National Park and Tiwai Island Wildlife Sanctuary) was inscribed in 2025. Current indexed UNESCO text was retrieved; direct HTTPS extraction returned 403. Trillion Trees' indexed project page independently reconfirmed the forest/cocoa context. No UNESCO endorsement of this artwork is implied.
No source photographs, textures, sacred motifs, flags or third-party artwork are copied. Earth/bark material and abstract relief are artistic interpretations, not a claim of local stone identification, harvested timber, botanical accuracy or conservation sponsorship.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Wide family silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y; retain the proven pilot shell, floor, rear hinge and latch pivot locations. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Hollow five-sided interior.
- Primary body: dark cocoa/umber earth-bark relief, generated warm brown map, sRGB target range around #523324 to #986044; irregular vertical shallow furrows, not Japan's horizontal cedar/finger-joint construction. Satin dark forest-green lid panel, target #173f30 to #47734a. Brass hardware, target warm muted gold, not silver.
- A thin dark green seam under 0.10 units tall; maintain rear hinge clearance. Reuse family latch and connected rear hardware; disclose extrapolated functional appearance.
- Lid landscape: asymmetrical low broadleaf forest grove with at least three individually branched trunks, broad flattened irregular canopies in at least three muted green tones, and visible buttress/root forms on a low forest-floor island. Main grove at rear half of lid, height no more than 1.15 units above lid. At least 30 broad leaf clusters, no conical pine/cedar needles. Entire grove and floor parented beneath Lid_Hinge.
- Body is identity-bearing, not just a stock box with a tree: new generated bark/earth maps, real shallow relief bands/ribs or root-like ridges and deep green lid create the forest-earth visual language. Do not imply bark is structural engineering.
- Staged 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening; preserve named pivots and choreography. Sample 81 poses against all static meshes plus latch-versus-lid; only explicit inherited mating bearings excluded. Ornament must move rigidly with lid.
- Procedural maps at 512px or below unless a measured budget justifies otherwise. Entire offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including runtime, actual GLB and actual Blender-source poster. Source/editable maps remain separate.
