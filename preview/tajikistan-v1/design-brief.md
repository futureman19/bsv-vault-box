# Tajikistan / Pamir Ridge & Alpine Lake

Written before authoring. The primary reference is this sourced written contract, not a supplied illustration. An invented miniature landscape, not a scale model of a named peak or lake.

## Research
- UNESCO, Tajik National Park (Mountains of the Pamirs): https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1252 — describes rugged glaciated peaks, high plateaux, deep gorges and numerous lakes.
- UNESCO inscription decision: https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/5130 — describes the juxtaposition of alpine-desert plateaux and glaciated mountains, and Sarez and Karakul lakes.
- IUCN World Heritage Outlook: https://worldheritageoutlook.iucn.org/node/1176 — discusses glaciation and high-altitude lakes.
Retrieved through web-search source excerpts; direct UNESCO extraction returned HTTP 403. No source photography or textures copied. Gray mineral stone, ivory snow and blue lake are artistic palette choices, not geological sampling. The brass family hardware is an invented fitting, not attributed traditional Tajik craft.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body exterior 3.72 X by 2.38 Y; wall height 1.46, from z=.10 to 1.56. Hollow inherited interior, no cube proportions. Coordinates: front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Gray-brown mineral body, portable 512px mottled stone color and roughness, compact 256px granular foot texture. Dominant stone sRGB approximately #85817c, warm satin brass. No wood grain or cedar branches.
- Dark narrow mineral seam at z=1.618, height .108; keep rear strip narrow to clear hinges. Satin stone lid 3.72 by 2.38 by .30 centered at z=1.828. Four small brass corner protectors, retained clasp and ring, four irregular mineral feet.
- Lid terrain: a continuous irregular rocky ridge across the rear half, footprint X [-1.45,1.45], Y [-.1,.94], highest elevation z=2.65–2.95. At least three visually distinct summits, pale snow on upper portions. No standalone cone primitives. Enclose the terrain edges down to the lid.
- One blue irregular alpine-lake inset in the front half, centered near (.10,-.48), footprint about 1.65 by .64. Water top at z=1.992–2.025; lake must remain visually exposed in normal mobile framing. Not a map of Sarez or Karakul, not fluid simulation.
- All terrain/lake/snow geometry parented to Lid_Hinge. Original 80-degree latch release in first quarter, then 105-degree lid opening; retain original pivot transforms. Collision sample all moving meshes against static meshes and latch against lid across 81 poses. Only explicitly named bearing contacts excluded.
