# Timor-Leste / Tais & Indigo

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced design contract, not a supplied artwork.

## Research
- UNESCO, Tais traditional textile: https://ich.unesco.org/en/USL/tais-traditional-textile-01688 — Timor-Leste's handwoven cotton, plant dyes, intergenerational skills and important social uses; motifs and colors differ across groups.
- UNESCO ICHCAP, Interconnectedness of Culture and Craft: https://unesco-ichcap.org/interconnectedness-of-culture-and-craft-tais-in-timor-leste — narrow cloth panels, plain weave and ikat yarn-resist techniques.

The design celebrates textile structure, not any particular community's protected motif. Rust/indigo/ivory stripes are an invented palette; no claim that these colors or the abstract broken-line pattern represent all Tais. No sacred figures, copied textile photographs or artist-authenticity claims. The chest frame and brass are collection fiction, not a Timorese historic object.

- Wide keepsake body 3.72 X by approximately 2.48 Y, body z .10–1.56; solid hollow interior liner retained. Dark warm timber frame with inset rust-red woven panels on four faces.
- Body cloth panels .96 high, with parallel indigo/ivory/rust bands and fine visible warp/weft. Procedural 512-square color and roughness maps, plus timber color map. Palette target rust RGB near (135,43,32), indigo (27,40,65), ivory (213,190,141). Generated illustrative textile, not a scan or exact traditional weave.
- Satin wood lid 3.72 by 2.38, z 1.678–1.978. A low rectangular woven runner sits on top, x ±1.55, y ±.82, top z 2.008–2.052 with gentle waves; its skirt reaches the lid. At least 20 fine fringe strands at each X end. This is rigid decorative relief, not simulated fabric.
- Narrow dark indigo seam <=.108 high, interrupted around hardware. Four compact feet and restrained brass corners; inherited long latch and side ring, not a newly claimed traditional fitting.
- Original pivots, rear +Y and front -Y. 80-degree latch release first, then 105-degree lid opening. All runner/fringe meshes must travel with lid. 81 sampled poses test all moving/static and latch/lid pairs with only the inherited explicit bearing exclusions.
- Source .blend independently reopened; geometry parity tolerance 1e-5; actual GLTFLoader rejects nonfinite or degenerate triangles. Verify runner height, dimensions, fringe counts and ancestry, decoded map variation.
- Real source-rendered poster, desktop/mobile offline file:// captures, native touch/drag/open/close/reset, reduced motion, corrupt-data and no-JS fallback, no external requests or overflow. Mobile emulation is not physical-device performance.
- Whole offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes; exact SHA-256 inventory and archive readback. Additive-only in this directory. No mint, node, keys, chain, deployment or production edits.
