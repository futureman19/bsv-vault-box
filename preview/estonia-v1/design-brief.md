# Estonia / Limestone & Bog — frozen pre-model brief

## Research and interpretation
- Visit Estonia, “Rocks, banks and caves in Estonia”, https://visitestonia.com/en/why-estonia/histories-written-in-stone identifies limestone as Estonia's national rock and describes its longstanding architectural use and northern coastal outcrops.
- Institute of Geology, Tallinn University of Technology, *Limestone – a National Stone of Estonia* (2007), http://gi.ee/geoturism/Carbrocks_ENG_100dpiS.pdf records national-stone designation in 1992 and white, yellowish and grey limestone varieties.
- Visit Estonia, “Five day trips from Tallinn for nature lovers”, https://visitestonia.com/en/what-to-do/five-day-trips-from-tallinn-for-nature-lovers describes the wooden boardwalk at Viru Bog in Lahemaa National Park.
Research retrieved through web-search excerpts; direct extraction backend unavailable. Sources inform material and landscape, not texture copying. No third-party images embedded. This is an invented limestone keepsake with an abstract bog miniature, not a scale map, traditional craft reproduction or a claim that limestone directly underlies the depicted bog.

Shallow irregular sediment bands and fine pores via 256px generated base-color/roughness/normal maps; no wood finger joints.
- Lid slab 3.72 × 2.38 × .30 centered at z=1.828. Four stone feet. Warm satin brass clasp, side ring and restrained corner caps preserve family hardware language. Muted dark green seam <=.108 high.
- Lid ornament: three separated organic bog pool basins at (-.85,.25), (.05,.36), (.88,.18), each with a closed peat/moss rim and visible dark blue-green inset water. Lowest ornament z>=1.97; highest <=2.28. Rings are geometry, not painted circles. Boardwalk: 18 small planks across the front at y=-.51, x=-1.36 to +1.36; ochre weathered timber, not a full-sized bridge. All ornament belongs to Lid_Hinge.
- Palette targets: limestone map mean RGB near (174,177,164), +/-30 each; peat ochre/olive, pool blue-green, modest brass. Pools and boardwalk must be readable in actual normal desktop and 390px mobile captures. Abstract organic ovals, not a faithful Viru map.
- Coordinates: front -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Retain inherited pivot positions, 80-degree latch release followed by 105-degree lid opening. Collision-sample all moving parts at 81 poses against every static mesh and latch against lid; only original named mating bearings exempt.
