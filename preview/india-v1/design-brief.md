# India / Marble & Parchin Kari

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this written contract, not a supplied illustration.

## Research
UNESCO https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/taj-mahal describes Agra's living Parchin Kari marble-inlay craft, floral/geometric patterns and lapis lazuli, jade and cornelian. UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/252/ describes white marble, stone inlay and floral ornament at the Taj Mahal. These support a regional craft-inspired design, not a claim to represent every Indian culture. No photographs, copied historical motifs, inscriptions, sacred emblems or miniature tombs are used. Patterns are original procedural floral interpretations; not artisan-made inlay or a monument replica.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide body 3.72 by 2.38, body z .10–1.56; rounded lid 3.72 by 2.38 by .30 at z 1.828. Five-sided hollow inherited liner.
- Warm ivory marble body/lid with subtle grey-gold irregular veins; portable 256px generated color and roughness maps. Ivory target RGB about 225/218/200, not wood grain.
- Dark green seam no more than .108 high; rear strip narrowed to avoid moving hinges. Four low marble feet. Satin brass family latch, ring, corner caps; original pivots retained.
- Lid contains one central eight-petal carnelian-red flower plus four smaller six-petal lapis-blue flowers, linked by green stems/leaves. Actual shallow geometry, seated at lid z 1.978; relief maximum z 2.04. Floral palette red (#a84426), blue (#254679), green (#30634b), ivory. An original raised intarsia-inspired interpretation rather than literal flush-cut stone work.
- Body carries a repeated small blue/green ornamental band; do not inherit cedar finger joints or a tree.
- All lid ornament parented to Lid_Hinge. Latch rotates 80 degrees during first quarter, lid then 105 degrees; coordinates front -Y/rear +Y. Same inherited pivot positions. Collision test every moving mesh against static geometry and releasing latch against lid over 81 poses; only original named bearing contacts exempt.
- Offline HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes; embedded runtime/GLB/actual source-render poster. Desktop and touch-emulated mobile file:// controls, drag, reset, reduced motion, actual corrupt-input fallback and no-JS poster.
- Independent saved-source reopening, source/GLB vertex parity <1e-5, actual-loader finite/nondegenerate mesh checks, theme count/height/ancestry checks, exact SHA-256 archive readback. Deliberate invalid-theme negative control must fail.

## Limits
Procedural stylized collectible, not photorealism, authentic historical pattern reconstruction, continuous collision certification or physical-phone testing. No minting, keys, nodes, deployment or on-chain storage. User waived individual art approval; delivery is an autonomous v1 candidate for collection integration.
