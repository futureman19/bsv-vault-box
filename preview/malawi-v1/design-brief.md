# Malawi v1 — Lake of Stars / Granite & Cichlids

Pre-model contract. Landscape-inspired miniature, not a surveyed site or an authentic traditional craft reproduction. No supplied concept image exists for this card; original geometry and generated maps only.

## Research
- UNESCO, Lake Malawi National Park: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/289/ — clear water, granitic hills, rocky shores and sandy bays; exceptional endemic cichlid diversity.
- Malawi tourism: https://visitmalawi.mw/lake-malawi-national-park/ — southern lake, islands, terrestrial miombo woodland and aquatic ecosystem.
- UNESCO conservation report: https://whc.unesco.org/document/180695 — massive rounded granite boulders washed by waves, sandy bays between rocky sections.
Retrieved via web search for this brief. Research informs forms/palette, not copied imagery. Fish motifs are stylized cichlid-inspired inlays, not species-identification claims. “Lake of Stars” is the artwork title, not a scientific designation.

## Measurable visual contract
- Retain wide family keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, height approximately 1.9; hollow five-sided interior, brass hardware and four stable feet. Front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: warm pale granite with irregular mineral speckle, not basalt columns or cedar grain; compact generated color/roughness/normal maps at 512px or less. Dominant warm grey/sand, dark charcoal flecks, restrained brass highlights.
- Lid: deep blue/turquoise lake enamel inset, bounded warm sand shoreline, at least three asymmetrically grouped rounded granite boulders rising 0.15–0.5 units above lid. No glacier or inherited Japanese tree. At least three small gold/blue cichlid-inspired fish silhouettes integrated into lake surface; separate body and tail geometry, not a text label.
- Lake blue target sRGB approximately #126B88 to #219DA9; granite #A99B83 to #D1C5AC; brass #B58B42. Lighting may alter rendered values. A thin dark seam (<0.12 high) separates body and lid.
- Proven family mechanical pivots unchanged: latch releases 80 degrees first, then lid opens 105 degrees; all lid ornaments travel with Lid_Hinge. Hardware kept clear of ornaments. Include every new ornament in 81-pose moving/static and latch/lid collision sampling; exemptions only named mating bearings.
- Actual saved-source poster and desktop/mobile closed/open captures must show lake/shore/boulders at normal viewing scale. Bounded two correction passes; disclose stylization, no photoreal-equivalence claim.
- Entire standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes, all runtime resources embedded. Source .blend, GLB and maps separate. Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile, controls, drag, interrupted reset, reduced motion, real corrupt-GLB fallback, no-JS poster, no external requests.

## Acceptance
Independent saved-source reopen, finite/valid real GLTFLoader geometry, source/export world-vertex parity tolerance 1e-5, explicit theme geometry/material/hierarchy tests, 81-pose sampled collisions, browser checks and byte/hash inventory. Gates must assert actual values and exit nonzero on failure. Sampled collision tests do not prove continuous mechanical safety; emulation does not prove physical-phone performance. No blockchain, deployment, shared-kit modifications, or other preview edits.
