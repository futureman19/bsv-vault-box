# Grenada / Nutmeg & Mace — frozen pre-model contract

## Research and interpretation
Grenada Tourism Authority describes Gouyave's nutmeg sorting, grading and packaging tradition: https://www.puregrenada.com/gouyave-nutmeg-processing-station . Its Laura's Herb & Spice Garden page describes nutmeg-shell paths and local spice-growing heritage: https://www.puregrenada.com/lauras-herb-spice-garden . Retrieved for this design via web search. These support a spice-inspired collectible, not an assertion that this invented box is a traditional Grenadian craft. No photographs or third-party textures are copied.

## Visual contract written before modeling
- Theme: **Nutmeg & Mace**. Wide warm nutmeg-brown spicewood body with fine irregular grain, dark chocolate interior, satin brass hardware and a restrained red-mace seam. Not another mountain landscape or cedar tree.
- Body approximately 3.72 wide by 2.38 deep, wood height approximately 1.9, matching the inherited Japan mechanism envelope; hollow five-sided liner. Seam <=0.10 units high, not intersecting the rear hinge sweep.
- Lid carries a sculptural botanical still-life: one large brown oval nutmeg with separate curling red/orange mace ribs, two golden split-fruit cheeks flanking the seed, and a short branch with at least three elongated green leaves. Invented arrangement, not a botanical reconstruction. Ornament occupies central lid, stays inside its XY perimeter and below 1.0 units above the lid. The negative spaces between mace ribs must leave the seed visible.
- Palette targets: body cocoa/nutmeg brown (#70432c family), seed dark brown (#4b2a1a), mace rich rusty red (#b43b24), fruit ochre (#c9974a), leaves muted deep green (#385f38), satin brass. Procedural color/normal/roughness maps <=512px per dimension; real nonzero map variation.
- Reuse articulated family hardware/pivots, not Japan ecology. Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z. All botanical lid pieces must descend from Lid_Hinge.
- Motion: latch release 80 degrees over first quarter then lid 105 degrees. Independently reopen saved source and collision-sample 81 poses with every moving mesh versus static and latch versus lid; only explicit inherited bearing contacts may be exempt.
- Source-authored actual render poster; source .blend and exported GLB; offline standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes. Open/close, drag, reset and reduced-motion behavior; no network requests.
- Independent source/export parity, actual GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry, theme dimensions/palette/ancestry, file:// desktop and mobile-touch controls, corrupt-input and no-JS fallback, byte/SHA-256 archive readback. Negative controls must genuinely fail. Mobile is emulation, not phone performance.

## Scope and limits
Only preview/grenada-v1 is writable. Existing kit/previews, chain/custody/mint paths, keys and production are out of scope. This is stylized procedural art, not photorealism, traditional craft certification, an approved historical artifact, or a minted asset. Per-asset human review is waived for this collection batch.
