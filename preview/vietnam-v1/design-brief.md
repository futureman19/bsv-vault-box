# Vietnam v1 — Emerald Karst

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced contract, not an existing approved illustration.

## Research
UNESCO's Ha Long Bay–Cat Ba Archipelago listing https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/672/ describes vegetated limestone islands and towering pillars rising from the sea, marine-invaded tower karst and sheer cliffs. The nomination summary https://whc.unesco.org/document/186551 describes forested peaks over emerald waters. Retrieved through web search; direct extraction backend unavailable. These inform a stylized landscape miniature, not a geographically exact diorama. No research images or third-party texture pixels are embedded.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide keepsake body: 3.72 × 2.38; body from z .10 to 1.56. Continuous pale warm limestone exterior with subtly mottled procedural color/roughness, not cedar finger joints. Hollow inherited liner.
- Satin limestone lid 3.72 × 2.38 × .30, centered z 1.828. Dark teal reveal under .12 high. Retain satin brass corner hardware, clasp and side ring; pale stone feet.
- Inset emerald water panel 3.14 × 1.84, top near z 2.01. Five separated irregular steep-sided limestone islands, heights .30–.68 above water, vegetation-colored crowns. All islands and water belong to Lid_Hinge. Island footprints remain within the water panel. No flags, temple, copied sacred motifs or unsupported inscriptions.
- Palette: limestone approximately #aaa58d, water #176c60, crown #304d26, warm gold. Authored procedural maps at 256–512px; no scans. Visible limestone ledges and forest caps are stylized, not photoreal foliage.
- Preserve inherited pivot matrices and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening; front -Y, rear +Y. Collision-sample 81 poses including every moving island and water panel; only explicit bearing contacts exempt.
- Offline embedded viewer under 5,000,000 bytes, actual source-render poster, native desktop/mobile controls, reduced motion, corrupt GLB and no-JS fallback; no requests.

## Acceptance
Independent saved-source reopen, geometry parity with GLB within 1e-5, hollow floor ray, all moving/static and latch/lid collisions sampled, five island ownership/height checks, runtime finite/nondegenerate meshes, desktop/mobile file:// checks, SHA-256 inventory and ZIP readback. Tests must fail on violations. Physical-phone speed, continuous mechanical collision proof, geographic accuracy, traditional artisan authorship and photorealism are not claimed. Local collectible art only; no chain operations or deployment.
