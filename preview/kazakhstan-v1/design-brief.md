# Kazakhstan / Steppe Felt & Brass — design contract v1

Written before authoring. This is an original material/craft-inspired collectible, not a replica of a historical chest or a comprehensive representation of Kazakhstan.

## Research and interpretation
- UNESCO, Decision 9.COM 10.24: https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/9.COM/10.24 describes Kazakh and Kyrgyz yurts as wooden circular frames covered with felt and braided ropes; craft includes wood, leather, metal, weaving, felting and ornamented coverings. It explicitly identifies zoomorphic, vegetative and geometric ornament. Search-retrieved text inspected for this brief; the practice is shared, not exclusive to Kazakhstan.
- UNESCO current element: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-knowledge-and-skills-in-making-kyrgyz-and-kazakh-yurts-turkic-nomadic-dwellings-00998 describes renewable natural materials, organic/geometric coverings and intergenerational craft. Do not mistake the current extended inscription for the original 2014 scope.
- Kalpak Travel, Ornaments of Central Asia: https://kalpak-travel.com/blog/introduction-ornaments-central-asia/ identifies the Kazakh koshkar muyiz (ram's horn) motif and its variations across Kazakh/Kyrgyz art. Use original paired curling geometry, not copied artwork or claims of exact ethnographic pattern reconstruction.
No third-party images or text are used as textures. No sacred emblem, inscription or official flag reproduction. Warm felt, oxblood horn-scroll applique and a raised wood lattice medallion evoke craft rather than miniaturizing a literal dwelling.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body width 3.72, depth 2.38, approximately 1.9 tall. Front is Blender -Y, rear hinges +Y, right +X; export (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: warm undyed wool/cream felt cladding (#C5AE82 target, with fine nonuniform fiber grain), dark warm wood structural edges; oxblood (#6D272D target) original paired curling bands on front/sides. Generated maps <=512px each initially; actual portable color/roughness maps, no scans. Targets are palette direction, not rendered pixel equalities.
- Lid: satin warm wood frame with a recessed cream felt panel, four paired oxblood horn-scroll ornaments surrounding a low raised circular wooden lattice medallion. Medallion approximately diameter 1.0, relief <=0.20; it is a contemporary geometric craft allusion, not an authenticated shanyrak or religious symbol. Real tube/ribbon geometry, not just painted circles.
- Thin dark seam <=0.10; continuous hollow inner liner and floor; inherited brass hardware, feet and mechanically seated rear hinges. Keep brass material response rather than yellow flat paint. No tree, glacier, Moroccan ridge or borrowed country artwork.
- All lid panel, curls and medallion parented under Lid_Hinge. Preserve proven pivots and staged 80-degree latch release then 105-degree lid opening. Reverse closes lid first. Sample at least 81 poses against every static mesh and latch-vs-lid; narrowly named mating bearings only may be exempted.
- Procedurally authored editable packed Blender source, embedded-map GLB, actual-source poster. No photoreal-equivalence claim. At normal desktop/mobile scale, cream field, red scrolls and circular lid ornament must be legible.
- Standalone HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes including runtime/model/poster; no network requests, drag/touch rotation, open/close, reset during motion, reduced-motion snap and genuine parser-failure/no-JS poster fallback.

## Acceptance gates (must actually fail on invalid output)
Saved-source reopening/hollow floor/pivots/81-pose collisions; source/export world-vertex parity <=1e-5; real GLTFLoader finite vertices and zero degenerate triangles; theme parentage and geometry counts; actual decoded image variation; desktop + touch-emulated mobile file:// interaction and overflow; negative-control rejection; exact bytes/SHA-256 inventory and required archive entries. Browser emulation is not a physical-phone performance result. No chain operations, publication, custody proof or public deployment.

Technical acceptance permits autonomous v1 completion per task directive. Visual result is an original stylized craft interpretation, not human-reviewed ethnographic or manufacturing certification. Stop after the bounded v1 acceptance, leaving batch integration to its separate card.
