## Research
- UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/754 identifies Lake Baikal in southeast Siberia as the oldest/deepest lake and a distinctive freshwater ecosystem (retrieved through web search).

These inform an abstract frozen-lake miniature, not precise bathymetry or a claim that one landscape represents all Russian cultures. No third-party pixels are used.
