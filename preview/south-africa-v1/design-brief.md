# South Africa / Table Mountain & Fynbos

Frozen before modeling. A regional landscape interpretation, not a claim that one Cape landscape represents every South African culture. No supplied concept artwork: this written contract is the primary design reference.

## Research
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table_Mountain — flat-topped Cape Town landmark; resistant quartzitic sandstone cliffs; fynbos vegetation. Retrieved search text used for factual inspiration, not texture pixels.
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1007/ — Cape Floral Region Protected Areas; contextual source for the region (not independently extracted in this run).
- https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain — park authority reference (direct extraction unavailable).

## Visual contract
- Wide 3.72 × 2.38 keepsake box; sandstone body from z .10 to 1.56, satin pale sandstone lid centered at z 1.828, thickness .30. Five-sided hollow interior retained. Warm gray/ochre stone color target approximately sRGB (171,156,129), irregular fine strata and granular pores; generated maps <=512px. Not wood, scans or exact geological reconstruction.
- Four granite feet, muted olive seam z1.618 with rear hinge clearance. Warm satin brass family hasp, medallion, small corner fittings and side ring. No national symbols or sacred ornament.
- Flat-topped irregular mesa on lid: footprint roughly 2.1 × .9, center (0,.20), foot z1.97, summit z2.65–2.75; steep segmented cliff face, fine geological ledges. Low olive fynbos-inspired clusters at its feet, no cedar/tree. Terrain and plants all lid-owned. This is a silhouette miniature, not a surveyed mountain or botanical model.
- Front -Y, rear +Y, up Z. Keep original mechanism pivot matrices; latch releases 80 degrees before lid opens 105 degrees. Sample 81 poses, all moving/static meshes and latch/lid pairs; only named mating bearings exempt.
- Offline HTML <5,000,000 bytes, embedded runtime/GLB/actual-render poster, native desktop/mobile controls, reset, reduced motion, corrupt-input and no-JS fallback. No external requests.
- Gates: independent saved-source reopening, bidirectional source/export vertex distance <1e-5; texture variation, mesa dimensions/flat summit and parentage; actual GLTFLoader finite/degenerate checks; byte inventory and ZIP readback. Negative-control detached terrain must fail ancestry.

## Limits
Procedurally authored miniature with simplified family hardware. No photoreal-equivalence, physical phone performance, continuous collision certification, issuance, hosting or chain claims. Explicit no-review directive: deliver autonomously after technical gates and actual-image inspection.
