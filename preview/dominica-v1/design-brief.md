# Dominica — Rainforest & Boiling Lake

Written before modeling. Primary design reference is this sourced contract, not supplied/approved promotional artwork.

UNESCO https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/814 describes Morne Trois Pitons National Park's tropical forest, precipitous volcanic relief, fumaroles and Boiling Lake; lake level and coloration vary. Retrieved via web search during authoring. This miniature juxtaposes those features, not a surveyed geographic model. Dominica, not Dominican Republic. No photographs, UNESCO imagery, indigenous motifs or factual inscriptions copied. All textures procedurally generated.

## Frozen visual contract
- Family wide box: body width 3.72, depth 2.38, walls z .10–1.56; continuous inherited hollow liner. Four irregular volcanic feet. Dark grey volcanic body (#343d3b approximate texture midtone), subtle speckling/pores, no wood courses or finger joints.
- Satin dark-stone lid 3.72 × 2.38 × .30, center z 1.828; narrow forest-green reveal height .108. Inherited small warm brass corner caps, side ring, front medallion and working visual hasp. These are invented family hardware, not traditional Dominica craft.
- Sculpted lid terrain spans x ±1.45, y ±.83, based z 1.98. Irregular rainforest volcanic ridges rise to z 2.65 or less. Three higher rear knolls suggest volcanic relief, not an exact landmark silhouette. Forest green canopy texture/geometry contrasts charcoal body.
- Readable pale grey-blue geothermal lake at front-center x=-.15, y=-.28, radii .55 × .35; raised irregular rocky rim surrounds flat recessed water. Lake water z=2.08; rim z 2.10–2.24. No opaque steam plume obscuring basin. Water is stylized opaque ceramic-like inlay, not fluid simulation. All terrain/lake/rim parented to Lid_Hinge.
- Blender front -Y, back +Y, right +X. Retain original hinge/latch matrices; latch rotates 80° first, lid 105° afterward. Collision sample all moving/static meshes and latch/lid at 81 poses, only original mating bearings excluded.
- 256px procedural stone color and roughness, 256px forest color maps. No oversized imagery. Standalone embedded HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes.

## Failable acceptance
Source reopen and bidirectional exported-vertex parity <1e-5; packed maps ≥3; sampled collisions empty; hollow floor below z .3; hinge attachments physically connected. Theme gate checks body/rim/lake geometry, terrain/lake lid ancestry, lake bounds/height and map variation; deliberate detached lake negative control must fail. Actual GLTFLoader finite/nondegenerate geometry and full-open viewport fit. Offline file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile open/close/drag/reset/reduced motion, corrupt-input and noJS poster, no network/errors/overflow. Inspect actual source poster and browser screenshots, not just assertions. Exact-byte SHA256 inventory and archive readback. Mobile emulation is not physical-phone validation; no photorealism, mint, custody or manufacturing claims.
