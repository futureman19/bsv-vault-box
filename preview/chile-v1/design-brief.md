# Chile / Atacama Salt & Copper — v1

Written before modeling. Primary reference is this sourced design contract, not a supplied illustration. One landscape-inspired interpretation of northern Chile, not a representation of all Chilean cultures.

## Research
- Chile's official tourism site, https://chile.travel/en/destinations/san-pedro-de-atacama/ (retrieved HTTP 200), describes an Atacama oasis, lagoons and salt flats, and surrounding desert scenery.
- Chile Travel, https://chile.travel/en/blog/7-chilean-salt-flats-that-will-amaze-you-and-how-to-visit-them/ identifies Salar de Atacama as Chile's largest salt flat (search excerpt consulted).
Sources inform the landscape idea only; no photographs or text are copied into textures. Copper-colored fittings are an artistic palette choice, not a claim about a historical craft or artifact. No sacred motifs, flags or literal geographic accuracy.

## Frozen measurable visual contract
- Preserve family wide keepsake body about 3.72 X by 2.38 Y with body height about 1.9; hollow liner and visible narrow seam. Front is -Y, rear +Y, up +Z; glTF maps (X,Z,-Y).
- Body: warm mineral terracotta/umber with fine irregular desert sediment bands, restrained salt-white flecks. Generated color/roughness/normal maps <=1024 pixels per dimension. Palette targets terracotta #995B42, deep umber #51352B, ochre #C88E58; these are approximate albedo targets, not exact rendered pixels.
- Lid: low irregular ochre desert terrain with a visibly exposed pale polygon-cracked salt basin (cream #E6DFCA), at least 0.7 by 0.45 units. Two asymmetrical distant ridges/volcanic silhouettes at rear; highest relief 0.35–0.75 units above lid. Not a glacier, cedar tree, dome or flag plaque.
- Salt basin foreground must remain legible at default desktop and 390px mobile framing; foreground ridge must not hide it. Named basin and ridge geometry, not just a flat image. All lid terrain follows Lid_Hinge.
- Brushed warm copper/brass hardware, existing latch and seated rear hinges, dark narrow gasket under 0.12 units. Maintain inherited pivot matrices: staged 80-degree latch release, then 105-degree lid opening.
- No speculative new mechanical parts. Sample 81 articulation poses including every moving ornament against static parts, and latch versus lid. Only explicit inherited mating bearings may be exempted.
- Real Blender editable source, exported GLB and actual-source rendered poster. No AI illustration substitute. Standalone embedded offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes.

## Acceptance written before model
Independent saved-source reopen and hollow-floor ray; source/export vertex parity within 1e-5; actual GLTFLoader finite geometry and no degenerates; nonconstant portable material maps (roughness green channel); basin size, ridge relief and lid ancestry assertions; motion endpoints and 81-pose sampled collision check. Browser file:// desktop and touch-emulated mobile: open/close, rotate, reset, reduced motion, no overflow/external requests/runtime errors, corrupt-model and no-JS fallback. Actual source/poster and browser screenshots visually inspected. Byte inventory and source ZIP readback. Negative controls must prove meaningful gates can fail. Sampled geometric evidence is not manufacturing certification; mobile emulation is not physical-device performance; stylized procedural art is not photorealistic or geography-exact.
