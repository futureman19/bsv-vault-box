## Research and interpretation
UNESCO identifies the ceramic-making processes of Talavera de la Reina and El Puente del Arzobispo in Spain alongside Puebla and Tlaxcala in Mexico. Ceramicists may perform the entire process or specialize. The committee records historical continuity of these practices.
