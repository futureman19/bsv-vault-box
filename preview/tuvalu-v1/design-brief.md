# Tuvalu / Pandanus & Lagoon

Frozen pre-model visual contract. No supplied artwork; this written brief is the primary reference, not a claim of approved traditional design.

## Research
- Pacific Community, Traditional knowledge in Tuvalu offers lessons for future of nations youth: https://www.spc.int/updates/blog/2021/08/traditional-knowledge-in-tuvalu-offers-lessons-for-future-of-nations-youth — dried pandanus leaves are woven into mats and used in roofing.
- Objectspace, To Weave Again: https://www.objectspace.org.nz/exhibitions/to-weave-again/ — Niutao pandanus weaving incorporates dyed fibre; nonu root gives red and togo bark black. Island traditions differ. Our simple interlace is invented and not a copy of a named family or ceremonial motif.
- Landscape inspiration is a low coral-island lagoon, not a surveyed geographic model. No research imagery embedded; all maps procedurally authored.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve inherited wide 3.72 by 2.38 keepsake silhouette, hollow inner shell and approximately 1.9-high body. Front -Y, rear +Y, up +Z.
- Body: warm straw pandanus-inspired woven panels with readable alternating over/under rectangular strips. Palette cream/straw (#d5b67c, #aa7a42), narrow dark brown/red accent borders (#493326, #974d37). This is stylized woven cladding, not a real watertight fibre vault.
- Lid: framed woven perimeter enclosing a shallow turquoise lagoon (#258e99) and an irregular ivory sand ring/arc (#e5d4a3). Relief remains low: maximum 0.32 above lid. No mountains, cedar trees, or Iceland glacier. Lagoon polygon must be contained by outer island footprint. No national flag or sacred symbols.
- Satin brass family hardware, narrow dark seam under 0.12, inherited pivot matrices. Release latch 80 degrees before opening lid 105 degrees. All island/water features owned by lid hierarchy.
- At least one compact procedural color/roughness/normal map set; no external textures. Author editable Blender scene and portable embedded GLB.
- Actual-source render poster, not AI artwork. Standalone HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes with all runtime assets embedded, rotation, open/close, reset, reduced-motion and genuine parser/poster fallbacks.

## Acceptance gates
Independent source reopen; source/export vertex parity within 1e-5; hollow floor ray; all moving geometry collision sampled over 81 staged poses with only named bearing-contact exemptions. Theme checks must verify weave variation, lagoon containment, relief height, lid ownership, and inherited pivot matrices. Actual GLTFLoader verifies finite geometry and nondegenerate triangles. Offline file:// desktop and mobile touch emulation exercises controls, framing, reduced motion, corruption and no-JS fallback, zero external requests/errors/overflow. Delivery inventory records exact bytes and SHA-256 with ZIP readback. Visual inspection of actual desktop/mobile closed/open views is separate from technical tests; no photorealism, surveyed geography or physical-phone claims.
