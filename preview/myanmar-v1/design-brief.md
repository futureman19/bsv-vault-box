# Myanmar / Bagan Lacquer — visual contract before authoring

## Research and limits
- Heritage Science, “The evolution of the materials used in the yun technique for the decoration of Burmese objects”, https://heritagesciencejournal.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s40494-019-0272-z describes engraved grooves filled with colour, black/red grounds and repeated lacquer coats, and Bagan production.
- Abbey Museum, https://abbeymuseum.com.au/burmeselacquerware describes polished lacquer over bamboo/wood and incised colour decoration, including a late nineteenth-century Bagan bowl.
- British Museum object https://britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_1998-0723-261 documents woven bamboo lacquer with yellow-on-green yun decoration.
These inform finish and palette only. This rectangular vault is invented, not a reproduction of a museum object or traditional sacred pattern. No source photographs are embedded; no physical toxic pigments are used. The woven substrate is not exposed or claimed modeled.

## Measurable contract
Wide 3.72 by 2.48 body with skin z=.10–1.56; satin-to-gloss near-black lacquer and vermilion linework. Continuous rather than finger-jointed external panels. Four dark feet. Hollow inherited interior. Thin dark red seam at z=1.618, height .108, with narrowed rear segment to clear hinge leaves. Lid slab 3.72 by 2.38 by .30 at z=1.828. Brass corner protectors, front long staged hasp and side ring preserve family mechanics.

Compact generated 512x512 lacquer colour and roughness maps and red lining map. Repeated fine nested diamonds/parallel borders are an invented geometric evocation of incised yun, not documented motif fidelity. On the lid, a low oval vermilion lacquer medallion (radii .73/.49, height .06) and three raised thin brass concentric ovals (max z 2.07), all lid-parented. No temple model, Buddha iconography, flags or text imitations. Brass is restrained hardware, not a claim that yun traditionally uses these vault fittings.

Front -Y; rear +Y; right +X; up +Z. Preserve exact inherited pivots: latch 80 degrees first, then lid 105 degrees. All moving meshes included in 81-pose collision test, only inherited mating-bearing exclusions allowed. No moving/static collisions. Source independently reopened and source/export maximum nearest-vertex error <1e-5. Theme tests count oval ornaments, prove lid parenting, dimensions, materials and packed maps; a detached-parent negative control must fail.

Offline file:// HTML <5,000,000 bytes embeds GLB, runtime and an actual source-render poster. Desktop/mobile-emulated controls, rotation, reset, reduced-motion, corruption/no-JS fallbacks, no HTTP requests/errors/overflow. Byte inventory plus archive readback. Technical passes do not establish photorealism, artisan authorship, physical-device performance or cultural representativeness. No mint, chain, deployment or custody action.
