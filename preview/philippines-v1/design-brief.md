# Philippines — Cordillera / Contour & Paddy

Written before authoring. Primary reference is this research-led contract, not an existing promotional image. A regional Ifugao landscape interpretation, not a claim to represent all Philippine cultures.

## Research
- UNESCO, Statement of Significance: http://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/1534 describes stone or mud retaining walls, contour-following pond fields and forest-fed irrigation.
- FAO, Ifugao Rice Terraces Mountain Landscape: https://fao.org/4/a1122e/a1122e19.pdf describes contour benches, wider lower stone-supported terraces, narrower upper paddies and watershed woodlots (muyong/pinugo).
Retrieved search excerpts informed geometry only. No photographs or third-party textures copied. Historical age claims are intentionally not reproduced; this is a stylized miniature, not a geographical survey or traditional craft replica.

## Frozen visual contract
- Wide 3.72 x 2.38 box; body from z .10 to 1.56; lid centered z 1.828, thickness .30. Warm gray-brown mineral body, compact procedural stone color/roughness maps; dark green narrow seam at z 1.618.
- Satin brass inherited latch, side ring and small corner caps. Hollow continuous liner and attached rear hinges retained. Four irregular stone feet.
- Six nested asymmetric contour terraces on lid, each an independently named solid retaining volume with a green inset paddy. Largest footprint <= 2.8 x 1.65; upper terraces narrower. Successive tops rise .10 units from z 2.08 to 2.58; outline has coherent low-frequency bends, not perfect concentric circles. Stone risers remain visibly exposed, paddy greens alternate subtly (#607c39 to #91a955). No temple, flags, sacred iconography or cedar tree.
- All twelve terrace/paddy meshes directly parented to Lid_Hinge. Shallow paddy surfaces describe fields, not simulated water or individual rice plants. Rear/interior/hardware are collection extrapolations.
- Existing pivot locations unchanged, front -Y/rear +Y. Latch releases 80 degrees then lid opens 105 degrees. Sample 81 poses including all moving ornament and latch/body pairs, only explicit mating bearings exempt.
- Actual saved-source poster; offline fully embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes. Desktop/mobile file:// controls, touch rotation, reduced motion, parser-error and no-JS fallback. Mobile means emulation, not physical-device performance.
