# Gambia v1 — Tanbi Tidal Bronze

Written before modeling. An original stylized landscape collectible, not a replica of historical Gambian craft or a geographic map.

## Researched basis
- Ramsar Sites Information Service, Tanbi Wetlands Complex: https://rsis.ramsar.org/ris/1657 describes marine/fluvial sediment deposition, estuarine/intertidal forests, mangrove-dominated swamps and Rhizophora species. Its estuary channels and rooted forest motivate this design.
- Gambia Ministry of Environment, protected areas: https://meccnar.gov.gm/information-protected-areas-gambia describes Tanbi on the southern shore of the River Gambia, seasonal creeks and intertidal/gallery forests.
Research is factual inspiration only; no source photographs or textures are copied. Bronze is an artistic material choice, not a claim of a specific local metalworking tradition. No sacred motifs or national-identity completeness claim.

## Visual contract
- Preserve wide keepsake silhouette: approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y with body height approximately 1.9. Hollow five-sided interior; front -Y, rear +Y, right +X, up +Z. glTF maps (X,Z,-Y).
- Body is warm sediment bronze/ochre with dark umber fine meandering tidal-line relief/map; palette targets ochre #987044, umber #493b2b, muted reed green #425f42. Procedural portable color and roughness maps <=512px, no scans.
- Shallow lid estuary tableau: continuous sinuous blue-green channel (#277d83) between irregular warm silt banks. Channel crosses lid left to right, with at least one tributary. Raised mangrove cluster at rear/right, visible branching prop roots and low irregular green canopy. At least three trunks, six exposed roots, three canopy masses; vegetation max 0.85 above lid. All ornament belongs to Lid_Hinge.
- Narrow dark seam <=0.10; satin bronze/brass hardware, inherited physically connected rear hinge and front hasp, restrained corners. Do not add cedar needles, granite mountain or glacier.
- Preserve exact inherited pivots and choreography: latch releases 80 degrees first, lid then opens 105 degrees. Evaluate every moving mesh against static meshes plus latch against lid at 81 poses; exempt only explicitly named intentional bearing contacts.
- Standalone offline HTML with drag, open/close, reset and reduced motion. Actual source-render poster, no AI substitute. Total HTML strictly <5,000,000 bytes.
