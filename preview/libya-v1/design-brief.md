# Libya v1 — Acacus / Wind-carved Arch

Brief frozen before model authoring. A landscape-inspired collectible, not a replica of a named monument or a comprehensive representation of Libya.

## Research and rights
- UNESCO, Rock-Art Sites of Tadrart Acacus: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/287/ establishes the Libyan Saharan massif and its prehistoric cultural importance.
- Acacus Mountains landscape overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acacus_Mountains describes differently coloured dunes, natural arches, isolated rocks, gorges and wadis, including Afzejare and Tin Khlega.
- Local tour landscape descriptions: https://tourslibya.com/acacus documents dunes along the eastern edge and prominent natural arches. Used for qualitative landscape inspiration, not its inconsistent compass directions or numerical dimensions.
Sources consulted through web retrieval before authoring. No source photographs, rock-art paintings, sacred motifs or inscriptions will be copied. Ochre mineral surfaces are an artistic interpretation, not a geological specimen identification. All maps are independently procedural.

## Measurable visual contract
- Wide keepsake silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X by 2.38 Y, exterior walls z=.10 to 1.56; lid approximately 3.72 by 2.38 by .30 at z=1.828. Continuous five-sided hollow liner; interior floor retained.
- Body: warm honey/ochre mineral surface with fine irregular wind-eroded horizontal relief, darker brown weathering. Palette targets sRGB approximately #b98243, #d9b378 and #65452c, not Morocco's salmon-pink strata. Compact generated color/roughness maps no larger than 1024 each axis.
- Lid: low sand-coloured dune field with two asymmetric flowing crests, and a true three-dimensional irregular arch with visible negative space, positioned rearward within lid bounds. Arch width .9–1.5, height .55–.9 above lid, no tree, glacier, tile mosaic or flat image stand-in. One small eroded satellite outcrop permitted. Terrain and arch are descendants of Lid_Hinge and move rigidly with it.
- Restrained warm brass family hardware, corner protectors, circular clasp medallion and connected rear bearings; no flag or political insignia. Dark umber narrow seam, maximum .11 high, clear of moving hinge leaves.
- Coordinates: front -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF (X,Z,-Y). Keep inherited Lid_Hinge and Latch_Pivot positions unchanged. 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening; reset cancels movement.
- All moving meshes sampled against static geometry plus latch against lid at >=81 poses. Only exact named mating bearing contacts exempt. No blanket terrain exemptions.
- Real source-render poster, separate editable packed .blend and GLB. Offline HTML strictly below 5,000,000 bytes including bundled runtime, model and poster; no runtime network dependencies.

## Acceptance gates
Independently reopen saved source; verify dimensions, hollow floor, pivot lineage, arch opening geometry and attachment. Check actual exported geometry/source parity within 1e-5 units. Parse with actual GLTFLoader: finite vertices, zero degenerate triangles and decoded procedural texture variation. Test motion endpoints/order, desktop and mobile-emulated file:// controls/drag/reset/reduced motion, real corrupt-model and no-JS fallback, no HTTP requests, no overflow. Capture and inspect normal-size closed/open views. Record exact byte counts and SHA-256 inventory; checks assert and can fail. Preserve real failure evidence if encountered. Technical gates do not establish photorealism, continuous mechanical clearance, physical-phone performance or human art approval. No blockchain actions or production deployment.
