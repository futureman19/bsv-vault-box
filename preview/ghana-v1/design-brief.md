# Ghana v1 — Kente & brass

## Research and interpretation (frozen before modeling)
UNESCO, Craftsmanship of traditional woven textile Kente (2024 inscription): https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/craftsmanship-of-traditional-woven-textile-kente-02130 describes Ghanaian silk/cotton/rayon strips woven on horizontal looms, named designs, community histories and intergenerational craft. UNESCO's associated photographic captions https://ich.unesco.org/en/01370?call=slideshow&id=02130&include=slideshow_inc.php&mode=scroll&width=620 describe strips sewn together and examples from Ashanti and Volta regions.

This original geometric design celebrates strip construction, not a copy of a named cloth, royal regalia or sacred symbol. No claim of authentic community-approved Kente, universal Ghanaian symbolism, historic wooden chest design, or color meanings. No third-party photographs/textures embedded. The dark wood and brass case are contemporary collectible design extrapolations; the textile-inspired panels are decorative rigid inlays, not simulated cloth.
