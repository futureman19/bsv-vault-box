# Iran / Turquoise & Star — pre-model visual contract

## Research and interpretation
The Metropolitan Museum's Cross-Shaped Tile (41.165.37), 13th century, probably Kashan, Iran, documents blue/turquoise and luster-painted opaque white glaze and star/cross architectural panels: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/450453 . Its Eight-Pointed Star-Shaped Tile (91.1.106), Kashan, documents puzzle-like architectural revetments: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/444459 . UNESCO's Meidan Emam conservation report describes mosaic tile and wooden girih decoration in Esfahan: https://whc.unesco.org/document/156850 . Sources retrieved through search excerpts; direct UNESCO page fetch returned HTTP 403. No source photographs or inscriptions are copied.

The primary reference is this written brief, not a prior approved illustration. This contemporary decorative object combines turquoise/cobalt glazed ceramic with ivory eight-pointed stars and warm brass. It does not reproduce a historical tile panel, religious building, script, or sacred inscription. The motif is independently generated; it represents one craft association, not all Iran.

## Limits
Procedural ceramic-inspired art, not artisan-made ceramic, a scan, an archaeological reconstruction or photoreal equivalence. Relief joints are decorative rather than a mathematically exact historic tiling. Sampled surface checks are not continuous mechanical certification. Mobile emulation is not physical-phone testing. Cosmetic lid motion is unrelated to blockchain custody; no minting, networking or public deployment.
