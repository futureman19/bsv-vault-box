# Kyrgyzstan v1 — Shyrdak / Crimson Felt

Written before modeling. This is an original craft-inspired BoxSV collectible, not a reproduction of a particular artisan's carpet or an ethnographic reconstruction. No primary artwork was supplied. The concept is a wide dark-walnut keepsake chest wrapped in crimson felt, with ivory interlocking horn-like scrolls, restrained brass fittings and a raised stitched lid medallion. No copied photographs, flags, sacred symbols or asserted symbolic meanings.

## Research
- UNESCO, Ala-kiyiz and Shyrdak, art of Kyrgyz traditional felt carpets: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ala-kiyiz-and-shyrdak-art-of-kyrgyz-traditional-felt-carpets-00693 . Describes felt carpets as integral Kyrgyz cultural heritage, communal making and intergenerational transmission, and their use warming/decorating homes.
- UNESCO/ICHCAP reference materials, Kyrgyz Republic: https://archive.unesco-ichcap.org/kor/ek/sub1/pdf_file/central_asia/Kyrgyz_2011_07_Reference_Materials.pdf . Describes shyrdak's mosaic technique, joined colored felt over a thicker backing, stitching, horn-inspired ornaments, and carpets stored on decorated wooden trunks (sundyk).
Research is factual inspiration only. All maps and scroll geometry will be generated independently. The palette and chest construction are artistic choices, not claims that all Kyrgyz craft shares this appearance.

## Measurable visual contract
- Preserve family wide silhouette: body approximately 3.72 X 2.38 units, height approximately 1.9; hollow five-sided inner shell. Front is Blender -Y, rear +Y, right +X; glTF maps (X,Z,-Y).
- Crimson felt exterior, target sRGB approximately #8e2537 with visible fine wool-like procedural variation; ivory #ead9b5 raised scroll appliques; dark brown exposed frame; antique brass hardware. Felt roughness >=0.8, nonmetallic; brass metallic >=0.65. No glossy plastic felt.
- Four outer wall panels and an inset lid panel carry felt. Lid has at least four mirrored horn-like scroll pairs, a perimeter stitched border and a central low-relief geometric medallion. Original simplified geometry, not a specific traditional motif copied or named. Relief must be real geometry, not only texture.
- Lid relief height <=0.16 above panel; all ornament remains within lid footprint and travels with Lid_Hinge. No terrain miniature needed: craft is the identity.
- Dark narrow seam <=0.10 units; keep hinge/hasp clearance. Brass family latch and rear hinge connection, four feet, restrained corner fittings. Rear/interior are extrapolated.
- Reuse existing pivots and 80-degree latch release before 105-degree lid opening. Independently reopen saved source and sample 81 poses across every moving/static pair plus latch versus lid; only explicitly named mating bearings exempt. No general ornament exclusions.
- Procedural maps <=512 pixels per dimension; portable base/roughness/normal where useful. Budget complete embedded HTML <5,000,000 bytes, not merely GLB.
- Actual-source render poster, editable packed blend, exported GLB, reproducible scripts and map files. No AI promotional substitute.
- Desktop and 390x844 touch-emulated mobile file:// browser checks: real loader, finite/nondegenerate geometry, open/close, drag, reset mid-motion, reduced motion, corrupt-input and no-JS poster, no network requests/errors/overflow. Capture normal-size closed/open views.
- Independent source/export geometry parity with declared float tolerance, factual theme assertions, exact sizes and SHA-256 inventory. Preserve real failure evidence when a gate fails; never relax the contract merely to pass.

## Acceptance limits
Technical acceptance is not photorealism, artisan endorsement, physical-phone performance, continuous collision proof, user art approval or blockchain custody verification. This pilot is offline art only, not minted or deployed. The user waived per-asset review; stop once the bounded technical/visual checks pass.
