Contents stay fixed while sealed. Ownership can change without unpacking.

Inspect before you accept

Package history · demo

One Box, successive seals. Each seal records its own contents; broken seals stay broken. This reusable lifecycle is a design demo, not implemented on-chain functionality.

Identity & evidence

DEMO-001 is a display identifier, not an on-chain Box. This prototype has no transaction history, confirmed owner, or verified assets.

What the seal does not mean

Not secret. Not a guarantee of value or permanent file storage. Not proof of payment or delivery.

Independent of your wallet

A Box is an on-chain package. A compatible wallet authorizes actions. This page only demonstrates that experience; it cannot sign or transfer anything.
