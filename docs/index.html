PROOF, NOT PROMISES

The seal already works on-chain.

A BoxSV seal is a covenant enforced by BSV miners — not a promise on a website. While a Box is sealed, its contents cannot be added, removed, or swapped. Opening is one-way and permanent. Two real Boxes sit on mainnet today, so don't take this page's word for it — check them yourself.

ONE BOX.
DISTINCT SEALS.

  1. Pack anything. A Box brings specific items together — a package, not a replacement for your wallet.
  2. Seal the contents. While sealed, contents cannot be added, removed, or swapped — enforced by miners, not by any website.
  3. Pass it on intact. Transfer ownership without unpacking. The sender can no longer open or change it — like a shipped package with a tamper seal.
  4. Open, then withdraw. Opening breaks that seal permanently. Taking the item out is a separate action.
  5. Reuse the Box, not the seal. Repack with a new item and issue a new numbered seal. The Box keeps its identity; earlier seals stay broken in its history.

Sealed means contents stay fixed — not that they are secret. Each seal has its own history.

WHAT GOES IN A BOX

Built for things you pass on.

  • Collectibles & NFTs. The starting point — pack a set, seal it, sell or ship it as one package.
  • Mystery packs. Contents drawn when the Box is opened (VAULT-0003) — nobody can peek or curate after sealing.
  • Trustless resale. An unopened Box keeps its seal verifiable on-chain, so buyers can check before they pay.
  • Gifts & drops. A present that travels sealed until the recipient opens it.
