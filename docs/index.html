HOW IT WORKS
AT LAUNCH

  1. Pay 1 BSV, win a box. Every time. The claw never misses — the mystery is what's sealed inside, not whether you win.
  2. Your box is sealed by miners, not by us. Each box is a VAULT-0001 covenant on BSV. Its state byte says SEALED, and consensus forbids faking or resealing it.
  3. Contents are committed before you pay. The inventory and odds live on-chain in a manifest before any sale. We can't swap, cherry-pick, or weigh the packs.
  4. The proposed draw happens at opening. At opening, your pack is drawn from your box id plus a future block hash (VAULT-0003). No committee, no oracle, no trusted party.
  5. Keep it sealed, trade it sealed. Sealed boxes are transferable and stay collectible. Anyone can verify any seal, forever, from public chain data.
  6. Open it whenever you want. Opening is a one-way on-chain event — it breaks the seal permanently and mints your pack. Opened boxes remain transferable.

THE RULES
AT LAUNCH

  1. One play = one sealed box, minted to your wallet's key. Only you can open it.
  2. Every play wins a box. Rarity is set by the published on-chain odds — not by us.
  3. A sealed box's seal has never been broken, by anyone, ever — that's verifiable, not promised.
  4. No refunds on opened boxes: opening is your choice, and it's final.
  5. The standard is open. Anyone can check our work: seal checker, specs & code.

The seal covenant has live mainnet proofs. VAULT-0003 draw-at-open is a draft, not a live prize service. This demo illustrates the experience; payment and minting are not enabled.
